# Research: How to Pray Salah According to Salafi Scholars

**Sources:** Shaykh al-Albani, Shaykh al-Uthaymin, Shaykh Ibn Baz, Shaykh al-Fawzan, Ibn Taymiyyah, Ibn al-Qayyim, Ibn Qudamah

## 1. THE ARKAN (PILLARS) OF SALAH — 14 PILLARS

According to Shaykh Muhammad Nasir al-Din al-Albani in "Sifat Salat an-Nabi ﷺ" and IslamQA (based on his work):

1. **Standing during obligatory prayers** (if one is able)
   - Proof: "When you stand for prayer, perform ablution perfectly, then face the qiblah and say takbeer." (Bukhari 756, Muslim 397) — Sahih

2. **The opening takbeer** (saying "Allahu Akbar")
   - Proof: "The key to the prayer is Purification. That which makes outside actions forbidden is the takbeer, and that which causes outside actions to become permissible is the tasleem." (Abu Dawud 61, Tirmidhi 3, Ibn Majah 275) — Graded Sahih by al-Albani (Sahih al-Jami' 576)

3. **Reciting al-Fatihah**
   - Proof: "There is no prayer for the one who does not recite the Opening of the Book (Fatihat al-Kitab)." (Bukhari 756, Muslim 394, Nasa'i 911, Ibn Majah 841) — Sahih (agreed upon)

4. **Ruku' (bowing)** — the least means bending so hands can touch knees
   - Proof: "Then bow until you are at rest in bowing." (Bukhari 756, Abu Dawud 859) — Sahih

5. **Rising from bowing**
   - Proof: "Then stand up straight." (Bukhari 756, Abu Dawud 859) — Sahih

6. **Standing up straight (I'tidal)** after ruku'
   - Proof: Same as above — Sahih

7. **Sujud (prostration)** — on seven body parts
   - Proof: "I have been ordered to prostrate on seven bones: the forehead (along with the tip of nose), both hands, both knees, and the toes of both feet." (Bukhari 809, Muslim 490) — Sahih (agreed upon)

8. **Rising from prostration**
   - Proof: "Then prostrate until you are at rest in prostration, then sit up." (Bukhari 756) — Sahih

9. **Sitting between the two prostrations**
   - Proof: Same as above — Sahih

10. **Being at ease (tuma'ninah) in each of these physical pillars**
    - Proof: "Then stand up straight until your spine is straight." (Bukhari 756) — Sahih

11. **The final tashahhud**
    - Proof: The Prophet ﷺ taught the tashahhud and said: "Say this in every sitting." (Tirmidhi) — Graded Sahih by al-Albani

12. **Sitting to recite the final tashahhud**
    - Proof: The Prophet ﷺ used to sit in the final tashahhud (Bukhari, Muslim) — Sahih

13. **The two salaams** (saying "as-salamu 'alaykum wa rahmatullah" twice)
    - Proof: Same hadith as #2 (Abu Dawud 61, Tirmidhi 3) — Graded Sahih by al-Albani

14. **Doing the pillars in the order mentioned**
    - Proof: The Prophet ﷺ taught the order (Bukhari 756) — Sahih

**Source:** IslamQA (65847) based on al-Albani's classification; "The Abridgement of the Prophet's Prayer Described" by al-Albani

## 2. THE WAJIBAT (OBLIGATORY PARTS) OF SALAH — 8 WAJIBAT

According to Shaykh al-Albani (IslamQA 65847):

1. **Takbeers other than the opening takbeer** (transitional takbirs)
   - Proof: The Prophet ﷺ would say takbeer when going down for ruku', sujud, and rising (Bukhari, Muslim) — Sahih

2. **Saying "Sami'a Allahu liman hamidah"** (for imam and one praying alone)
   - Proof: "When the Imam says: 'Sami' Allahu liman hamidah,' then say: 'Rabbana wa lakal-hamd.'" (Bukhari 796, Muslim 409) — Sahih (agreed upon)

3. **Saying "Rabbana wa lakal-hamd"**
   - Proof: Same as above — Sahih

4. **Saying "Subhana rabbiyal-'Azim"** once when bowing
   - Proof: "When one of you bows, let him say in his bowing: 'Subhana Rabbiyal-'Azim' three times." (Ibn Majah 888, Tirmidhi 261) — Graded Hasan by al-Albani

5. **Saying "Subhana rabbiyal-A'la"** once when prostrating
   - Proof: "When one of you prostrates, let him say in his prostration: 'Subhana Rabbiyal-A'la' three times." (Ibn Majah 890, Tirmidhi 261) — Hasan

6. **Saying "Rabb ighfir li"** between the two prostrations
   - Proof: "The Prophet ﷺ used to say between the two prostrations: 'Rabbighfir li, Rabbighfir li.'" (Ibn Majah 897, Nasa'i 1145) — Graded Sahih by al-Albani (Sifat as-Salah 3/118)

7. **The first tashahhud**
   - Proof: The Prophet ﷺ used to sit and recite tashahhud in the first sitting (Bukhari, Muslim) — Sahih

8. **Sitting for the first tashahhud**
   - Proof: Same as above — Sahih

**Source:** IslamQA (65847); Mukhtasar Sifat Salat an-Nabi by al-Albani

## 3. THE SUNAN (RECOMMENDED ACTS) OF SALAH

According to Shaykh al-Albani in "Sifat Salat an-Nabi ﷺ":

1. **Raising hands at takbeer al-ihram** (to shoulders or ears)
   - Proof: Ibn Umar: "I saw the Messenger of Allah ﷺ when he said the opening takbir of the prayer, raise his hands until they were level with his shoulders." (Nasa'i 876, Abu Dawud 761) — Sahih

2. **Placing right hand over left on chest**
   - Proof: "He used to place his right hand on his left hand." (Muslim, Abu Dawud) — Sahih

3. **Looking at place of prostration**
   - Proof: The Prophet ﷺ would look at his place of prostration (Bayhaqi) — Graded Sahih by al-Albani

4. **Reciting opening supplication (du'a al-istiftah)**
   - Proof: Abu Hurayrah: "O Allah, put a great distance between me and my sins..." (Abu Dawud 775, Tirmidhi 243) — Graded Sahih by al-Albani (Sahih al-Jami')

5. **Saying "Amin" after Fatihah** (loudly in audible prayers)
   - Proof: "When the Imam says 'Amin', then you should all say 'Amin'." (Bukhari 782, Muslim 410) — Sahih

6. **Reciting a surah after Fatihah** (in first two rak'ahs)
   - Proof: "The Prophet ﷺ used to recite al-Fatihah and another surah in the first two rak'ahs." (Bukhari 762, Muslim 451) — Sahih

7. **Saying takbeers when going down and coming up** (other than opening)
   - Proof: "He would say takbeer when bowing, when prostrating, and when rising." (Bukhari, Muslim) — Sahih

8. **Making the supplications in ruku' and sujud more than once**
   - Proof: The Prophet ﷺ would say tasbih three times minimum (Tirmidhi 261) — Hasan

9. **Placing hands on knees during ruku'** with back straight
   - Proof: "When he bowed, he placed his hands on his knees and made his back straight." (Nasa'i, Abu Dawud) — Sahih

10. **Sitting muftarishan** (on left foot, right foot upright) between prostrations and in tashahhud
    - Proof: Abu Humaid described the Prophet's sitting (Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi) — Sahih

**Source:** "The Prophet's Prayer Described" by al-Albani; Mukhtasar Sifat Salat an-Nabi

## 4. CONDITIONS (SHURUT) OF SALAH

According to Shaykh Salih al-Fawzan in "Al-Mulakhkhas al-Fiqhi" and Ibn Baz:

1. **Wudu (ablution)**
   - Proof: Quran 5:6; "No prayer is accepted without purification." (Tirmidhi) — Graded Sahih by al-Albani

2. **Facing the Qibla**
   - Proof: Quran 2:144; Bukhari 756 — Sahih

3. **Covering the awrah**
   - Proof: Quran 7:31; "None of you should pray in a single garment that does not cover his shoulders." (Bukhari, Muslim) — Sahih
   - Man's awrah: navel to knees; Woman: all except face and hands

4. **Intention (niyyah)**
   - Proof: "Actions are but by intentions, and every man shall have only that which he intended." (Bukhari 1, Muslim 1907) — Sahih (agreed upon)
   - Intention is in the heart, not spoken aloud (al-Albani, Ibn Baz)

5. **Time (entering of prayer time)**
   - Proof: Quran 4:103; "The time of Zuhr is when the sun passes its zenith..." (Muslim 610-613) — Sahih

**Source:** "Al-Mulakhkhas al-Fiqhi" by al-Fawzan; "The Description of the Prophet's Prayer" by Ibn Baz; IslamQA

## 6. THE OPENING DU'A (DU'A AL-ISTIFTAH)

According to Shaykh al-Albani and IslamQA (242032):

### Version 1 (Abu Hurayrah) — Most Authentic:
"Allahumma ba'id bayni wa bayna khatayaya kama ba'adta bayna al-mashriqi wal-maghrib. Allahumma naqqini min al-khataya kama yunaqqa ath-thawb al-abyad min ad-danas. Allahumma ighsil khatayaya bil-ma'i wath-thalji wal-barad."

Translation: "O Allah, put a great distance between me and my sins, as great as the distance You have made between the East and the West. O Allah, cleanse me of sin as a white garment is cleansed from filth. O Allah, wash away my sins with water, snow and hail."

**Reference:** Abu Dawud (775), Tirmidhi (243)
**Grading by al-Albani:** Sahih (Sahih al-Jami')

### Version 2 (Aishah) — "Subhanaka":
"Subhanak Allahumma wa bi hamdik, wa tabarak asmuka wa ta'ala jadduka wa la ilaha ghayruk."

**Reference:** Abu Dawud (775), Tirmidhi (243), Ibn Majah
**Grading by al-Albani:** Sahih (Sahih al-Jami')

### Version 3 (Ali) — "Wajahtu Wajhi":
"Inni wajjahtu wajhiya lilladhi fataras-samawati wal-arda hanifan wa ma ana minal-mushrikin..."

**Reference:** Muslim, Abu Dawud (760), Tirmidhi
**Grading by al-Albani:** Sahih

**Source:** IslamQA (242032); Abu Dawud 775 commentary by al-Albani

## 7. RECITING TA'AWWUDH AND BISMILLAH

According to Shaykh al-Albani:

**Ta'awwudh (Seeking Refuge):**
- Ruling: Sunnah to recite silently before recitation
- Proof: The Prophet ﷺ would seek refuge from Satan before reciting (Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi) — Sahih

**Bismillah:**
- Al-Albani's position: Bismillah is part of Surah al-Fatihah (stronger opinion), should be recited as part of Fatihah
- Proof: Anas: "The Prophet ﷺ, Abu Bakr and Umar used to begin the Salat with 'Al-hamdu lillahi Rabbil-'alamin' and they would not recite Bismillah aloud." (Bukhari, Muslim — meaning they recited it silently) — Sahih

**Source:** "Halaqah 47" by al-Albani; Bulugh al-Maram 219

## 8. AL-FATIHAH — "NO PRAYER IS VALID WITHOUT FATIHAH"

**Hadith:** "There is no prayer for the one who does not recite the Opening of the Book (Fatihat al-Kitab)."

**References:** Bukhari (756), Muslim (394), Nasa'i (910, 911), Ibn Majah (841), Abu Dawud
**Arabic:** "لا صلاة لمن لم يقرأ بفاتحة الكتاب"
**Grading by al-Albani:** Sahih (agreed upon)

**Additional Hadith:** "Every prayer in which Fatihat al-Kitab is not recited is deficient, deficient." (Ibn Majah 840, Nasa'i) — Graded Sahih by al-Albani

**Note:** This applies to the imam, one praying alone, and the follower (according to the stronger opinion, as per Ibn Hibban 1789 where the Prophet told Abu Hurayrah: "Recite it to yourself" even when behind imam).

**Source:** Bukhari 756; Muslim 394; Ibn Hibban 1789; IslamQA

## 9. RECITING A SURAH AFTER FATIHAH

### Is it Fard, Wajib, or Sunnah?

**Shaykh al-Uthaymin said:**
"According to the majority of scholars, reciting another surah (after al-Fatihah) is Sunnah and is not obligatory, because it is obligatory to recite only al-Fatihah." (Al-Sharh al-Mumti')

**Proof:**
Abu Hurayrah: "In every prayer there is recitation. What we heard the Prophet ﷺ say, we tell you, and what he did not tell us about we cannot tell you. Whoever recites Umm al-Kitab (i.e., Surat al-Fatihah) has done enough, and whoever does more than that, that is better." (Bukhari 738, Muslim 396) — Sahih (agreed upon)

**Application:**
- First two rak'ahs: Sunnah to recite a surah after Fatihah
- Third and fourth rak'ahs: Only Fatihah (no additional surah)
- If one cannot memorize a full surah, reciting even one ayah is sufficient (per Ibn Uthaymin)

**Source:** IslamQA (6422); Al-Sharh al-Mumti' by al-Uthaymin

## 10. RUku' — WHAT TO SAY

**What to say:** "Subhana rabbiyal-'Azim" (سبحان ربي العظيم)
**Translation:** "Glory be to my Lord, the Most Great"

**References:** Ibn Majah (888), Tirmidhi (261), Nasa'i (1047), Abu Dawud (869)

**Hadith text:** "When one of you bows, let him say in his bowing: 'Subhana Rabbiyal-'Azim' three times, then he has completed his bowing. And that is the least of it."

**Grading by al-Albani:** Hasan (Tirmidhi 261); Sahih (Nasa'i 1047)

**Additional:** "When the verse 'So exalt the name of your Lord, the Most Great' was revealed, the Prophet ﷺ said: 'Put it in your ruku'.'" (Abu Dawud 869, Tabarani 15019) — Graded Sahih by al-Albani

## 11. RISING FROM RUku'

**Hadith:** "When the Imam says: 'Sami' Allahu liman hamidah' (Allah hears those who praise Him), then say: 'Rabbana wa lakal-hamd' (Our Lord, to You be praise)."

**References:** Bukhari (796), Muslim (409), Nasa'i (12.24)
**Grading:** Sahih (agreed upon)

**Who says what:**
- **Imam and one praying alone:** Says both "Sami'a-Allahu liman hamidah" and "Rabbana wa lakal-hamd"
- **Follower (ma'mum):** Only says "Rabbana wa lakal-hamd" (not "Sami'a-Allahu liman hamidah")

**Source:** Bukhari 796; Muslim 409; Nasa'i 12.24; Ahmad 19401

**References:** Ibn Majah (890), Tirmidhi (261), Nasa'i (1047), Abu Dawud (869)
**Grading by al-Albani:** Hasan (Tirmidhi 261); Sahih (Nasa'i 1047)

### The 7 Body Parts of Sujud:

**References:** Bukhari (809), Muslim (490, 491), Nasa'i (1094, 1096, 1097)

## 14. THE TASHAHHUD — WHICH VERSION IS MOST AUTHENTIC?

### According to Shaykh al-Albani:
**Most Authentic:** Ibn Mas'ud's version

**Text:**
"At-tahiyyatu lillahi, was-salawatu wat-tayyibatu. As-salamu 'alayka ayyuhan-nabiyyu wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh. As-salamu 'alayna wa 'ala 'ibadillahis-salihin. Ashhadu alla ilaha illa Allah, wa ashhadu anna Muhammadan 'abduhu wa rasuluh."

**References:** Bukhari (835, 6265), Muslim (402), Abu Dawud (969), Ibn Majah (899), Nasa'i
**Grading:** Sahih (agreed upon)

**Note:** Imam Ahmad and Abu Hanifah chose this version because its chain is most confirmed. The Prophet ﷺ taught it "just as a surah of the Qur'an is taught" (Bukhari 6265).

**Source:** Islamweb (375343); Bukhari 835; Muslim 402; Ibn Majah 899

## 15. SALAWAT IBRAHIMIYYAH — EXACT TEXT AND REFERENCE

**Arabic:**
"اللهم صل على محمد وعلى آل محمد كما صليت على إبراهيم وعلى آل إبراهيم إنك حميد مجيد، وبارك على محمد وعلى آل محمد كما باركت على إبراهيم وعلى آل إبراهيم إنك حميد مجيد"

**Translation:**
"O Allah, send prayers upon Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad, as You sent prayers upon Ibrahim and upon the family of Ibrahim. You are indeed Worthy of praise, Full of glory. O Allah, send blessings upon Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad, as You sent blessings upon Ibrahim and upon the family of Ibrahim. You are indeed Worthy of praise, Full of glory."

**References:** Bukhari (multiple), Muslim (405), Nasa'i (1291), Tirmidhi, Ibn Majah (904), Tabarani (16402)
**Hadith:** Ka'b ibn 'Ujrah narrated that a man asked: "We have come to know about how to greet you, but how should we send blessings upon you?" The Prophet ﷺ taught him this salutation.
**Grading:** Sahih (agreed upon - Bukhari and Muslim)

**Source:** Muslim 405; Nasa'i 1291; Tabarani 16402; Bukhari

## 16. THE SALAM — EXACT REFERENCE

**Hadith:** "The key to the prayer is Purification. That which makes outside actions forbidden is the takbeer, and that which causes outside actions to become permissible is the tasleem."

**References:** Abu Dawud (61), Tirmidhi (3), Ibn Majah (275), Bayhaqi (3.482)
**Grading by al-Albani:** Sahih (Sahih al-Jami' 576)

**What to say:**
- **First salam (right):** "As-salamu 'alaykum wa rahmatullah" (obligatory)
- **Second salam (left):** "As-salamu 'alaykum" or "As-salamu 'alaykum wa rahmatullah" (sunnah)

**Proof of two salaams:**
Ibn Mas'ud: "I saw the Messenger of Allah ﷺ saying the salam to his right and to his left: As-salamu 'alaykum wa rahmatullah, As-salamu 'alaykum wa rahmatullah." (Nasa'i 1319)

**Al-Albani's position:** "The giving of one tasleem is obligatory, which must be done... And giving two tasleems is sunnah, and it is permissible to leave the second tasleem." (The Authentic Base)

**Source:** Nasa'i 1319; Bayhaqi 3.482; The Authentic Base by al-Albani

## 17. SUJUD AS-SAHW (FORGETFULNESS PROSTRATION)

### According to Shaykh al-Albani:

**General Rule:**
"(It is due upon a person) under any circumstances if (something was done or not done in prayer) out of forgetfulness, even if it is a sunnah." (Silsilat al-Huda wa al-Nur, tape 183)

**When to do it (before or after salam):**

**Shaykh al-Albani's position:**
"Regarding every sujud us-sahw, its doer has a choice between prostrating before the salam or after the salam. And the detailed explanation that is mentioned in some books, especially the Hanbali ones, that (the sujud us-sahw) is before the salam for making additions (in the prayer), and after the salam for omissions, this is baseless."

"The one who forgets in prayer has a choice: if he wants, he makes the taslim (first) and that is absolutely better; and if he wants, he does not make the taslim except after completing the two prostrations of forgetfulness."

**Causes (three):**
1. Doing something extra (adding rak'ah, sujud, etc.)
2. Omitting something (leaving out wajib or rukn)
3. Being uncertain (doubt about number of rak'ahs)

**What to say during sujud as-sahw:**
"The same as the usual dhikr said during prostrating and sitting in-between the prostrations; i.e., there isn't anything special."

**If intentionally left out:**
"If he intentionally leaves out the sujud us-sahw, the prayer does not become invalid. But, he will be a sinner because of his leaving out the two prostrations of forgetfulness."

**Source:** alalbani.info (al-Albani's archive); IslamQA (77430, 12527)

## 18. KHUSHU' — WHAT IBN AL-QAYYIM AND AL-'UTHAYMIN SAID

### Ibn al-Qayyim (in Madarij as-Salikin and Ar-Ruh):

"Ibn al-Qayyim (rahimahullāh) compared khushūʿ in ṣalāh to the soul in the body. When the soul leaves the body, the body dies. Similarly, without khushūʿ the ṣalāh lacks its soul, spirit and essence. Likewise, praying ṣalāh without khushūʿ is like gifting a dead servant to a king. He then writes, 'Allah will neither accept it nor reward him for it, even if the obligation (of ṣalāh) has been legally fulfilled.'" (Life With Allah)

**Definition of khushu':**
"The khushoo' or humility, therefore, is the standing of the heart before the Lord with humility and lowliness and with full concentration towards Him." (Al Jumuah Magazine translation from Madarij al-Salikin)

**On the difference between true and hypocritical khushu':**
"The khushoo' of true faith is when the heart feels aware and humble before the greatness and glory of Allah, and is filled with awe, fear and shyness, so that the heart is utterly humbled before Allah and broken, as it were, with fear, shyness, love and the recognition of the blessings of Allah and its own sins." (33 Ways of Developing Khushoo')

### Shaykh al-Uthaymin:

"The most important affair is Khushoo and that one's heart is attentive, which is the substance and essence of the prayer. If a person has Khushoo and his heart is attentive, this will have an effect after the prayer." (Salafi Dawah Manchester)

**On those who pray without khushu':**
"How can it befit a believer that he pecks the ground like the pecking of a crow whilst he knows that he is standing before his Lord? How can it befit a believer that he pecks the ground in his prayer whilst he knows that he will not be rewarded for it? He has not discharged his obligation with this kind of prayer. This type of prayer is the same as if he had not prayed at all." (The Etiquette of the Congregational Prayer)

**On achieving khushu':**
"Khushoo' is a matter of great importance in the salaah. It can be achieved by contemplating, remembrance, humility to Allah and the rewards with Him." (Maktabah Ibn Uthaymeen)

**Source:** Madarij as-Salikin by Ibn al-Qayyim; Ar-Ruh by Ibn al-Qayyim; "The Etiquette of the Congregational Prayer" by al-Uthaymin; Salafi Dawah Manchester

## 19. CAN A BEGINNER HOLD A PHONE/BOOK IN PRAYER?

**NOT FOUND FROM SALAFI SOURCE specifically addressing holding a phone/book for learning purposes during prayer.**

However, related rulings found:

**On looking at items during prayer:**
Ibn Qudamah said in Al-Mughni: "It is disliked for a person to look at what distracts him or look in a book as 'Aa'ishah narrated that the Prophet prayed on a Khameesah (a garment with patterns), then said: 'The pattern on this distracted me.'" (Islamweb 202426)

**On cellphones in masjid:**
Shaykh Abdurrazzaq al-Badr spoke about the calamity of smartphones, saying: "Some are in 'Itikaaf, but in reality they are in a state of 'Itikaaf on their cellphone... This indicates that even in his obligatory prayer, he is preoccupied with what is on his device." (Darussaafi)

**On excessive movement:**
If holding a phone requires much movement that is more than usual, it may invalidate the prayer (Islamweb 202426)

**Conclusion:** No specific Salafi fatwa found explicitly permitting holding a phone/book during prayer for learning purposes. The general principle is to avoid distractions and excessive movement in prayer.

**Source:** Islamweb (202426); Darussaafi; Al-Mughni by Ibn Qudamah

## 20. IS PRAYER VALID IN ENGLISH?

**On reciting Qur'an in translation:**
**NOT FOUND** from al-Albani, Ibn Baz, or al-Uthaymin explicitly stating that prayer with English translation of Qur'an is valid.

**On supplicating in other languages:**
Islamweb states: "The most correct opinion is that it is permissible to say the authentic supplications in prayer in a language other than Arabic for whoever is unable to supplicate in Arabic. However, it is not permissible to do so for someone who is able to supplicate in Arabic. This is the view of the Shaafi'i and Hanbali schools." (Islamweb 90869)

**On reciting Tashahhud and Salawat in English:**
Islamweb states: "The Fuqaha differ about the reading of Tashahud and the prayers upon the Prophet while in prayer in language other than Arabic. Some of them say that it is permissible to say them in another language for whoever is unable to say them in Arabic, but it is not allowed for someone who can... Anyway, if your wife is able to say them in Arabic, then it is better and safer. If not, then there is no harm on her, Insha Allah to say them in English." (Islamweb 88268)

**On al-Albani's position:**
In "The Abridgement of the Prophet's Prayer Described," al-Albani states: "It is obligatory upon even those who do not know Arabic that they memorize [Surah al-Fatihah]." (Section 50)

**Conclusion:**
- Qur'an MUST be recited in Arabic (consensus)
- Supplications (du'a) may be said in another language if unable to say them in Arabic (per some scholars)
- Tashahhud and Salawat: Some scholars permit in another language for those unable, but Arabic is required if able
- **No Salafi scholar found stating that entire prayer can be performed in English translation**

**Source:** Islamweb (90869, 88268); "The Abridgement of the Prophet's Prayer Described" by al-Albani
