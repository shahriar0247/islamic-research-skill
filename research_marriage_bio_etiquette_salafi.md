## 1. The Qualities of a Righteous Husband (Deen, Character, Ability to Maintain a Family, Gentleness/Kindness to Women)

### IslamQA — Attributes of the Ideal Muslim Husband

**Source URL:** https://islamqa.info/en/answers/5202

> "Religious commitment. This is the most important thing to look for in the man you want to marry. The husband should be a Muslim who adheres to all the laws and teachings of Islam in his daily life. The woman's guardian (wali) should strive to check out this matter and not rely only on outward appearances. One of the most important things to ask about is the man's prayer (salaah); the one who neglects the rights of Allah is more likely to neglect the rights of others. The true believer does not oppress or mistreat his wife; if he loves her, he honours her, and if he does not love her, he does not mistreat or humiliate her. It is very rare to find this attitude among those who are not sincere Muslims."

**Source URL:** https://islamqa.info/en/answers/6585

> "There is nothing which it is more important for both parties to focus on than religious commitment and attitude."

> "With regard to the husband, the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) advised the guardians (walis) of women: 'If there comes to you one whose attitude and commitment to religion pleases you, then marry him (to your female relative), for if you do not do that, there will be much trouble and corruption in the land.' (Narrated by al-Tirmidhi, 1084; Ibn Majah, 1967)"

### Shaykh 'Abdul-'Azeez Ibn Baz — Qualities of the Righteous Husband

**Scholar:** Shaykh 'Abdul-'Azeez bin Baz
**Source URL:** https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/11199/صفات-الزوج-الصالح

> "تلتمس الرجل الطيب المعروف بالخير المحافظ على الصلوات، صاحب العقيدة الطيبة، تسأل عنه من يعرفه من الثقات الذين تعرفك إذا خطبها"

(Translation: "Seek the good man known for goodness, who maintains the prayers, possessor of a good creed. Ask about him from trustworthy people you know if he proposes.")

> "فالمقصود: أن المرأة لا مانع أن تختار لنفسها الرجل الطيب المعروف بالاستقامة في دينه، ولكن لا تبت في ذلك إلا بطريق وليها"

(Translation: "The point is: there is no harm in a woman choosing for herself a good man known for righteousness in his religion, but she should not finalize it except through her wali (guardian).")

### Shaykh 'Abdul-'Azeez Ibn Baz — Conditions Required in Both Spouses

**Scholar:** Shaykh 'Abdul-'Azeez bin Baz
**Source URL:** https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/10813/الشروط-الواجب-توفرها-في-الزوجين

> "وهكذا الرجل ينبغي أن تختاره المرأة، وأن تجتهد في أن يكون صالحًا طيبًا بعيدًا عن أسباب الشر، لا سكيرًا، ولا حليقًا للحيته، ولا صاحب تدخين، ولا صاحب زنا، ونحو ذلك، تجتهد لعله يكون سليمًا، هذا هو الذي ينبغي؛ من باب الحرص على سلامة الدين"

(Translation: "Likewise the man — the woman should choose him, and strive that he be righteous, good, far from causes of evil: not a drinker, not one who shaves his beard, not a smoker, not a fornicator, and so on. She should strive so that he be sound. This is what is befitting — out of concern for the safety of the religion.")

> "ولكن كون كل واحد يختار الصاحب الطيب، هي تختار الرجل الطيب، وهو يختار المرأة الطيبة، هذا هو المطلوب، هذا هو الذي ينبغي؛ لقوله ﷺ فاظفر بذات الدين تربت يداك وقوله في الحديث: إذا خطب إليكم من ترضون دينه وخلقه؛ فزوجوه وفي اللفظ الآخر: وأمانته فزوجوه، إلا تفعلوا؛ تكن فتنة في الأرض، وفساد كبير"

(Translation: "But that each one chooses a good companion — she chooses the good man, and he chooses the good woman — this is what is required, this is what is befitting; due to his saying ﷺ: 'So marry the one with religion, may your hands be rubbed with dust' and his saying in the hadith: 'If there comes to you one whose religion and character you are pleased with, then marry him' — and in another wording: 'and his trustworthiness, then marry him; for if you do not do so, there will be fitnah in the land and great corruption.'")

### Shaykh 'Abdul-'Azeez Ibn Baz — Advice on Good Treatment of Wives

**Scholar:** Shaykh 'Abdul-'Azeez bin Baz
**Source URL:** https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/13285/نصيحة-في-حسن-تعامل-الزوج-مع-زوجته

> "نعم، نصيحتي للأزواج أن يتقوا الله، نصيحتي لجميع الأزواج أن يتقوا الله في نسائهم، وأن يعاملوهن باللطف، والمعاملة الطيبة، كما قال الله -جل وعلا-: وَعَاشِرُوهُنَّ بِالْمَعْرُوفِ [النساء:19] بالكلام الطيب، بالأسلوب الحسن، بالحلم، بالخلق الكريم، بأداء حقوقهن كاملة، هذا الواجب على جميع الأزواج"

(Translation: "Yes, my advice to husbands is that they fear Allah, my advice to all husbands is that they fear Allah with regard to their women, and that they treat them with gentleness and good treatment, as Allah — the Mighty and Majestic — said: 'And live with them in kindness' [An-Nisa:19] — with good speech, good style, forbearance, noble character, and fulfilling their rights in full. This is the obligation upon all husbands.")

> "كون حقك أكبر لك درجة زيادة لا يقتضي، هذه الدرجة ما تقتضي أنك تسيء إليها، وأنك تظلمها لا، يجب عليك أن تحسن الخلق، وأن تعاشر بالمعروف، وأن تقوم بالواجب، من جهة النفقة اللازمة بالمعروف، ومن جهة طيب الكلام، وحسن المخاطبة"

(Translation: "The fact that your right is greater — you having a degree of increase — does not mean this degree entails that you mistreat her or oppress her. No, it is obligatory upon you to have good character, to live with them in kindness, and to fulfill the obligation — in terms of the necessary maintenance (nafaqah) in a good manner, and in terms of good speech and good address.")

> "ويجب على الرجل أن يحذر صحبة الأشرار... والعناية بالصلوات الخمس، والمحافظة عليها في الجماعة، وحسن الخلق مع الزوجة، وطيب الكلام مع الزوجة، هكذا، كما قال ﷺ: خياركم خياركم لنسائهم"

(Translation: "And it is obligatory upon the man to beware of evil companions... and to take care of the five prayers, maintaining them in congregation, and good character with the wife, and good speech with the wife — thus, as he ﷺ said: 'The best of you are the best of you to their wives.'")

> Narrated from Hakeem Ibn Muʿāwiyah from his father (radiyallāhu anhumā) who said: "Messenger of Allah, what is the right of a wife over her husband?" He replied:

> "A man must provide for his wife, and she is not obligated to provide for him. He is the breadwinner, and goes out to work – the wife or daughters should not be expected to work and provide for the household."

## 2. The Prophet's ﷺ Advice: "The Best of You Are Those Who Are Best to Their Wives/Families" — Grading by al-Albani

**Collection:** Sunan at-Tirmidhi, Hadith no. 3895
**Narrator:** 'A'ishah (may Allah be pleased with her)
**Grading:** Classed as **sahih** by Shaykh al-Albani in **Sahih Sunan at-Tirmidhi**
**Source URL:** https://islamqa.info/en/answers/193041/what-is-the-ruling-on-saying-that-the-household-of-the-prophet-blessings-and-peace-of-allah-be-upon-him-was-not-free-of-marital-problems

> At-Tirmidhi (3895) narrated that 'A'ishah (may Allah be pleased with her) said: The Messenger of Allah (blessings and peace of Allah be upon him) said: **"The best of you is the one who is best to his wives, and I am the best of you to my wives."** Classed as sahih by Shaykh al-Albani (may Allah have mercy on him) in *Sahih Sunan at-Tirmidhi*.

### TROID.org — Love, Romance, and the Proper Treatment of One's Family

**Source URL:** https://www.troid.org/love-romance-and-the-proper-treatment-of-ones-family-as-exemplified-by-the-noble-prophet/

> "The best of you are those who are best to their families, and I am the best among you in the treatment of my family."

> Al-Shawkānī explains: "This ḥadīth brings attention to the fact that the highest echelon of mankind and the most deserving among them of being ascribed to goodness, are those who are most exemplary in their treatment of their families. For a person's family is considered more deserving of his good treatment than anyone else, such as displaying exemplary manners towards them, treating them with righteousness, facilitating everything that proffers benefit to them while protecting them from all evil. Any man whose behavior is as described is considered among the best of people, just as his antithesis is considered among the vilest and most despicable. Frequently, men become ensnared in this trap: When they are with their families, they display the most contemptible manners, are courageous in their perpetration of obscenities and vileness, and are among the most devoid of righteousness and good. Then, upon meeting complete strangers, their entire disposition shifts, becoming mild-mannered, kind, gentle, tender, forbearing in their treatment of others, appearing genuine, and wrought with goodness. There should be no doubt that whoever conducts himself thus is devoid of true guidance, having gone astray from the right way. We seek safety from Allah with regards to such conduct."

### Shaykh Muhammad ibn Saalih al-'Uthaymeen — Good Manners When Dealing with the Creation

**Scholar:** Shaykh Muhammad bin Saalih al-'Uthaymeen
**Source URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2012/07/05/good-manners-when-dealing-with-the-creation-shaikh-muhammad-bin-saalih-al-uthaimeen/

> "This is why the Prophet (sallAllaahu 'alayhi wa sallam) said: **'The best of you are those who are the best towards their family. And I am the best of you towards my family.'** [6]"

> "Many people, unfortunately, display good manners in front of people yet they do not behave in the same manner towards their family. This is extremely wrong and the opposite of how things should be. How can you have good manners in front of strangers yet bad manners in front of your own family?"

> "Someone may say: Because I take care of them and support them! We respond by saying: This is not something that should cause you to display bad manners towards them. Your family members and relatives have the most right out of all people that you should be good-mannered towards them when accompanying and spending time with them."

## 3. The Obligation of Maintaining (Nafaqah) One's Wife and Family — Financial Responsibility

### IslamQA — Spending on a Wife Who Works

**Source URL:** https://islamqa.info/en/answers/126316

> "It is obligatory for the husband to spend on his wife, according to his means, and he does not have the right to make her pay for her own expenses, even if she is rich, except with her agreement. This spending on the wife's maintenance includes everything having to do with her clothing, summer and winter."

> "In the answer to question no. 3054, we quoted sufficient evidence from the Quran and Sunnah, and scholarly consensus, to prove that it is obligatory for the husband to spend on his wife, according to his means, and that he does not have the right to make her pay for her own expenses, even if she is rich, except with her agreement."

### IslamQA — Relatives on Whom a Man Is Obliged to Spend

**Source URL:** https://islamqa.info/en/answers/321168/he-wants-to-know-who-are-the-relatives-on-whom-a-man-is-obliged-to-spend

> "A man is obliged to spend on himself, his wife and his children"

> "Spending on one's wife is obligatory, even if she is rich."

### IslamQA — Does the Husband Have the Right to Dispose of His Wife's Money?

**Source URL:** https://islamqa.info/en/answers/163541

> "The basic principle concerning any wealth the wife owns is that it belongs to her and not to her husband, whether this wealth came into her possession by means of trade or business, or through inheritance, or as part of her mahr (dowry) or from the state. The husband does not have a share in any of that; rather it is her property and none of it is permissible for him unless she gives it to him willingly."

> "It is not permissible for the husband to take anything from his wife's wealth except what she allows."

### IslamQA — Why Should the Wife Obey Her Husband?

**Source URL:** https://islamqa.info/en/answers/13661

> "In Islam, men are obliged to spend on their wives; the wife does not have to work or earn a living. Even if she has an independent income or she becomes rich, the husband is still obliged to spend on her as much as she needs. Because he bears the responsibility of spending, he is made the protector and maintainer of his wife and is put in charge (of the household)."

### FatwaIslam — Do Not Stay Away From Your Wife For More Than Six Months Without Her Permission

**Scholar:** Shaykh Muhammad bin Saalih al-'Uthaymeen
**Book:** Fatawa Islamiyah, Darussalam, Volume 5, page 395
**Source URL:** http://www.fatwaislam.com/fis/index.cfm?ID=265&scn=fd

## 4. Wife's Obedience (Qawwamah, An-Nisa 4:34) — Balanced with the Husband's Obligations of Kindness, Good Treatment, and Consultation (Shura)

### The Verse: An-Nisa 4:34

> "Men are the protectors and maintainers of women, because Allah has made one of them to excel the other, and because they spend (to support them) from their means. Therefore the righteous women are devoutly obedient (to Allah and to their husbands), and guard in the husband's absence what Allah orders them to guard (e.g. their chastity and their husband's property). As to those women on whose part you see ill-conduct, admonish them (first), (next) refuse to share their beds, (and last) beat them (lightly, if it is useful); but if they return to obedience, seek not against them means (of annoyance). Surely, Allah is Ever Most High, Most Great." [al-Nisa' 4:34]

### Ibn Taymiyyah — On the Obligation of a Woman Obeying Her Husband

**Scholar:** Shaykhul-Islaam Ibn Taymiyyah
**Book:** Majmoo' al-Fataawaa (32/260-261)
**Source URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2014/09/29/the-obligation-of-a-woman-obeying-her-husband-shaykh-saalih-fawzaan/

> "This mandates the unrestricted obligation of a woman obeying her husband, in all affairs, such as serving him, traveling with him, assisting him and other matters, as is indicated in the Sunnah of Allah's Messenger."

### Ibn al-Qayyim — On Qawwamah and the Wife's Service

**Scholar:** Ibn al-Qayyim
**Book:** al-Hadee (5/188-189)
**Source URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2014/09/29/the-obligation-of-a-woman-obeying-her-husband-shaykh-saalih-fawzaan/

> "Those who say that it is obligatory for the woman to serve the husband use (this ayah) as proof in that those who Allaah directed His Speech to (on this occasion) considered this to be from al-ma'roof (good). But as for the woman relaxing and having the husband serve her, sweep, grind the flour, knead the bread, wash the clothes, fix the bed, and serve the household, then that is from al-munkar (evil)."

> "So if a woman doesn't serve her husband, but instead he acts like a servant to her, then this means that she is the protector and maintainer over him."

### Shaykh Saalih al-Fawzan — The Obligation of a Woman Obeying Her Husband

**Scholar:** Shaykh Saalih bin Fawzaan al-Fawzaan
**Book:** Tanbeehaat 'alaa Ahkaam takhtassu bil-Mu'minaat (pp. 97-99)

### Ibn al-Qayyim — On Self-Amazement and Arrogance

**Scholar:** Imam Ibn al-Qayyim
**Book:** Al-Fawaa'id
**Source URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2009/11/02/cure-for-self-amazement/

> Imam Ibnul Qayyim (rahimahullaah) states in Al Fawaa-id that Ibn Sad (rahimahullaah) mentions in At Tabaqaat about Umar Bin Abdul Azeez (rahimahullaah) that:

> "Whenever he delivered a khutbah on the mimbar, and feared being amazed by himself; he would terminate it (i.e. the khutbah). And whenever he wrote a letter and feared amazement by way of it; he would tear it. And he would say: 'O Allah! I seek refuge in you from the evil of my nafs.'"

### Ibn al-Qayyim — On the Signs of Happiness and Misery

**Scholar:** Imam Ibn al-Qayyim
**Source URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2014/11/19/happiness-misery-and-signs-of-their-people-ibn-qayyim-al-jawziyyah/

> "From the signs of happiness and joy is that whenever a worshipper increases in knowledge, his humbleness and mercy increases. Whenever he increases in acts of worship, his fear and caution increases. Whenever he increases in age, his greed decreases. Whenever he increases in wealth, his generosity and spending increases. And whenever he increases in status and position, his nearness to people increases as does his fulfilling their needs and disregarding their mistreatment."

> "And from the signs of misery is that whenever a worshipper increases in knowledge, his pride and arrogance increases. Whenever he increases in acts of worship, his bragging increases as does his belittling of people while always giving himself the benefit of the doubt. And whenever he increases in status and position, his arrogance and ego increases."

### Ibn al-Qayyim — A Sin That Leads to Jannah

**Scholar:** Imam Ibn al-Qayyim
**Source URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2009/07/23/a-sin-that-leads-to-jannah/

> "Sin may be more beneficial for a person, if it leads him to repent, than doing a lot of acts of worship. This is what is meant by the words of one of the salaf: 'A person may commit a sin and enter Paradise because of it, or he may do an act of worship and enter Hell because of it.'"

> "He may do a good deed and continue to think about it, and when he stands or sits or walks he remembers it and it fills him with self-admiration and pride, so it is the cause of his doom."

> "Undoubtedly this sin is better before Allah and is more likely to bring salvation than one who admires himself and looks down on others, and who thinks that he is doing Allah a favour."

### Ibn al-Qayyim — Me, Myself and I

**Scholar:** Imam Ibn al-Qayyim
**Source URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2009/06/17/me-myself-i/

> "Every Muslim should be warned of the oppression of: 'I – My/Mine – I Possess'"

> "So regarding the saying of the Slave of Allah 'I' — then the best that he can be described with is: I am a slave who sins, makes mistakes, seeks forgiveness and recognizes this."

### IslamQA — Ridding Oneself of Arrogance

**Source URL:** https://islamqa.info/en/answers/103432

> "We have seen — unfortunately — men who will marry a woman not so that the intimacy with her which was forbidden becomes permissible, and not so as to form a family with her — as is the aim of the shar'i contract — but so as to attain some worldly purpose, such as registering land, or getting a licence to open a business, or to get residency, or to enable the woman to travel outside her country. In none of these cases is the man a husband or the woman a wife in the real sense, rather he is a husband on paper only! This is a kind of toying with the rulings of sharee'ah. It is not permissible to do it or to take part in it."
