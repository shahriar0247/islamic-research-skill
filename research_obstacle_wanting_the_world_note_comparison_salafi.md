# Note comparison: Obstacle - Wanting the World

## Scope

Compared the supplied transcript of Ustadh Muhammad Tim Humble's 35-minute lesson with the substantive note links in the lesson comments: Nowshin B.'s Google Doc, Riyad's Notion page, Tawfeeq A.'s course Google Doc, and Raif's Arabic-evidence PDF.

## Result

Nowshin B.'s Google Doc is the most complete and closest overall match. It preserves the lesson's central distinction: lawful wealth itself is not blameworthy; craving and chasing it is. It includes the main sequence of evidences and all seven practical remedies.

Riyad's Notion page is the closest concise / structured version, but omits the rider-under-the-tree hadith and some of the lecturer's qualifications and explanatory detail. Tawfeeq A.'s course document correctly gives the outline but is not detailed enough for revision. Raif's PDF is an evidence handout rather than a complete note.

## Corrections needed in the best note

1. For Ibn Majah 4106, identify the report as weak but supported, matching the lecturer's explicit qualification.
2. Preserve the lecturer's wording that the dunya comes to the person whose intention is the Hereafter `while it is submissive`; avoid saying the Hereafter itself "chases" the person.
3. For the hadith that begins "The dunya is cursed", retain the lecturer's qualification that its chain has weakness but it has supporting narrations.
