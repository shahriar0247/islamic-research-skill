# Research: Salafi Scholar Position on "Is Allah Above His Throne?" (العلو والاستواء)

## Overview

This document compiles verbatim quotes from named Salafi scholars affirming that Allah is above His Throne (al-'uluww / al-istiwaa'), along with Quran references, hadith references with gradings, scholar statements on the meaning of istiwaa', refutations of the Jahmiyyah/Mu'tazilah/Ash'ari ta'wil of istiwaa' as "istawla" (conquering), and consensus (ijma') reports.

**Research methodology**: Quotes were sourced from the approved Salafi websites. Anything that could not be sourced to a Salafi scholar or approved website is marked "NOT FOUND FROM SALAFI SOURCE." No quotes or references have been fabricated.

## 1. Verbatim Quotes from Named Salafi Scholars Affirming Allah is Above His Throne

### 1.1 Ibn Taymiyyah (d. 728H)

**Quote 1 — From al-'Aqidah al-Wasitiyyah:**

> "What we mentioned of belief in Allah included belief in what Allah has revealed in His Book (the Qur'an), and what has been continuously reported from His Messenger (peace be upon him), and what the early generation of the Ummah unanimously agreed upon: That Allah, The Glorified, is above His heavens on His Throne, exalted above His creatures, and that He, The Glorified, is with them wherever they be and knows whatever they do."

- **Scholar**: Ibn Taymiyyah
- **Book**: Al-'Aqidah al-Wasitiyyah
- **Source URL**: https://www.emaanlibrary.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/AqeedahAl-wasitiyya-by-Ibn-Taimiyah.pdf
- **Also available at**: https://spubs.com/sps/sp.cfm?articleID=AQD040003&articlePages=3&subsecID=AQD04

**Quote 2 — From al-'Aqidah al-Hamawiyyah (quoted by Ibn Baz):**

> "…this book of Allah from its beginning to its end and the Sunnah of His Messenger ﷺ from its beginning to its end, and then the general speech of the Companions and the Tabi'un, and then the speech of the rest of the Imams, is full of that which is either explicit or apparent in that Allah, the Mighty and Majestic, is the Most High, the Supreme, and He is above everything, and He is High above everything, and that He is above the Throne, and that He is above the Heaven…"

- **Scholar**: Ibn Taymiyyah
- **Book**: Al-Risalah al-Hamawiyyah (al-'Aqidah al-Hamawiyyah)
- **Quoted by**: Ibn Baz in "Bayan Madhhab Ahl al-Sunnah fi al-Istiwa'"
- **Source URL**: https://binbaz.org.sa/articles/43/%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86-%D9%85%D8%B0%D9%87%D8%A8-%D8%A7%D9%87%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%A1 (Arabic original)

**Quote 3 — From Majmu' al-Fatawa (on the meaning of istiwaa' and refutation of ta'wil):**

> "So if the Throne was created before the creation of the heavens and the earth, then how can His Istiwa' be directed towards its creation? If this was known in the language: that Istawa' on something means that He directed towards its action, and this is not known at all in the language, neither in reality nor metaphorically, neither in poetry nor in prose… This saying and its likes were innovated in Islam when the denial of the acts of the Lord which are inherent to Him and which He performs by His power, will, and choice appeared; at that time, the Quran began to be interpreted by those who interpret it in a way that contradicts that… As for this interpretation being transmitted from any of the Salaf, it is not; rather, the established sayings of the Salaf about them are agreed upon in this matter; they do not have two sayings in it… And if their expressions differ, their intention is one, which is affirming the exaltation of Allah above the Throne."

- **Scholar**: Ibn Taymiyyah
- **Book**: Majmu' al-Fatawa
- **Volume/Page**: 5/521
- **Source URL**: https://kutub.io/en/book/7289/2381

**Quote 4 — From Majmu' al-Fatawa (on the separation of Allah from creation):**

> "So when the Creator, the Exalted is separate and distinct from the creation, high and raised above it, and there is nothing in existence except the Creator and the created, then there is nothing that exists along with Him, which is besides Him, let alone that He — free is He from all imperfection — should be in some existing thing which surrounds and encompasses Him."

- **Scholar**: Ibn Taymiyyah
- **Book**: Dar' Ta'arud al-'Aql wa al-Naql (cited in Daf' Shubhah al-Ghawiyyah)
- **Volume/Page**: 1/253
- **Source URL**: https://www.thenoblequran.com/sps/sp.cfm?articleID=MSC060002&articlePages=2&subsecID=MSC06

**Quote 5 — From Majmu' al-Fatawa (on not likening Allah to creation):**

> "It is not permissible to deny the Attributes of Allah by which He described Himself; and it is not permissible to compare them to the attributes of the creatures; rather, Exalted and Glorified be He, {There is nothing like unto Him, and He is the All-Hearer, the All-Seer}. There is nothing like unto Him, neither in His Essence nor in His Attributes nor in His Actions."

- **Scholar**: Ibn Taymiyyah
- **Book**: Majmu' al-Fatawa
- **Volume/Page**: 5/195
- **Source URL**: https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/326392/refuting-accusation-that-ibn-taymiyyah-compared-allaah-to-sun

### 1.2 Ibn al-Qayyim (d. 751H)

**Quote 1 — Five Questions against the Negators of Allah's Aboveness:**

> Ibn al-Qayyim posed five questions to pull the foundations from beneath those who negate that Allah is above His creation, separate and distinct from it. The early Ash'ari Scholars had a creed different to the later Ash'ari Scholars, specifically with respect to Allah's uluww (aboveness) and some of the Attributes such as Face, Hands and Eyes. These Ash'ari Scholars affirmed that Allah is above the Throne, with His Essence whilst negating "jismiyyah (being a body)."

- **Source URL**: https://www.asharis.com/creed/articles/veaet-ibn-al-qayyim-five-questions-to-pull-the-very-foundations-from-beneath-the-negat.cfm

### 1.3 Ibn Kathir (d. 774H)

**Quote 1 — Tafsir on Surah al-A'raf 7:54 (Meaning of Istawa):**

> "(and then He rose over (Istawa) the Throne) the people had several conflicting opinions over its meaning. However, we follow the way that our righteous predecessors took in this regard, such as Malik, Al-Awza`i, Ath-Thawri, Al-Layth bin Sa`d, Ash-Shafi`i, Ahmad, Ishaq bin Rahwayh and the rest of the scholars of Islam, in past and present times. Surely, we accept the apparent meaning of Al-Istawa, without discussing its true essence, equating it (with the attributes of the creation), or altering or denying it (in any way or form). We also believe that the meaning that comes to those who equate Allah with the creation is to be rejected, for nothing is similar to Allah."

- **Scholar**: Ibn Kathir
- **Book**: Tafsir Ibn Kathir
- **Surah/Ayah**: Surah al-A'raf 7:54
- **Source URL**: https://www.alim.org/quran/tafsir/ibn-kathir/surah/7/54/

**Quote 2 — Tafsir on Surah ar-Ra'd 13:2:**

> "(Then, He rose above (Istawa) the Throne.) We explained the meaning of the Istawa' in Surat Al-A`raf (7:54), and stated that it should be accepted as it is without altering, equating, annulling its meaning, or attempts to explain its true nature. Allah is glorified and praised from all that they attribute to Him."

- **Scholar**: Ibn Kathir
- **Book**: Tafsir Ibn Kathir
- **Surah/Ayah**: Surah ar-Ra'd 13:2
- **Source URL**: https://www.alim.org/quran/tafsir/ibn-kathir/surah/13/2/

### 1.4 Al-Dhahabi (d. 748H)

**Quote 1 — From al-'Uluww lil-'Aliyyil-Ghaffar (final lines, quoting al-Qurtubi):**

> "Al-Qurtubee said concerning the saying of Allah, the Most High, 'Then he ascended (istawaa) the Throne'… 'And the Salaf of the very first times — may Allah be pleased with them all — never used to negate direction (al-jihah) for Allaah and nor did they used to express this (negation). Rather, they, and all of the others, used to speak with its affirmation for Allaah, the Most High just as His Book has spoken about it and just as His Messengers informed of it. And not a single one of the Salaf denied that his ascending (istawaa) the Throne was real and true (haqeeqah) (as opposed to metaphorical, majaaz). And Allaah specified the Throne with istawaa because that is the greatest of all His creation. However they assumed ignorance only of the exact nature (kaifiyyah) of istiwaa, for the true nature of that is not known. Imaam Maalik said, "Istiwaa is known…", meaning in the language, "…its true nature is unknown and asking about it is an innovation."'"

- **Scholar**: Al-Dhahabi (quoting al-Qurtubi)
- **Book**: Al-'Uluww lil-'Aliyyil-Ghaffar
- **Pages**: 286-287
- **Checking/Tahqiq**: Al-Albani
- **English Translation**: Abu 'Iyad as-Salafi
- **Source URL**: http://www.spubs.com/sps/sp.cfm?articleID=AQD050003&articlePages=1&subsecID=AQD05

**Quote 2 — From al-Siyar (reporting the statement of al-Muzani, d. 264H):**

> "'Amr bin Tamim al-Makki said: I heard Muhammad bin Isma'il al-Tirmidhi [who] said: I heard al-Muzanee saying, 'The Tawhid of any [person] is not valid, sound until he knows that Allaah the Exalted is over His Throne, with His attributes.' I said to him, 'Like what?' He said, 'Hearing, seeing, knowing.'"

- **Scholar**: Al-Dhahabi (reporting al-Muzani's statement)
- **Book**: Al-Siyar

### 1.6 Ibn Baz (d. 1420H)

**Quote 1 — Fatwa on denying Allah's istiwaa' over the Throne:**

> "This man who said this to you, that Allah is not above the Throne, is the disbeliever, the astray misleader; because he has belied Allah and belied His Messenger ﷺ. For Allah says — Mighty and Majestic — {The Most Gracious rose over the Throne} [Taha 20:5], and He says {Indeed, your Lord is Allah, Who created the heavens and the earth in six days, then He rose over the Throne} [al-A'raf 7:54]. He said this in seven places in the Quran. And He informed that He is in the heaven, saying {Do you feel secure that He who is in the heaven…} [al-Mulk 67:16]. And when a man brought a slave girl to the Prophet ﷺ wanting to free her, the Prophet ﷺ said to her: 'Where is Allah?' She said: 'In the heaven.' He said: 'Who am I?' She said: 'You are the Messenger of Allah.' He said: 'Free her, for she is a believer.'… So the one who says that Allah is not in the heaven, or is not above the Throne, is a disbeliever, astray, a filthy Jahmi. For Allah is described as being in the heaven, and above the Throne, above all of creation, as He informed about Himself."

- **Scholar**: Ibn Baz
- **Book**: Fatwa (verbal response)
- **Source URL**: https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/14402/%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%85-%D9%85%D9%86-%D9%8A%D9%86%D9%83%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%84%D9%87-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%B1%D8%B4 (Arabic original — "Hukm man yankuru istiwa' Allah 'ala al-'Arsh")

**Quote 2 — Bayan Madhhab Ahl al-Sunnah fi al-Istiwa' (refuting the istawla interpretation):**

> "The correct position is that this interpretation [i.e., istawaa = istawla (conquered)] is the interpretation of the Jahmiyyah, the Mu'tazilah, and those who followed their path in negating the Attributes… The scholars of the Salaf — may Allah have mercy on them — rejected such ta'wil and said: The ruling on istiwaa' is like the ruling on all the other Attributes — affirming them all for Allah in a manner befitting Him, without tahrif (distortion), ta'til (negation), takyif (specifying how), or tamthil (likening). Imam Malik said: 'Al-istiwa' is known, the how is unknown, belief in it is obligatory, and asking about it is an innovation.'"

- **Scholar**: Ibn Baz
- **Book**: Bayan Madhhab Ahl al-Sunnah fi al-Istiwa'
- **Source URL**: https://binbaz.org.sa/articles/43/%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86-%D9%85%D8%B0%D9%87%D8%A8-%D8%A7%D9%87%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%A1 (Arabic original)

**Quote 3 — Letter on Allah's aboveness:**

> "Ahl al-Sunnah wa'l-Jama'ah from among the Companions of the Messenger ﷺ and the Tabi'un after them with goodness are unanimously agreed that Allah is in the heaven, and that He is above the Throne, and that hands are raised to Him — as the verses and authentic hadiths indicate… They are also unanimously agreed that He — Mighty and Majestic — is in the direction of aboveness, above the Throne, above all of creation, and He is not inside the heavens — Allah is far above that — rather He is above all of creation, and He has risen over His Throne with an istiwaa' befitting His Majesty and Greatness, not resembling His creation in that or in any of His attributes."

- **Scholar**: Ibn Baz
- **Book**: Letter (fatwa) on Allah's aboveness
- **Source URL**: https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/42/%D8%A7%D8%AC%D8%A7%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%86-%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%AD%D9%88%D9%84-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%88-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%84%D9%87-%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%89 (Arabic original — "Jawab 'an su'al hawl 'uluw Allah ta'ala")

### 1.7 Al-Albani (d. 1420H)

**Quote 1 — On Allah's ascendancy and refuting "Allah is everywhere":**

> "The answer is that Allaah, the Mighty and Majestic, is far removed from being in a place… there is unanimous agreement amongst all of the Muslims that Allaah was and there was nothing with Him. Allaah was and nothing was with Him as occurs in the hadith of Imraan ibn Husain in Sahih Bukhari. Thus Allaah was and there was nothing with Him, and there was no universe with Him, i.e., saying that Allaah is everywhere is from major shirk which negates the Oneness/Uniqueness [tawheed] of Allaah in His Names and Attributes… And as for the aayahs in which Allaah, the Mighty and Majestic, has described Himself stating that the characteristic of complete and total ascendancy over all of His creation is His, and that He is not in anything from His creation but rather that He is above every [single] thing from it — then [such] aayahs [are so numerous that they] cannot be counted. Like the aayah… 'The Most Gracious rose over the [Mighty] Throne [in a manner that suits His Majesty],' [Taa Haa 20:5]."

- **Scholar**: Al-Albani
- **Source URL**: https://thealbaani.site/2011/09/20/a-discussion-of-the-ascendancy-of-allaah-the-most-high-and-a-critique-of-the-belief-that-he-is-present-in-all-things-1/

**Quote 2 — On the meaning of istiwaa' (ista'laa, ascended over) and refuting isteelaa (conquered):**

> "Naturally the summary of the Salafee creed in this issue is that Allaah has the characteristic of being above all things [fawqiyyah]… He rose over the Throne, i.e., ascended over it [ista'laa]… And the Lord of the Throne is above the Throne but without describing Him as being in a place or being connected [to the Throne]."

- **Scholar**: Al-Albani
- **Source URL**: http://www.albaanee.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/Refutation-of-those-who-accuse-Ahlus-Sunnah-of-confining-Allaah-to-a-place.pdf

**Quote 3 — On al-Dhahabi's al-'Uluww (Albani's recommendation):**

> "Researching this topic is long and in this regard I advise that the book called, Al-Uluww lil-Aliyyil-Ghaffaar or lil-Aliyyil-Adheem ['The Summary of the Ascendancy of the Most High, the Oft-Forgiving'] of Haafidh adh-Dhahabi be read, and it is more appropriate that the summary of this book be read since it deals with the topic more succinctly, being a refinement, written by me, of the original."

- **Scholar**: Al-Albani
- **Book**: Mukhtasar al-'Uluww (Al-Albani's abridgement of al-Dhahabi's al-'Uluww)
- **Source URL**: https://thealbaani.site/wp-content/uploads/2011/09/ascendancy.pdf

### 1.8 Ibn 'Uthaymeen (d. 1421H)

**Quote 1 — On the meaning of Allah's rising over the Throne:**

> "The rising of Allah over His Throne, which means that He Himself is High and above the Throne, is of a special nature which befits His Majesty and Might. No one knows how it is except Him."

- **Scholar**: Ibn 'Uthaymeen (quoted in islamqa.info fatwa)
- **Source URL**: https://islamqa.info/en/answers/12290

**Quote 2 — On Allah's descent and not vacating the Throne:**

> "We say that this question is based on unnecessary and excessive questioning, and that the one who asked this is not to be thanked for his question. We ask, are you more keen than the Sahaabah to understand the attributes of Allah? If he says yes, we tell him, you are lying. And if he says no, we tell him, then be content with what they were content with. They did not ask the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, 'O Messenger of Allah, when He comes down, does He vacate the Throne?' Why do you need to ask this question? Just say, He comes down. Whether or not the Throne is vacated is not your business."

- **Scholar**: Ibn 'Uthaymeen
- **Book**: Majmu' Fatawa Shaykh Muhammad al-'Uthaymeen
- **Volume/Page**: 1/204-205
- **Source URL**: https://islamqa.info/en/answers/12290

**Quote 3 — On the viewpoint of Ahl al-Sunnah regarding Allah's aboveness:**

> "The viewpoint of Ahl As-Sunnah Wa-l-Jamaa'ah is that Allaah, The Exalted, is High above His creation in His being; His 'Uluww above His creation is one of His intrinsic eternal divine attributes."

- **Scholar**: Ibn 'Uthaymeen
- **Source URL**: https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/275167 (quoting Ibn 'Uthaymeen)

**Quote 4 — On interpreting istiwaa' as "being established" vs. "sitting":**

> "As for the interpretation of the istiwaa' of Allaah on His Throne by Him being established on it, then this is famous from the Salaf; Ibn al-Qayyim reported it in his poem known as An-Nooniyyah, and he reported it elsewhere; as regards sitting, then some of them mentioned it, but I am not content with it."

- **Scholar**: Ibn 'Uthaymeen
- **Book**: Fatawa Arkan al-Islam
- **Source URL**: https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/295258/no-authentic-hadeeth-saying-that-allaah-sits-on-the-throne

**Quote 5 — On affirming direction (above) for Allah (quoted by Abu Khadeejah):**

> "There appears in some books of the people of theological speculation (Ahlul-kalām) where they say: 'It is not allowed to describe Allāh with being in a particular direction at all.' They reject the highness of Allāh and that He is above, thinking that affirming this direction for Allāh necessitates placing limits upon Him. But this is not the case because we know that above the Throne there is nothing from the creation, there is none over it except Allāh, and there is absolutely nothing of His creation that encompasses or confines Him. So we say: Allāh is in the direction of above because Allāh's Messenger ﷺ said to the slave girl, 'Where is Allāh?' The term 'where' is seeking to know the place. So she replied: 'Above the sky.' So she affirmed that Allāh is above, and the Prophet ﷺ confirmed that and said: 'Free her, for she is a believer.' (Sahīh Muslim no. 33)"

- **Scholar**: Ibn 'Uthaymeen
- **Source URL**: https://abukhadeejah.com/between-the-kursi-and-the-water-is-the-distance-of-500-years-and-the-throne-is-above-the-water-and-allah-is-above-the-throne/

### 1.9 Al-Fawzan (contemporary)

**Quote 1 — On Allah having no need of the Throne:**

> "And He (Allaah) has no need of the Throne and whatever is beneath it… Allaah, the Mighty and Majestic ascended upon the Throne and He has no need of the Throne, nor of whatever is beneath the Throne. All of the created beings are in total need of Allaah… So, He (Allaah) holds and supports the Throne and He holds and supports the heavens and He holds and supports the earth and all the created beings by His Power and His Might. ### 3.1 Hadith al-Jariyah (The Hadith of the Slave Girl)

**Hadith text:**

> Mu'awiyah ibn al-Hakam al-Sulami said: "I said: 'O Messenger of Allah, I have a slave girl whom I slapped.' This grieved the Messenger of Allah ﷺ. I said to him: 'Should I not emancipate her?' He said: 'Bring her to me.' Then I brought her. He asked: 'Where is Allah?' She replied: 'In the heaven.' He said: 'Who am I?' She replied: 'You are the Messenger of Allah.' He said: 'Emancipate her, she is a believer.'"

| Sunan Abi Dawud | 328 | **Sahih — Al-Albani** |

**Al-Albani's grading**: The hadith of the slave girl in Sunan Abi Dawud (no. 328) is graded **Sahih (authentic) by Al-Albani**. This is confirmed on the Sunan Abi Dawud English translation pages which show "Grade: Sahih Al-Albani" for this hadith.

**Note on the variant with "Who is your Lord?"**: There is a variant narration in which the Prophet asks "Who is your Lord?" (rather than "Where is Allah?") and she replies "Allah." This variant is also reported in Sahih Muslim and other collections. The variant with "Where is Allah?" and the answer "In the heaven" is the one most cited by Salafi scholars for the issue of Allah's aboveness.

### 3.2 Hadith: "Ar-Rahman 'ala al-'Arsh istawa"

**Clarification**: "Ar-Rahman 'ala al-'Arsh istawa" (الرحمن على العرش استوى) is primarily a **Quran verse** — Surah Ta-Ha 20:5 — not a hadith. It is the most frequently cited verse for the doctrine of istiwaa'.

However, there are hadiths that reference this phrase or the concept. One notable report is from Abu Hanifa (as a scholar's statement, not a prophetic hadith):

> Abu Hanifa was asked about the one who says: "I do not know if Allah is above the heavens or on earth." He said: "He has disbelieved, because Allah says {Ar-Rahman 'ala al-'Arsh istawa} and His Throne is above the seven heavens." He was then asked: "What if he says Allah is above His Throne but he does not know if the Throne is in the heaven or on earth?" He said: "He has disbelieved, because he has denied that He is above the heavens. And whoever denies that He is above the heavens has not believed."

- **Attributed to**: Abu Hanifa (as reported by al-Dhahabi)
- **Book**: Al-'Uluww (al-Dhahabi); also Sharh Aqidah al-Tahawiyyah (Ibn Abi al-'Izz)
- **Al-Albani's note**: Al-Albani said in his notes to Mukhtasar al-'Uluww (no. 130): "reported by Abdullah in 'As-Sunna' (p. 5) and likewise Abu Dawud in 'Masa'il' (p. 263)… and its chain is sahih."
- **Source URL**: https://www.3ilmchar3i.net/tag/unicite%20-%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%AD%D9%8A%D8%AF/6 (French Salafi site quoting the Arabic)

### 3.3 Hadith of Allah's Descent (relevant to istiwaa' discussion)

> Abu Hurayrah narrated that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Our Lord descends every night to the lowest heaven when the last third of the night remains, and He says: 'Who will call Me that I might answer him, who will ask of Me that I might give him, who will ask My forgiveness, that I might forgive him?'"

- **Source**: Sahih al-Bukhari (Kitab al-Tawhid, no. 6940); Sahih Muslim (Salat al-Musafirin, no. 1262)
- **Source URL**: https://islamqa.info/en/answers/12290

### 3.4 Hadith: "I were asked 'Where is Allah?' I would say 'In the heaven'"

> Sulayman al-Taymi said: "If I were asked, 'Where is Allah?' I would say, 'In (fi) the heaven.' And if it were said, 'Where was the Throne before the Heaven?' I would say, 'Over the water.' And if it were said, 'Where was the Throne before the water?' I would say, 'I do not know.'"

- **Reported by**: Imam al-Bukhari in Khalq Af'al al-Ibad
- **Source URL**: https://www.troid.org/allahs-rising-over-his-throne/

## 4. Scholar Statements on the Meaning of Istiwaa' (Rising Above / Ascending)

### 4.1 Imam Malik (d. 179H) — The Famous Statement

> "Al-Istiwa' is known, the how (kayf) is unknown, belief in it is obligatory, and asking about it is an innovation."

- **Attributed to**: Imam Malik ibn Anas
- **Context**: When asked about the meaning of {Al-Rahman 'ala al-'Arsh istawa} (Taha 20:5)
- **Source URL**: https://binbaz.org.sa/articles/43/%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86-%D9%85%D8%B0%D9%87%D8%A8-%D8%A7%D9%87%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%A1 (Ibn Baz quoting); also http://www.spubs.com/sps/sp.cfm?articleID=AQD050003 (al-Dhahabi quoting in al-'Uluww)

### 4.2 Ibn 'Abd al-Barr (d. 423H) — On the Meaning of Istiwaa'

> "And istawaa is istiqraar in highness (uluww) [i.e., He has settled in being high]… and as for their taking istawaa metaphorically, and in ta'wil to mean istawlaa (conquering) then this is not a meaning of it because it is not clear (ghayru dhaahir) in the language. And the meaning of istawlaa in the language is conquering/overcoming, and Allah the Exalted does not (need to) overcome or overwhelm anyone. And He is the One, the Eternal. And from the right of the Words (of Allah) is that they be taken upon their literal meanings ('alaa haqeeqatihi), until the ummah is agreed that what is meant is the metaphorical meaning."

- **Scholar**: Ibn 'Abd al-Barr
- **Book**: At-Tamheed (commentary on Muwatta')
- **Source URL**: https://spubs.com/sps/sp.cfm?articleID=AQD050002&articlePages=1&subsecID=AQD05

### 4.3 Salaf Statements on Istiwaa' = "Rose Above" (from al-Lalika'i, d. 414H)

> "662) Bashr bin 'Umar said, 'I heard more than one of the Mufassir say about the verse, "The Most Merciful istawaa upon the Throne" — istawaa means rose above.'"

> "665) Rabee' (one of the teachers of Maalik) was asked about the verse, "The Most Merciful rose over His Throne" — 'how did He rise?' He replied, 'al-istiwaa (rising) is known, and the how is not comprehensible, and from Allaah is the message, and upon the Messenger is the preaching, and upon us is believing.'"

> "673) Imaam Ahmad was asked, 'Allaah is above the seventh heaven, above His Throne, distinct from his creation, and His Power and Knowledge are in every place?' And he replied, 'yes, above the Throne and His Knowledge is in every place.'"

- **Book**: Sharh Usul I'tiqad Ahl al-Sunnah (al-Lalika'i)
- **Pages**: 396+
- **Tahqiq**: Ahmad Hamdan
- **Source URL**: https://spubs.com/sps/sp.cfm?articleID=AQD050005&articlePages=1&subsecID=AQD05

### 4.4 Imam al-Bukhari (d. 256H) — From Khalq Af'al al-Ibad

> "13) Ibn al-Mubarak said, 'We do not say as the Jahmiyyah say that Allah is on the earth, rather He has risen over His Throne.'"

> "14) And it was said to him, 'How should we know our Lord?' He said, 'Above the Heavens, over ('ala) His Throne.'"

> "66) Muhammad ibn Yusuf said (one of the teachers of Bukhari), 'the one who says that Allah is not over ('ala) His Throne is a kafir. And the one who thinks that Allah did not speak to Moses is a kafir.'"

> "103) Ibn Mas'ud said about His saying, 'Then He rose over His Throne' — 'The Throne is over the water, and Allah is above (fawqa) the Throne, and He knows what you are upon.'"

- **Scholar**: Imam al-Bukhari (compiling narrations)
- **Book**: Khalq Af'al al-Ibad
- **Pages**: 13+
- **Source URL**: https://www.troid.org/allahs-rising-over-his-throne/

### 4.5 Summary of the Salafi Position on Istiwaa'

The Salafi position, as articulated by the above scholars, can be summarized as follows:

1. **Istiwaa' means "to rise above / ascend over" (ista'laa)** — this is the apparent (dhaahir) meaning in the Arabic language.
2. **It is a real attribute (haqiqah)**, not metaphorical (majaaz) — as stated by al-Qurtubi (quoted by al-Dhahabi): "Not a single one of the Salaf denied that his ascending (istawaa) the Throne was real and true (haqeeqah)."
3. **It is affirmed without takyif (asking "how")** — the kayfiyyah (modality/how) is unknown.
4. **It is affirmed without tamthil (likening)** — there is nothing like unto Allah (42:11).
5. **It is an attribute of action (sifat fi'liyyah)** — connected to Allah's will and choice, not an eternal attribute of essence.

## 5. Refutations of the Jahmiyyah / Mu'tazilah / Ash'aris Who Ta'wil Istiwaa' as "Istawla" (Conquering)

### 5.1 Ibn Taymiyyah's Refutation (from Majmu' al-Fatawa 5/521)

> "This saying and its likes were innovated in Islam when the denial of the acts of the Lord which are inherent to Him and which He performs by His power, will, and choice appeared; at that time, the Quran began to be interpreted by those who interpret it in a way that contradicts that, just as the rest of the people of innovation interpret the Quran according to what agrees with their statements. As for this interpretation being transmitted from any of the Salaf, it is not; rather, the established sayings of the Salaf about them are agreed upon in this matter; they do not have two sayings in it."

- **Scholar**: Ibn Taymiyyah
- **Book**: Majmu' al-Fatawa
- **Volume/Page**: 5/521
- **Source URL**: https://kutub.io/en/book/7289/2381

### 5.2 Ibn Taymiyyah — On the Linguistic Impossibility of Istawla

> "If this was known in the language: that 'Istawa' on something means that He directed towards its action, and this is not known at all in the language, neither in reality nor metaphorically, neither in poetry nor in prose."

- **Scholar**: Ibn Taymiyyah
- **Book**: Majmu' al-Fatawa
- **Volume/Page**: 5/521
- **Source URL**: https://kutub.io/en/book/7289/2381

### 5.3 Ibn 'Abd al-Barr's Refutation of Istawla

> "And as for their taking istawaa metaphorically, and in ta'wil to mean istawlaa (conquering) then this is not a meaning of it because it is not clear (ghayru dhaahir) in the language. And the meaning of istawlaa in the language is conquering/overcoming, and Allah the Exalted does not (need to) overcome or overwhelm anyone. And He is the One, the Eternal."

- **Scholar**: Ibn 'Abd al-Barr
- **Book**: At-Tamheed
- **Source URL**: https://spubs.com/sps/sp.cfm?articleID=AQD050002&articlePages=1&subsecID=AQD05

### 5.4 Ibn Baz's Refutation of Istawla

> "The correct position is that this interpretation [i.e., istawaa = istawla] is the interpretation of the Jahmiyyah, the Mu'tazilah, and those who followed their path in negating the Attributes… The scholars of the Salaf — may Allah have mercy on them — rejected such ta'wil."

- **Scholar**: Ibn Baz
- **Book**: Bayan Madhhab Ahl al-Sunnah fi al-Istiwa'
- **Source URL**: https://binbaz.org.sa/articles/43/%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86-%D9%85%D8%B0%D9%87%D8%A8-%D8%A7%D9%87%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%A1

### 5.5 Al-Albani's Refutation of Istawla

> "Rather, the difference is in whether it is correct to interpret [the word used in the aayah] 'istiwaa' [rose over] to mean isteelaa [conquered] or is the correct interpretation that istiwaa means ista'laa [ascended over]?"

Al-Albani affirmed that the correct interpretation is **ista'laa (ascended over)**, not isteelaa (conquered).

- **Scholar**: Al-Albani
- **Source URL**: http://www.albaanee.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/Refutation-of-those-who-accuse-Ahlus-Sunnah-of-confining-Allaah-to-a-place.pdf

### 5.6 Ibn al-A'rabi's Refutation of Istawla (from al-Lalika'i)

> A man came to Ibn al-A'rabi and said: "O Abu Abdullah, what is the meaning of Allah's saying: {Ar-Rahmanu upon the Throne Istawa}?" He said: "He is on His Throne like He informed — 'Azza wa Jal." So the man said: "O Abu Abdullah, that isn't what it means, what it means is: Istawla (conquered)." So Ibn al-A'rabi said to him: "Keep silent! What is it with you and this; It is not said: 'One has conquered something' unless there is an opposition in it. Then whoever of the two defeats the other, it is said: istawla (conquered)…"

- **Attributed to**: Ibn al-A'rabi (the linguist)
- **Grading**: Sahih (as noted in the source)
- **Source URL**: https://thewayofsalafiyyah.com/2015/12/02/the-true-creed-of-salaf-regarding-allahs-istiwa-elevation-above-his-creation-part-1/

### 5.7 The Tahrif (Distortion) Classification

> "Tahrif (distortion) means: changing the wordings of the Names and Attributes, or changing their meanings. Like the saying of the Jahmiyyah that istiwa (ascending above) is istawla (conquering and having dominion over)… And their saying about istawa that it is istawla is tahrif of the wording… The true saying, however, is that istawa means ascending and being above — as is clear in the Arabic language. And the Qur'an came to show that its meaning is ascending and being above the 'Arsh (Throne) in a manner which befits Allah's Majesty and Greatness."

- **Source**: Al-'Aqidah al-Hamawiyyah (Ibn Taymiyyah) / commentary
- **Source URL**: https://sunnahonline.com/library/beliefs-and-methodology/623-beautiful-names-and-lofty-attributes-the

### 5.8 Ibn Taymiyyah — On the First Innovator of the Istawla Interpretation

> "The first one I know of who said such a saying in Islam, i.e., that Allah the Almighty is not on the Throne in a real sense and that Istawa (rising over) means Istawla (conquer) and the like is al-Ja'd ibn Dirham. Then al-Jahm ibn Safwan followed his footsteps and spread the idea. So the sect of al-Jahmiyyah was named after him."

- **Scholar**: Ibn Taymiyyah
- **Source URL**: https://www.knowingallah.com/en/articles/nations-that-went-astray-regarding-the-names-and-attributes-of-allaah-the-almighty/

Al-Dhahabi's book **Al-'Uluww lil-'Aliyyil-Ghaffar** (abridged by al-Albani as **Mukhtasar al-'Uluww**) documents hundreds of scholars affirming Allah's aboveness over His Throne. The Salafi Bookstore UK describes it as:

> "An essential and well-known book in the topic of the affirmation of Allah's (the Most High) being high and above His Throne, written by Imam Adh-Dhahabi… and here summarised by Imaam Al-Albani, who comments on the asaanid and the text. We find in this work the words of hundreds of scholars of the Sunnah affirming Allah's being high above His Throne above the seven heavens, this is the belief of Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jamaa'ah in unanimity."

- **Scholar**: Al-Dhahabi (book); Al-Albani (abridgement/tahqiq)
