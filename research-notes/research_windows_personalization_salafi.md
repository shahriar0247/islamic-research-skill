# Research: Islamic Ruling on Using Third-Party Tools to Change Windows Personalization Settings (Salafi Scholarly Opinions)

## Executive Summary

**Key Finding:** Based on the researched Salafi sources, using third-party tools to modify Windows settings (like dark mode/light mode) that Microsoft has locked behind activation **appears to be impermissible (haram)** because:

1. It violates the terms and conditions agreed to when using the software
2. The Prophet (ﷺ) stated: "The Muslims are bound by their conditions"
3. Violating licensing conditions constitutes a breach of intellectual property rights
4. No specific Salafi fatwa was found addressing the exact scenario of third-party personalization tools, but the general principles on fulfilling contracts and respecting software terms apply

**Important Distinction:**
- **A) Piracy/Cracking (KMS, activators, cracks)** - **CLEARLY HARAM** (explicitly stated in multiple fatwas)
- **B) Third-party tools to change settings without activation** - **LIKELY HARAM** based on general principles of fulfilling contracts and conditions, though no direct fatwa addresses this specific scenario

## Part A: Piracy/Cracking Software - Clearly Haram

### 1. IslamQA (Shaykh Muhammad Salih al-Munajjid)

**Source:** https://islamqa.info/amp/en/answers/102352

**Quote:**
> "If the owners and inventors of the program have stated that it is copyrighted, and that is not permitted to make copies of it for personal or public use, then the basic principle is that this condition of theirs should be fulfilled, because the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allaah be upon him) said: 'The Muslims are bound by their conditions.' And he (peace and blessings of Allaah be upon him) said: 'Whoever reaches a permissible thing (that is permissible to anyone) first is more entitled to it.' This is what was quoted as evidence by Shaykh Ibn Baaz (may Allaah have mercy on him) in his fatwa with the Standing Committee. See Fataawa al-Lajnah al-Daa'imah (13/188)."

**Quote:**
> "This is supported by the fact that copyright of writings, inventions and products, and other tangible and intangible rights, are guaranteed and it is not permissible to transgress against them without the permission of the owners. That includes tapes, disks and books."

**Quote:**
> "Undoubtedly those who produce tapes and disks have put time, effort and money into producing them, and there is nothing in sharee'ah to prevent them from taking the profit that results from this work. The one who transgresses against their rights is wronging them and consuming their wealth unlawfully."

### 2. IslamWeb

**Source:** https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/327097/to-what-extent-one-is-sinful-for-using-pirated-software

**Quote:**
> "According to the predominant opinion in this regard, it is absolutely impermissible to use pirated software. The Council of the Islamic Fiqh Academy (IIFA), in its fifth session in Kuwait, stated, 'Copyrights belong to their holders and have, in contemporary times, financial value that can be traded. These rights are recognized by the sharee'ah, and it is impermissible to violate them.'"

**Source:** https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/326080/using-cracked-software-for-lack-of-legitimate-software

**Quote:**
> "The council of the International Islamic Fiqh Academy (IIFA), in its fifth session, asserted the necessity of observing copyrights and the prohibition of violating them and that authorship and invention are rights belonging to their holders and have, in contemporary times, financial value which can be traded. These rights are recognized by Islamic law, and it is impermissible to violate them."

**Quote:**
> "Accordingly, it is impermissible for you to use these cracked software programs to earn a living."

**Source:** https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/268114/jail-breaking-iphone-or-windows-system

**Quote:**
> "These programs and the likes of them are private financial rights of their makers; they alone are entitled to dispose of the software by selling them or permitting their usage. Therefore, no one has the right to violate these rights by jail-breaking, copying or downloading the programs without the permission of their makers. There is no difference in this regard whether the program makers are Muslims or non-Muslims or whether the person who copies them needs them or not."

### 3. IslamWeb - Violating Licensing Conditions

**Source:** https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/264511/violating-licensing-conditions-of-software-programs

**Quote:**
> "With regard to the first part of your question regarding the violation of the terms and conditions of the owner of the software program, according to the preponderant opinion, it is an obligation to abide by those terms and it is not permissible to violate them. These conditions imply the possession of a utility to utilize it according to a determined extent and a specific manner. Based on such conditions, the contract has been concluded and agreed upon. So, violating such conditions constitutes a breach of the intellectual rights."

### 4. Shaykh Ibn 'Uthaymin on Conditions

**Source:** https://islamqa.info/en/answers/308096

**Quote:**
> "Shaykh Ibn 'Uthaymin (may Allah have mercy on him) said: To conclude, the basic principle is that stipulating conditions is something that is permissible and valid, whether that has to do with marriage, buying and selling, renting and hiring, collateral, or endowments (waqfs). The ruling on stipulated conditions in contracts is that if the conditions are valid and permissible, they must be fulfilled, in the case of marriage and other matters, because of the general meaning of the verse in which Allah, may He be exalted, says (interpretation of the meaning): {O you who have believed, fulfill [all] contracts} [al-Ma'idah 5:1]. Fulfilling contracts includes fulfilling all the conditions and clauses thereof, because all of that is part of the contract." (Ash-Sharh al-Mumti' 12/164).

## Part C: Intellectual Property Rights - Salafi Scholarly Consensus

### 1. Islamic Fiqh Council Statement

**Source:** https://islamqa.info/en/answers/34510

**Quote:**
> "The Islamic Fiqh Council has issued a statement forbidding transgression against the intangible rights of others (such as copyright and patents etc). The text of this statement is as follows: Trade names, business names, trademarks, written works, inventions and patents are all rights that belong to their owners or authors. According to modern customs and usage they have acquired considerable monetary value. These rights are respected in sharee'ah and it is not permissible to transgress against them."

**Quote:**
> "This applies equally to both Muslims and kaafirs who are not at war against Muslims."

## Part D: Software Terms and Conditions - Islamic Ruling

### 1. IslamWeb on Breaching Terms and Conditions

**Source:** https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/283420/breaching-the-terms-and-conditions-of-downloaded-software

**Quote:**
> "One must adhere to the conditions that he accepts relating to the permission of the owner of the software program for downloading it and the like, unless these are conditions for something unlawful, because unlawful conditions are void and it is not an obligation to fulfill them. In fact, it is not permissible to fulfill such conditions."

**Quote:**
> "Not fulfilling such conditions is not a breach of contract or covenant as the Prophet, sallallaahu 'alayhi wa sallam, said to 'Aa'ishah when she sponsored the manumission of Bareerah, while Bareerah's master had set a condition that he would be the Walaa' beneficiary of Bareerah's inheritance (i.e. the right of inheritance would vest in them): 'Take her (free her) and set the condition that they are her Walaa' beneficiary, as the Walaa' belongs to the one who sponsors the manumission,' and 'Aa'ishah did so. Then, the Prophet, sallallaahu 'alayhi wa sallam, stood up and addressed the people; he praised Allaah and then said: 'What is the matter with people who stipulate conditions that are not in the Book of Allaah. Every condition that is not in the Book of Allaah is not valid, even if they stipulate a hundred conditions.'"

**Source:** https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/324250/violating-terms-of-using-websites-does-not-entail-expiation

**Quote:**
> "The users should abide by the terms and conditions of the website as long as they are not against the Islamic sharee'ah. Allaah, The Exalted, says (what means): {O you who have believed, fulfill (all) contracts....} [Quran 5:1] The Prophet, sallallaahu 'alayhi wa sallam, said, 'Muslims are bound by their conditions, except the conditions that make what is lawful unlawful, or what is unlawful lawful.' [At-Tirmithi classified it as hasan saheeh (good or sound)]"

## Part E: Your Specific Scenario - Analysis

### Scenario Description:
- User wants to use Windows without activating it (no piracy/cracking)
- Microsoft allows installation and use of unactivated Windows
- Microsoft restricts personalization features (dark mode, light mode, wallpapers, themes, colors) to activated copies only
- User wants to use THIRD-PARTY TOOLS (not cracks, not activation bypasses, not KMS emulators) to change settings like dark mode/light mode
- These tools modify Windows settings directly via registry or system APIs
- They do NOT activate Windows, do NOT bypass WGA, do NOT remove watermarks, and do NOT pirate anything

### Analysis Based on Research:

**NOT FOUND FROM SALAFI SOURCE:** No direct fatwa was found addressing the specific scenario of using third-party tools to modify Windows personalization settings without activation.

**However, the following general principles apply:**

1. **Terms and Conditions are Binding:**
   - When you install Windows, you agree to Microsoft's terms and conditions
   - Microsoft's terms state that personalization features require activation
   - Using third-party tools to bypass this restriction violates the agreed terms
   - IslamWeb states: "according to the preponderant opinion, it is an obligation to abide by those terms and it is not permissible to violate them"

2. **The Hadith Principle:**
   - "The Muslims are bound by their conditions" (Sahih, graded by al-Albani)
   - This applies to all valid conditions that do not make halal haram or haram halal
   - Restricting personalization to activated copies is a valid business condition

3. **Intellectual Property Rights:**
   - The Islamic Fiqh Council states that copyright and related rights are protected in Sharia
   - Violating licensing conditions constitutes a breach of intellectual property rights

4. **Distinction from Piracy:**
   - Your scenario is different from piracy/cracking (which is clearly haram)
   - However, it still involves violating the terms of use agreed upon with Microsoft
   - The violation is of the usage restrictions, not of the licensing itself

### Conclusion:

Based on the general principles found in Salafi sources:

**Using third-party tools to change Windows personalization settings that Microsoft has locked behind activation is LIKELY HARAM because:**

1. It violates the terms and conditions agreed to when using Windows
2. The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "The Muslims are bound by their conditions"
3. Violating software terms constitutes a breach of intellectual property rights
4. The restriction of personalization features is a valid business condition (not making halal haram or haram halal)

**However:**
- No specific Salafi fatwa was found addressing this exact scenario
- This conclusion is based on applying general principles to the specific case
- A person should consult with a knowledgeable Salafi scholar for a direct ruling on this specific scenario

## Part F: Alternative Recommendations

### 1. Use Free/Open Source Alternatives

**Source:** https://islamqa.info/index.php/en/answers/116782/downloading-free-books-and-programs-from-the-internet

**Quote:**
> "Similarly, there is nothing wrong with downloading free computer programs and apps that are available for the Internet."

**Source:** https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/430804/asking-for-money-to-make-an-open-source-software-program

**Quote:**
> "If someone wants to make these hardware and software products to be open source and available for people to benefit from for free, there is nothing wrong with asking people to donate money for this purpose, and this is not considered begging (dispraised asking for money) but rather falls under the category of cooperation in righteousness."

**Recommendation:** Consider using Linux distributions (e.g., Ubuntu, Fedora, Linux Mint) which are:
- Completely free and open source
- Do not require activation
- Allow full personalization without restrictions
- Permissible to use according to Islamic principles

### 2. Purchase Legitimate Windows License

If Windows is necessary for specific reasons:
- Purchase a legitimate license to activate Windows
- This fulfills the contract and allows access to personalization features
- Supports the rights of the software creators

### 3. Accept Unactivated Windows Limitations

- Use Windows in its unactivated state as permitted by Microsoft
- Accept the limitations on personalization features
- This is the most cautious approach from an Islamic perspective
