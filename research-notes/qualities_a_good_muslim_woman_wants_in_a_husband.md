# Qualities a Good Muslim Woman Wants in a Husband

**صفات الزوج الصالح التي تطلبها المرأة المسلمة**

*Based on the Quran, Sunnah, and the statements of Salafi scholars — Ibn Baz, Ibn Uthaymeen, al-Fawzan, Ibn Taymiyyah, Ibn al-Qayyim, Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di, al-Albani (may Allah have mercy on them all).*

---

## A Note on "Arab" vs "Non-Arab" and "Traditional" vs "Non-Traditional"

The Salafi scholars do **not** categorize women by Arab/non-Arab or traditional/non-traditional. They address all Muslim women with the **same religious criteria**. Ibn al-Qayyim stated:

> "Compatibility in marriage is only in religious commitment. A Muslim woman should not be given in marriage to a disbeliever, or a chaste woman to an immoral man. The Quran and Sunnah do not pay attention to any compatibility beyond that."
> — **Ibn al-Qayyim, Zad al-Ma'ad, 5/144**

Shaykh Ibn Baz called lineage-based compatibility restrictions "a great error, a monstrous ignorance, oppression against the woman." The Prophet ﷺ said: "There is no superiority of an Arab over a non-Arab... except in taqwa." (Musnad Ahmad; cited by Ibn al-Qayyim)

So the qualities below apply to **every** Muslim woman seeking a husband, regardless of culture.

---

## The Foundational Principle

The Prophet ﷺ gave the primary criterion for choosing a husband:

> **"إِذَا جَاءَكُمْ مَنْ تَرْضَوْنَ دِينَهُ وَخُلُقَهُ فَأَنْكِحُوهُ"**
> "If there comes to you one whose **religion** and **character** pleases you, then marry [her to him]."
> — **Tirmidhi 1084; graded Sahih by al-Albani**

Shaykh Ibn Baz said:

> **"تلتمس الرجل الطيب المعروف بالخير المحافظ على الصلوات، صاحب العقيدة الطيبة"**
> "Seek the good man known for righteousness, maintaining the prayers, with good creed."
> — **Ibn Baz, Fatwa: "صفات الزوج الصالح"** (https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/11199/%D8%B5%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B2%D9%88%D8%AC-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B5%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD)

And on rejecting a suitor who lacks these:

> **"قد أحسنت في رفضه... هذا لا يرضى خلقه، ولا دينه"**
> "You did well in rejecting him... his character is not pleasing, nor is his religion."
> — **Ibn Baz** (https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/15427/%D9%88%D8%AC%D9%88%D8%A8-%D8%B1%D9%81%D8%B6-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B2%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AC-%D8%A8%D9%85%D9%86-%D9%84%D8%A7-%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%86-%D9%84%D9%87-%D9%88%D9%84%D8%A7-%D8%AE%D9%84%D9%82)

## The Complete List of Qualities

### 1. Religious Commitment (الدين) — The Most Important

The single most important quality. Shaykh al-Ghudayaan said:

> "The most important attributes which a woman should seek are **good character and Din**. As regards wealth and lineage, then these are secondary. The one possessing Din and good character will be a blessing for her and her children, and she will learn good manners and the Din from him."
> — **Troid.org / Shaykh al-Ghudayaan**

Specifically, a woman should verify:
- He **prays** (the one who neglects Allah's rights will neglect hers — IslamQA 5202)
- He has **correct aqeedah** (creed)
- He avoids **haram** (alcohol, zina, smoking, shaving the beard — Ibn Baz, fatwa 10813: https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/10813/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AC%D8%A8-%D8%AA%D9%88%D9%81%D8%B1%D9%87%D8%A7-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B2%D9%88%D8%AC%D9%8A%D9%86)
- He does not abandon prayer (a non-praying man is a disbeliever and not a match for a Muslim woman — Ibn Baz, Fatwa Islamiyah)

### 2. Good Character (حسن الخلق)

> **"خَيْرُكُمْ خَيْرُكُمْ لِأَهْلِهِ وَأَنَا خَيْرُكُمْ لِأَهْلِي"**
> "The best of you are the best to their families, and I am the best of you to my family."
> — **Tirmidhi 3895; Hasan Sahih**

The Prophet ﷺ warned Fatimah bint Qays about Abu Jaham: "His stick never leaves his shoulder" — meaning he beat women. A woman should seek a man who is **gentle, patient, kind, not harsh, not violent**.

### 3. Ability to Provide (النفقة / القدرة على الإنفاق)

> **"الرِّجَالُ قَوَّامُونَ عَلَى النِّسَاءِ... وَبِمَا أَنفَقُوا مِنْ أَمْوَالِهِمْ"**
> "Men are maintainers of women... because they spend from their wealth."
> — **Quran 4:34**

The Prophet ﷺ told Fatimah bint Qays about Mu'awiyah: "He is a poor man who has no wealth." It is not required that he be rich — only that he has **enough halal income to support his family without begging**. (IslamQA 5202; Muslim 1480)

### 4. Good Treatment and Companionship (المعاشرة بالمعروف)

> **"وَعَاشِرُوهُنَّ بِالْمَعْرُوفِ"**
> "And live with them in kindness."
> — **Quran 4:19**

Ibn Baz described the husband's obligation:

> **"أن يعاشرها بالمعروف، فلا يؤذيها، ولا يضربها بغير حق، ولا يعنف عليها بغير حق، ولا يكون معبسًا في وجهها بغير حق، ولا يقصر في نفقتها بغير حق، بل عليه أن يقوم بنفقتها المعتادة... وأن يكون حسن الخلق، طيب البشر مع زوجته"**
> "He must treat her with good treatment — not harm her, not beat her without right, not be harsh, not frown at her, not fall short in her spending. Rather he must provide her customary spending... and be of good character, cheerful with his wife."
> — **Ibn Baz** (https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/15561/%D8%AD%D9%82%D9%88%D9%82-%D9%83%D9%84-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B2%D9%88%D8%AC%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AE%D8%B1)

### 5. Gentleness and Patience with Her Faults

> **"لَا يَفْرَكْ مُؤْمِنٌ مُؤْمِنَةً إِنْ كَرِهَ مِنْ خُلُقِهَا شَيْئًا رَضِيَ مِنْ خُلُقِهَا شَيْئًا آخَرَ"**
> "No believing man should hate a believing woman. If he dislikes one of her characteristics, he may be pleased with another."
> — **Sahih Muslim**

Ibn Kathir explained 4:19: "Live with them honorably — by saying kind words, treating them kindly, and making your appearance appealing for them, as much as you can, just as you like the same from them."

### 6. Protectiveness (الغيرة الشرعية)

Shaykh al-Fawzan said:

> "The Prophet ordered us to take good care of the women... what is more important is to **protect them from indecencies** — the woman is weak — and from trying appearances and letting them do whatever they want."
> — **al-Fawzan** (abdurrahman.org)

A woman wants a man with **praiseworthy ghayrah** — protective jealousy that guards her honor, not the blameworthy kind that is baseless suspicion.

### 7. Chastity and Lowering the Gaze (العفة وغض البصر)

> **"قُل لِّلْمُؤْمِنِينَ يَغُضُّوا مِنْ أَبْصَارِهِمْ وَيَحْفَظُوا فُرُوجَهُمْ"**
> "Tell the believing men to lower their gaze and guard their chastity."
> — **Quran 24:30**

Ibn al-Qayyim stated: "A chaste woman should not be given to an immoral man." A woman should seek a man who **guards his own chastity** — one who does not look at other women, does not free-mix, does not flirt.

### 8. Knowledge of the Quran and Sunnah (العلم)

> "It is preferable that he should have **knowledge of the Quran and Sunnah**; if you find someone like this it is good, otherwise realize that this is something rare."
> — **IslamQA 5202**

The man is the **shepherd** of his household:

> **"كُلُّكُمْ رَاعٍ وَكُلُّكُمْ مَسْئُولٌ عَنْ رَعِيَّتِهِ... وَالرَّجُلُ رَاعٍ فِي أَهْلِهِ وَهُوَ مَسْئُولٌ عَنْ رَعِيَّتِهِ"**
> "Each of you is a shepherd... a man is the shepherd of his household and is responsible for them."
> — **Bukhari 7138, Muslim 1829**

### 9. Good Family and Lineage (الحسب والنسب) — Secondary

> "It is preferable that he should come from a **good family and a known lineage**. If two men propose and are equal in religion, preference is given to the one from a good family — because the righteousness of his close relatives could be passed on to his children."
> — **IslamQA 5202**

But this is **secondary** to religion. Ibn al-Qayyim and Ibn Baz both emphasized: compatibility is **only** in religion, not in lineage, wealth, or social status.

### 10. Physical/Appearance (الجمال)

The Prophet ﷺ commanded looking at the prospective spouse:

> **"انْظُرْ إِلَيْهَا فَإِنَّهُ أَحْرَى أَنْ يُؤْدَمَ بَيْنَكُمَا"**
> "Look at her, for that is more likely to create lasting affection between you."
> — **Tirmidhi 1087; graded Sahih by al-Albani**

Physical attraction is legitimate and acknowledged by the scholars. Ibn Uthaymeen described "physical beauty" as one of the two types of beauty sought in marriage (the other being "spiritual beauty" — religion and character). (IslamWeb 163819)

### 11. Sexual Fulfillment (إفاءة الحق)

Ibn Taymiyyah stated:

> "The husband must render his wife her conjugal rights in a goodly manner (bil-ma'ruf). This is one of her **most crucial rights**. It is more important than even feeding her."
> — **Ibn Taymiyyah, Majmu' al-Fatawa**

A woman has the right to intimacy. A husband who abandons his wife for long periods without her permission must return within half a year. (Ibn Uthaymeen, fatwaislam.com)

### 12. Justice and Fairness (العدل) — If Polygamous

> **"وَلَن تَسْتَطِيعُوا أَن تَعْدِلُوا بَيْنَ النِّسَاءِ وَلَوْ حَرَصْتُمْ"**
> "You will never be able to do perfect justice between wives even if it is your ardent desire."
> — **Quran 4:129**

> "A man who has two wives and does not deal justly with them will be resurrected on the Day of Judgment with half his body paralysed."
> — **Tirmidhi 1141**

### 13. Not Being Abusive (عدم الإيذاء)

> **"لَا ضَرَرَ وَلَا ضِرَارَ"**
> "There should be neither harming nor reciprocating harm."
> — **Ibn Majah; graded Sahih by al-Albani**

The hadith of Mu'awiyah al-Qushayri specifies the wife's rights:

> "That you should **feed her when you eat, clothe her when you clothe yourself, do not strike her face, do not insult her**, and do not separate from her except in the house."
> — **Abu Dawud 2142; graded Sahih by al-Albani in Irwa' al-Ghalil 7/98**

### 14. Helping in the House (الإعانة في البيت)

> **"كَانَ فِي مِهْنَةِ أَهْلِهِ"**
> "He used to serve his household."
> — **Bukhari 5363** (Aisha describing the Prophet ﷺ)

The best of creation helped in his house. A woman values a man who is not above serving his family.

### 15. Consulting Her (المشاورة)

The Prophet ﷺ consulted Umm Salama at the Treaty of Hudaybiyyah and followed her advice. A woman wants a man who **listens to her opinion** and involves her in family decisions.

### 16. Being a Good Father (الأبوة الصالحة)

> "A man is responsible for his family with regard to **teaching them** and making sure that they follow Islam."
> — **IslamQA 13802**

A woman seeks a man who will be a **religious guide, teacher, and protector** for her future children — not just a provider.

### 17. Giving the Mahr Willingly (إيتاء المهر)

> **"وَآتُوا النِّسَاءَ صَدُقَاتِهِنَّ نِحْلَةً"**
> "And give the women their dowry with a good heart."
> — **Quran 4:4**

As-Sa'di explained: "with a good heart, willingly, with peace of mind — do not delay it or diminish anything from it." Ibn Baz added: the mahr should be **small and easy**, not a burden.

### 18. Respecting Her Right to Consent (الاستئذان)

> **"الثَّيِّبُ أحقُّ بنفسِها من وليِّها والبِكرُ يستأمرُها أبوها"**
> "The previously married woman has more right to herself than her guardian, and the virgin's father seeks her permission."
> — **Muslim 1421; graded Sahih by al-Albani**

A woman wants a man who **respects her agency** — and a wali who does not force her. Ibn Baz stated clearly: "Her wali has no right to force her to marry someone she does not accept."

### 19. Stipulating Conditions (اشتراط الشروط)

Ibn Taymiyyah ruled that a woman **can stipulate conditions** in her marriage contract — such as that he not take a second wife, or that she not move from her home:

> "Yes, these conditions and similar ones are valid according to the madhhab of Imam Ahmad and others among the Sahabah and Tabi'een."
> — **Ibn Taymiyyah** (IslamQA 143120)

### 20. Tranquility, Love, and Mercy (السكينة والمودة والرحمة)

The ultimate goal of marriage, as Allah stated:

> **"وَمِنْ آيَاتِهِ أَنْ خَلَقَ لَكُم مِنْ أَنْفُسِكُمْ أَزْوَاجًا لِتَسْكُنُوا إِلَيْهَا وَجَعَلَ بَيْنَكُم مَّوَدَّةً وَرَحْمَةً"**
> "And of His signs is that He created for you spouses that you may find tranquility in them; and He placed between you affection and mercy."
> — **Quran 30:21**

As-Sa'di explained: spouses are created "compatible with you and you with them... that you may find tranquility in them, and He placed between you love and mercy."

A woman wants a man who brings her **tranquility (sukun)**, not anxiety; **love (mawaddah)**, not coldness; **mercy (rahmah)**, not harshness.

## What a Woman Should Avoid

Shaykh Ibn Baz listed the traits to avoid in a husband:

> **"لا سكيرًا، ولا حليقًا للحيته، ولا صاحب تدخين، ولا صاحب زنا"**
> "Not a drunkard, not one who shaves his beard, not a smoker, not one who commits adultery."
> — **Ibn Baz** (https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/10813/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AC%D8%A8-%D8%AA%D9%88%D9%81%D8%B1%D9%87%D8%A7-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B2%D9%88%D8%AC%D9%8A%D9%86)

And al-Ghudayaan added: "She should avoid him — especially those who are **negligent about the prayers**, or one who is known to **drink intoxicants**."

A man who does not pray is a disbeliever and **not a match** for a Muslim woman. (Ibn Baz, Fatwa Islamiyah)

---

## Conclusion

The qualities a good Muslim woman wants in a husband are not cultural preferences — they are **religious criteria** established by the Quran, Sunnah, and the scholars of this Ummah. The foundation is **deen and khuluq** (religion and character). Everything else — wealth, lineage, appearance, knowledge — is secondary and weighed against these two.

A woman has the **right to choose**, the **right to consent**, the **right to reject** a suitor who lacks religion and character, and the **right to stipulate conditions** in her marriage contract. Her wali must seek her permission and cannot force her.

The man she seeks is one who will bring her **tranquility, love, and mercy** — as Allah intended when He said: **"لِتَسْكُنُوا إِلَيْهَا"** (that you may find tranquility in them).
