## 1. Kitab at-Tawheed

### Author and historical context

**Source: Abu Khadeejah, "Part 1. Kitab at-Tawhid: An amazing authorship..."**  
URL: https://abukhadeejah.com/kitab-at-tawhid-an-amazing-authorship-and-journey-into-the-history-of-saudi-arabia-and-muhammad-ibn-abdul-wahhab-part-1/

- Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab was born in the village of al-'Uyaynah in Najd. His father was a Hanbali scholar and qadi. He memorised the Qur'an by age ten, studied fiqh, tafsir and hadith, and travelled to Makkah, Madinah, Basra, Baghdad, etc.
- He witnessed grave worship, tree worship, amulets, soothsaying, etc. in Najd and elsewhere.
- "Shaykhul-Islaam Muhammad bin Abdul Wahhaab is of course the author of this work: 'Kitaab at-Tawheed'... By way of whom, Allaah revived the 'aqeedah of Tawheed, Allaah revived the belief of Tawheed, in the region of Najd... in the 12th century after the Hijrah. He composed Kitaab at-Tawheed into sixty six chapters..."

**Source: Abu Khadeejah, Part 2**  
URL: https://abukhadeejah.com/kitab-at-tawhid-an-amazing-authorship-and-journey-into-the-history-of-the-saudi-link-with-muhammad-ibn-abdul-wahhab-part-2/

- His da'wah was first aided by the Ameer of 'Uyaynah, then by Muhammad ibn Sa'ud in Dir'iyyah.
- He destroyed the shrine over the grave of Zayd ibn al-Khattab, levelled graves, cut down a tree worshipped by women, and wrote letters/treatises.
- He wrote Kitaabut-Tawheed, Al-Usooluth-Thalaathah, Usoolus-Sittah, Kashfush-Shubuhaat, Qawaa'id al-Arba', Masa'il al-Jaahiliyyah, etc.

**Source: Abu Khadeejah, "The First Explanation of Kitaab at-Tahweed"**  
URL: https://abukhadeejah.com/the-first-explanation-of-kitaab-at-tahweed-by-shaikh-sulaimaan-b-abdullaah-aal-ash-shaikh/

- First explanation: *Tayseer al-'Azeez al-Hameed fee Sharhi Kitaab at-Tawheed* by Sulayman b. Abdullah b. Muhammad b. Abd al-Wahhab (d. 1233H), grandson of the author. He finished at the chapter on deniers of Qadar, leaving seven chapters incomplete.
- "Every Sharh that was compiled after Shaikh Sulaimaan b. Abdullaah benefitted from his explanation."

### Key quotes from Kitab at-Tawheed

**Source: AbdurRahman.org English PDF of Kitab at-Tawheed**  
URL: https://abdurrahman.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/kt-english.pdf

- Chapter 1 uses Qur'an 51:56, 16:36, 17:23-24, 4:36, 6:151-153.
- Ibn Mas'ud: "Whoever wishes to ascertain the very will of Prophet Muhammad... let him read the Statement of Allah: 'Say: Come, I will recite what your Lord has prohibited you from: Join not anything in worship with Him...'"
- Hadith of Mu'adh (Bukhari & Muslim): right of Allah / right of slaves.
- Chapter 66: "Do not let Satan get you carried away" / "I am Muhammad, the slave of Allah and His Messenger."
- Chapter 67: Qur'an 39:67, hadiths about Allah's Hand / Throne / Kursi / heavens.

**Source: Abu Khadeejah Part 1 (transcript of author's introduction)**

- "Tawheed is a principle of Islaam, upon which Islaam is built, it is the condition of its acceptance, by way of it, the good deeds are accepted and the evil deeds are forgiven, by it the servant enters into Jannah, and he is saved from the Hellfire. Due to it, the Messengers of God and the polytheists fell into disputes, and due to it, the swords of Jihaad clashed and due to it both Hellfire and Paradise were created."
- "The opposite of at Tawheed is Shirk (polytheism), i.e. the opposite of worshipping Allaah alone is shirk, which is to set up rivals unto Allaah in worship. Shirk nullifies the good deeds..."

### Tawheed categories / methodology

**Source: Salafi Publications, "A Primer on Tawheed" (TAW010002)**  
URL: http://spubs.com/SPS/sp.cfm?subsecID=TAW01&articleID=TAW010002&pfriend=

- "As for Tawheed, then know... that our Salaf have divided it into three categories; each one dependant on the other... Tawheed ur-Ruboobiyyah, Tawheed ul-Uloohiyyah, Tawheed ul-Asmaa was-Sifaat"
- Ruboobiyyah = creating, owning, regulating/administering creation; evidence Yunus 10:31, Mu'minoon 23:84-89.
- Uluhiyyah = unification of all acts of servants for Allah alone; evidence Ghaafir 40:60.
- Asmaa wa Sifaat = firm belief in everything in the Book and authentic Sunnah; without Takyeef, Tamtheel, Ta'weel, Tahreef, Ta'teel; evidence Ikhlaas 112, A'raaf 7:180, Shoora 42:11.

**Source: AbdurRahman.org, "The Characteristics of the Saved Group" — Ibn Uthaymeen**  
URL: https://abdurrahman.org/2015/01/09/most-distinguished-qualities-of-the-saved-group-shaykh-ibn-uthaymeen/

- "With respect to 'Aqeedah, you find it holding on to that which is proven by the Qur'aan and Sunnah from pure Tawheed pertaining to the Ruboobiyyah, Uloohiyyah, and al-Asmaa' was-Sifaat (Names and Attributes)."

**Source: AbdurRahman.org, "Tawheed" category — Ibn Baz on the foundations of Islam**

- "The religion of Islam is built upon two foundations: The first: that none is worshipped except Allah alone. The second: that Allah is not worshipped except in the way legislated by Allah and His Messenger... And this is the meaning of the testimony: 'There is no god worthy of worship except Allah, and Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah.'"

**Source: darpdfs.org, "An Explanation of the Six Principles" by Salih al-Fawzan**  
URL: https://darpdfs.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/An-Explanation-Of-The-Six-Principles-Sh.-Salih-al-Fawzan.pdf

- "The greatest issue that Allah and His Messenger clarified in this Qur'an is the issue of tawhid and shirk, because tawhid is the foundation of Islam and the religion. It is what all deeds are based upon, whereas shirk invalidates and corrupts this foundation and renders it non-existent."

## 2. Al-Aqeedah al-Wasitiyyah

### Author

**Source: Salafi Publications, "Aqeedatul-Waasitiyyah - The Text" page 1**  
URL: http://spubs.com/SPS/sp.cfm?subsecID=AQD04&articleID=AQD040003&articlePages=1

- "Taqi ud-Din Abu-l-'Abbas Ahmad Ibn 'Abd al-Halim Ibn 'Abd as-Salam Ibn Taimiyah al-Harrani al-Hanbali, was born on Monday the 10th of Rabi' al-Awwal 661 H./22nd of January 1263 C.E. at Harran."
- Died in Damascus jail, 20 Dhu-l-Qa'dah 728 H./26-27 September 1328 C.E.

### Reason for writing

**Source: Salafi Publications, Wasitiyyah page 2**  
URL: http://spubs.com/SPS/sp.cfm?subsecID=AQD04&articleID=AQD040003&articlePages=2

- Ibn Taimiyah said: "A Shafi'ite judge from Wasit (in Iraq) whose name is Radiy ad-Din al-Wasiti, visited me on his way to Hajj... He complained to me of the people's situation in that country... under the Tatars (Mongols) rule of ignorance, injustice, and loss of faith and knowledge. He asked me to write him an 'Aqidah... I declined saying: Many creeds have been written. Refer to the scholars of the Sunnah. However, he persisted... So I wrote this one for him while I was sitting one afternoon."

### Text and structure

**Source: Salafi Publications, Wasitiyyah page 3**  
URL: http://spubs.com/SPS/sp.cfm?subsecID=AQD04&articleID=AQD040003&articlePages=3

- Opening: "This is the belief of the saved group, the victorious ones to the Day of Judgment, the people of the Sunnah and the Jama'ah (i.e., The belief in Allah, His angels, His books, His messengers, the resurrection after death, the belief in Divine Decree (qadar), be it good or bad)."
- "Part of the belief in Allah is the belief in how He has described Himself in His Book (the Qur'an) and in how His Messenger Muhammad (peace be upon him) has described Him. Believe without distorting or denying and without questioning or shaping..."
- Sections: Allah's Names and Attributes in the Qur'an; Attributes in the Sunnah; Belief in the Hereafter; Divine Decree; Iman; Companions/family/wives; Karamat al-Awliya; Path of Ahl al-Sunnah.

**Source: Salafi Publications, Wasitiyyah page 5/7**  
URL: http://spubs.com/SPS/sp.cfm?subsecID=AQD04&articleID=AQD040003&articlePages=7

- Attributes in the Qur'an: Life, Knowledge, Might, Hearing, Seeing, Will, Love, Mercy, Pleasure, Anger, Coming, Face, Hand, Eyes, Forgiveness, Oneness, Istiwa, Highness, Ma'iyyah, Speech.
- Attributes in the Sunnah: Allah descends, laughs, marvels, places Foot over Hell, talks directly, Throne above water, "Where is Allah?" etc.

### Methodology quote

**Source: Salafi Publications, "How to Believe in the Attributes of Allaah" (Sharh Aqeedat ul-Waasitiyyah by Salih al-Fawzan)**  
URL: http://spubs.com/SPS/sp.cfm?articleID=AQD030009&articlePages=1&subsecID=AQD07

- "And it is from eemaan in Allaah to have eemaan in what Allaah has described Himself with in His Book and in what His Messenger (sallallaahu alayhi wasallam) has described Him with, without changing the original meaning [of these texts] (Tahreef), without denying such attributes for Allaah (Ta'teel), without enquiring into their true nature (Takyeef) and without likening [His attributes] to those of the creation (Tamtheel)."

### Scholarly praise

**Source: Salafi Dawah Manchester, "Two Stories..."**  
URL: https://salafidawahmanchester.com/2021/08/26/two-stories-one-about-al-aqeedah-al-waasitiyyah-and-the-other-about-kitaab-at-tawheed/

- "Shaikhul Islaam Ibn Taymiyyah's book Al-Aqeedah Al-Waasitiyyah is a precious book in its subject matter; rather it is one of the best and one of the most precious summaries put together on the Aqeedah of Ahlus Sunnah Wal Jamaa'ah."
- Ibn Taymiyyah: "I give you three years to read this book and bring out from it a single letter that is not from the speech of the pious predecessors... if one brings a single letter from anyone amongst the first three generations, I will recant."
- Al-Dhahabi: "Then there was an agreement – after the gathering – that Al-Aqeedah Al-Waasitiyyah is a book of Salafi Creed and a good book."
- Ibn Rajab: "There was agreement that the book is a Sunni Salafi Aqeedah book."

**Source: AbdurRahman.org, Ibn Baz on Ma'iyyah / Wasitiyyah**

- "Ahl-ul-Sunnah wal-Jama`ah believe in the Names and Attributes of Allah and understand their meanings but do not know their manner. Only Allah knows the exact manner of assuming His Attributes..."

### Hadith references from Wasitiyyah text

- "Our Lord descends during the last third of each night..." — agreed upon (Bukhari & Muslim).
- "Allah is happier with the repentance of his 'abd..." — agreed upon.
- "Allah laughs about two men who kill each other but both enter paradise" — agreed upon.
- "On the Day of Judgment... the Lord Almighty places His Foot (over it)" — agreed upon.
- "Allah will talk to each person directly without a mediator" — agreed upon.
- "Where is Allah? ... In the heaven" — Muslim.
- "The Throne is above the water, and Allah is on the Throne" — good, Abu Dawud and others.

## 3. Notes on Salafi methodology

- Tawhid: Rububiyyah, Uluhiyyah, Asma wa Sifat.
- Qur'an/Sunnah as sources; understanding of the Salaf.
- Rejection of Tahreef, Ta'teel, Takyeef, Tamtheel, Ta'weel.
- Middle path between ta'teel (Jahmiyyah/Mu'tazilah) and tamtheel (Mushabbihah).
- Rejection of bid'ah and shirk.
