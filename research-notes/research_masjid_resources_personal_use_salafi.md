# Research: Islamic Ruling on Using Masjid Resources for Personal Islamic Learning

## Executive Summary

Based on research from Salafi-approved sources (IslamQA, IslamWeb, BinBaz.org.sa, FatwaIslam, and others), the general ruling is that **it is NOT permissible** to use masjid resources (paper, printer, ink, electricity, etc.) for personal Islamic learning courses that are not directly for the masjid's use, unless explicit permission is granted by the waqf administrators or the donor's conditions allow it. The principle is that waqf (endowment) must be used for the purpose it was endowed for, and using it for personal benefit without authorization constitutes consuming others' property unlawfully.

---

## Key Findings from Salafi Sources

### 1. General Principle: Waqf Must Be Used for the Purpose It Was Endowed For

**Source: IslamQA (Fatwa 141395)**

> "If the one who gives a waqf stipulates conditions governing the waqf, then the majority of scholars are of the view that reference must be made to the conditions stipulated by the donor, because the conditions mentioned by the donor of a waqf are what govern the ways in which it is to benefit people; these conditions carry weight so long as they are not contrary to sharee'ah."

**Shaykh Ibn 'Uthaymeen (may Allah have mercy on him) said:**
> "It is obligatory to act in accordance with the conditions stipulated by the donor of the waqf… i.e., what was stipulated in terms of description, restrictions or the absence thereof, users and so on. Reference should not be made to the opinion of the supervisor regarding that; rather reference should be made to what was stipulated by the one who set up the waqf, and his conditions should be adhered to, unless they are contrary to sharee'ah."

Source: https://islamqa.info/en/answers/141395

---

### 2. Taking Masjid Items for Personal Use is Prohibited

**Source: IslamQA (Fatwa 170589) - Ruling on taking the Mushaf out of the mosque**

**Shaykh Ibn 'Uthaymeen (may Allah have mercy on him) was asked:**
> "Is it permissible to take a Mushaf from the mosque then return it?"
>
> "It is not permissible to take a Mushaf from the mosque then return it, because the Mushafs that are found in mosques are awqaaf donated for the public benefit, for everyone who enters the mosque to benefit from. If someone takes them, then this implies that he is keeping them for himself and depriving others of it, which is haraam and is not permissible even if he replaces it with another Mushaf."

Source: https://islamqa.info/amp/en/answers/170589

---

### 3. Using Masjid Tools/Equipment for Personal Benefit

**Source: IslamWeb (Fatwa 32666) - حكم استخدام أدوات المسجد في غير ما أوقفت له**

> "فلا يجوز استخدام أدوات المسجد في غير ما تم الوقف له، إلا بإذن الواقف أو ما جرت به عادة البلد، لأن جريان العادة بذلك دون نهي الواقف منزل منزلة رضاه"

Translation: "It is not permissible to use the tools of the masjid for other than what they were endowed for, except with the permission of the waqf founder or according to the custom of the land, because the custom prevailing without the prohibition of the founder is regarded as his approval."

**Ibn al-Humaam said:**
> "وليس لمتولي المسجد أن يحمل سراج المسجد إلى بيته"

Translation: "And the masjid administrator is not permitted to carry the masjid lamp to his house."

Source: https://www.islamweb.net/ar/fatwa/32666

---

### 4. Using Masjid Electricity/Utilities

**Source: IslamWeb (Fatwa 503905) - أحكام استعمال كهرباء المسجد في غير أوقات الصلاة للتدريس**

> "ثم إن استعمال كهرباء المسجد في غير أوقات الصلاة، يرجع فيه إلى الجهة المسؤولة عن كهرباء المسجد... فإن كانت تسمح بذلك، فإن الأمر مباح، وإن كانت تلك الجهة لا تسمح باستعمال الكهرباء في غير أوقات الصلاة؛ فلا يجوز استعمالها للتدريس، فإن شرط الواقف يُعمل به إذا لم يكن فيه معصية الله تعالى."

Translation: "Using the masjid's electricity outside prayer times depends on the authority responsible for the masjid's electricity... If they permit it, then it is permissible. If that authority does not permit using electricity outside prayer times, then it is not permissible to use it for teaching, for the condition of the waqf founder is to be followed if it does not involve disobedience to Allah."

**From the Kuwaiti Islamic Fiqh Encyclopedia:**
> "شرط الواقف إذا كان غير مخالف للشرع، وليس فيه ضرر بالوقف، ولا بالمستحقين، فإنه يجب اتباعه، ولأن الواقف مالك، فله أن يجعل ماله حيث يشاء، ما لم يكن معصية."

Translation: "The condition of the waqf founder, if it is not contrary to Islamic law, and does not cause harm to the waqf or its beneficiaries, must be followed, because the founder is the owner and has the right to place his wealth wherever he wishes, as long as it is not disobedience."

Source: https://islamweb.net/ar/fatwa/503905

---

### 5. Donated Money Must Be Used for the Specified Purpose

**Source: IslamWeb (Fatwa 129509) - وجوب مراعاة مقصود المتصدق**

> "فالأموال التي توقف على جهة معينة كالمسجد، لا يجوز صرفها إلى غير هذه الجهة، وكذلك الحال في أموال الصدقات والتبرعات، فقد نص الفقهاء على وجوب مراعاة مقصود المتصدق"

Translation: "Funds endowed for a specific purpose such as the masjid cannot be spent on other than that purpose. The same applies to charity and donations - the jurists have stated the obligation of observing the donor's intention."

Source: https://www.islamweb.net/ar/fatwa/129509

---

### 6. Ibn Baz on Masjid Waqf Items

**Source: BinBaz.org.sa - حكم أخذ المصحف الموقوف خارج المسجد**

**Shaykh Ibn Baz (may Allah have mercy on him) said:**
> "لا يجوز أخذه؛ لأن مصاحف المسجد تبقى في المسجد ولا تؤخذ"

Translation: "It is not permissible to take it, because the masjid's Mushafs should remain in the masjid and not be taken."

> "الأقرب لا يجوز؛ لأن الواقف أراد بقاءها في المسجد حتى ينتفع بها المصلون والقارئون الذين يأتون المسجد لأجل ذلك"

Translation: "The closest view is that it is not permissible, because the waqf founder intended for them to remain in the masjid so that the worshippers and reciters who come to the masjid for that purpose may benefit from them."

Source: https://binbaz.org.sa/categories/fiqhi/148?page=4

### 9. Specific Ruling: Using Waqf Bus for Other Charitable Purposes

**Source: IslamQA (Fatwa 141395)**

> "You did well by not allowing them to transport the mosque students to Taraaweeh prayer in the bus belonging to the Qur'an memorisation school, because this is not one of the interests that will benefit the women's section. If the bus is a waqf that was set up as such by the donor, then in such cases reference must be made to the conditions he made when giving the waqf. If he gave it as a waqf specifically to the women's section, then it should not be used for any other purpose that is not connected to the women's Qur'an memorisation section, even if it is charitable work."

This directly applies to the scenario of using masjid resources for personal Islamic learning - even if it's for a good purpose, if the waqf was designated for a specific purpose, it cannot be used for other purposes.

Source: https://islamqa.info/en/answers/141395

### 10. Ibn Qudaamah on Masjid Purpose

**Source: IslamQA (Fatwa 96101)**

> "It is not permissible to plant a tree in the mosque. This was stated by Ahmad, who said: If the date palm was planted after it became a mosque, then this was planted unlawfully, and I would not like to eat from it. If the imam uproots it, that is permissible, because the mosque was not built for that purpose, rather it was built for the remembrance of Allaah, prayer and reading Qur'aan"

**Book:** Al-Mughni (5/370)

Source: https://islamqa.info/index.php/en/answers/96101

### 11. FatwaIslam on Taking Masjid Property

**Source: FatwaIslam.com (Fatwa 924)**

> "It is not permissible to take anything away from the area of the Masjid and add it to the mentioned house. This is because the fundamental principle regarding religious donations (Awqaf) is that they must remain the way that they were (when donated). Thus no one should take liberties concerning the original trust (Waqf) by converting it from that which is superior (i.e., a Masjid) to that which is inferior (i.e., a house)."

Source: https://www.fatwaislam.com/fis/index.cfm?ID=924&scn=fd

## Application to the Specific Scenario

### Scenario:
A person wants to print their own ilm course pages and Islamic learning materials using the masjid's printer and paper, which were donated for the masjid's use. The person is not currently a khatib or official of the masjid, but may become one in the future.

### Ruling:
Based on the evidence from Salafi sources:

1. **The printer, paper, and ink are waqf (endowment) for the masjid** - they were donated for the masjid's use and benefit.

2. **Using them for personal Islamic learning courses that are NOT directly for the masjid's use is NOT permissible** without explicit permission from the masjid administration or waqf supervisors.

3. **The principle established by the scholars:**
   - Shaykh Ibn 'Uthaymeen: Conditions of the waqf founder must be adhered to
   - Ibn Baz: Masjid Mushafs and items must remain in the masjid
   - IslamWeb: Masjid tools cannot be used for other than their endowed purpose without permission

4. **Even if the purpose is good (Islamic learning), this does not make it permissible** if the resources were endowed specifically for the masjid's use. The fatwa about the waqf bus explicitly states that even charitable work does not justify using waqf resources for purposes other than what they were endowed for.

5. **"Maybe becoming a khatib in the future" does not change the ruling now** - the resources are currently waqf for the masjid, and a hypothetical future role does not authorize present personal use.

6. **This falls under the prohibition of consuming others' property unlawfully (An-Nisa 4:29)** because the masjid resources belong to the waqf/beneficiaries, not to individuals.

### Exception:
If the masjid administration or waqf supervisors explicitly permit the use of the printer, paper, and ink for personal Islamic learning, then it becomes permissible. As stated in the IslamWeb fatwa: "إلا بإذن الواقف أو ما جرت به عادة البلد" (except with the permission of the waqf founder or according to the custom of the land).
