## 1. Introduction: The Importance of Jumuah

Friday (Jumuah) is the most virtuous day of the week. Allah ﷻ made it a weekly Eid for the Muslims, and He singled it out with a prayer that replaces the Dhuhr prayer — a prayer consisting of **two rak'ahs** (not four), preceded by **two khutbahs** (sermons). The Jumuah prayer is one of the most important acts of worship in Islam, and Allah ﷻ specifically commanded it in the Quran:

> **يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا إِذَا نُودِيَ لِلصَّلَاةِ مِن يَوْمِ الْجُمُعَةِ فَاسْعَوْا إِلَى ذِكْرِ اللَّهِ وَذَرُوا الْبَيْعَ ذَلِكُمْ خَيْرٌ لَّكُمْ إِن كُنتُمْ تَعْلَمُونَ**
>
> *"O you who believe! When the call is proclaimed for the prayer on the day of Friday (Jumu'ah), come to the remembrance of Allah and leave off business. That is better for you if you did but know!"*
> — (Quran, Surah Al-Jumu'ah 62:9)

The "remembrance of Allah" (dhikr Allah) in this verse refers to the **khutbah**, as stated by Sa'id ibn al-Musayyab, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, and others among the Salaf (Islamweb, [source](https://islamweb.net/en/fatwa/87967/the-khutba-and-friday-prayer)).

## 2. The Conditions for the Validity of Jumuah Prayer

For the Jumuah prayer to be valid, several conditions must be met. The scholars have discussed these in detail:

### 2.1 The Khutbah Is a Condition for the Validity of the Prayer

**Shaykh Ibn Baz** (may Allah have mercy on him) stated:

> "خطبة الجمعة شرط من شروط الصلاة؛ لأن الرسول ﷺ حافظ عليها، كان يخطب في الجمعة، ولم يتركها مرة واحدة، فهي شرط من شروط صحة صلاة الجمعة أن يخطب خطبتين؛ لقول النبي ﷺ: **صلوا كما رأيتموني أصلي** وكان يخطب خطبتين -عليه الصلاة والسلام- نعم."
>
> *"The Jumuah khutbah is a condition for the prayer; because the Messenger ﷺ maintained it — he used to deliver the khutbah on Jumuah and never abandoned it even once. It is a condition for the validity of the Jumuah prayer that two khutbahs be delivered, due to the saying of the Prophet ﷺ: 'Pray as you have seen me pray,' and he used to deliver two khutbahs — peace and blessings of Allah be upon him."*
> — Shaykh Ibn Baz, [binbaz.org.sa](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/9265/%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%85-%D8%AE%D8%B7%D8%A8%D8%AA%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A9)

### 2.2 The Minimum Number of Worshippers

There is a well-known difference of opinion among the scholars regarding the minimum number required for Jumuah to be valid. The Shafi'i and Hanbali schools require forty persons, based on the report from Jabir (may Allah be pleased with him): *"According to the Sunnah, the Friday prayer should be performed when there are forty or more people"* (Islamweb, [source](https://islamweb.net/en/fatwa/82027/minimum-number-of-people-for-jumu%E2%80%99ah-prayer)).

However, **Shaykh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah** (may Allah have mercy on him) chose the view that the minimum is **three**: one imam and two listeners:

> "Shaykhul-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah chose the view that the minimum number of worshipers required for the Friday prayer to be valid is three: one imaam and two listeners. Some scholars held the same opinion as well. Some scholars even held that the minimum number of worshipers required for the Friday prayer to be valid is two: the imaam and one listener."
> — Islamweb, [source](https://islamweb.net/en/fatwa/356992/friday-prayer-must-be-offered-in-congregation)

**Shaykh Ibn Baz** (may Allah have mercy on him) strongly supported this view:

> "الصواب: أنه لا يشترط لها أكثر من ثلاثة؛ لأنه ليس هناك دليل على اثني عشر، ولا على أكثر من اثني عشر، الجمعة تجب في الجماعة، والجماعة أقلها ثلاثة: الإمام، واثنان معه، هذا هو أصح ما قيل في ذلك."
>
> *"The correct view is that no more than three are required; because there is no evidence for twelve, or for more than twelve. Jumuah is obligatory in congregation, and the minimum of a congregation is three: the imam and two with him. This is the most correct view on this matter."*
> — Shaykh Ibn Baz, [binbaz.org.sa](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/13309/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B0%D9%8A-%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B9%D9%82%D8%AF-%D8%A8%D9%87-%D8%B5%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A9)

He further said:

> "أما قول من قال: إنه لا بد من اثني عشر، أو لا بد من أربعين، فهذه أقوال ليس عليها دليل، بل ما ورد في ذلك إما ضعيف، وإما صحيح ليس بدال على ما ادعاه المدعي، فإذا كان في البلد ثلاثة، أو أربعة، أو عشرة مستوطنون؛ فإنهم يقيمون الجمعة، ويصلون الجمعة وتجزئهم على الصحيح."
>
> *"As for the saying of those who say there must be twelve, or forty — these are positions with no evidence. Rather, what has been reported in that regard is either weak, or authentic but not indicating what the claimant claims. So if there are three, four, or ten residents in a town, they establish Jumuah and pray it, and it is valid for them according to the correct view."*
> — Shaykh Ibn Baz, [binbaz.org.sa](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/13309/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B0%D9%8A-%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B9%D9%82%D8%AF-%D8%A8%D9%87-%D8%B5%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A9)

### 2.3 Other Conditions

Islamweb summarizes the prerequisites:

> "The prerequisites of the Jumu'ah prayer are of three kinds: 1. Conditions that are compulsory for the validity of the Jumu'ah prayer and which, if met, the Jumu'ah becomes obligatory. 2. Conditions which if present, the Jumu'ah prayer becomes obligatory. 3. Conditions that are compulsory for the validity of the Jumu'ah prayer."
>
> "There is no disagreement among the scholars that a congregation is a necessary condition for the validity of the Jumu'ah prayer."
> — Islamweb, [source](https://islamweb.net/en/fatwa/82835/conditions-of-the-jumu%E2%80%99ah-prayer)

The generally agreed-upon conditions include:
- Being a **resident** (not a traveler)
- Being in a **settlement/town** (not the open desert)
- **Congregation** (the number is disputed, as above)
- The **khutbah** (as discussed)
- The **time** of Jumuah (discussed below)

## 3. Who Is Obligated to Attend Jumuah?

Jumuah is obligatory upon **free, adult, resident Muslim men**. It is **not obligatory** upon women, travelers, slaves, the sick, or those with valid excuses — though if they attend and pray with the congregation, it counts for them.

### 3.1 Women

**Shaykh Ibn Baz** (may Allah have mercy on him) said:

> "المرأة ليس عليها جمعة، الجمعة من شأن الرجال، فتصلي ظهرًا أربع ركعات إذا كانت مقيمة، وركعتين إذا كانت مسافرة، وليس عليها جمعة، إنما الجمعة في حق الرجال فقط، لكن لو حضرت مع الرجال، وصلت الجمعة معهم أجزأتها، كما كان بعض النساء يصلين مع النبي ﷺ."
>
> *"A woman is not required to attend Jumuah — Jumuah is the affair of men. She prays Dhuhr as four rak'ahs if she is resident, and two rak'ahs if she is traveling. Jumuah is not upon her; it is only for men. But if she attends with the men and prays Jumuah with them, it suffices her, just as some women used to pray with the Prophet ﷺ."*
> — Shaykh Ibn Baz, [binbaz.org.sa](https://binbaz.org.sa/categories/fiqhi/54/fatwa?page=14)

### 3.2 Travelers

**Shaykh Ibn Baz** (may Allah have mercy on him) said:

> "المسافر لا تلزمه الجمعة، لكن إذا حضرها أجزأته، إذا مر ببلد، وصلى معهم أجزأته، وإلا فلا تلزمه، وقد صلى النبي ﷺ في حجة الوداع يوم الجمعة صلاة الظهر يوم عرفة، ولم يصل جمعة؛ لأنه مسافر، صلاها ظهرًا."
>
> *"A traveler is not obligated to attend Jumuah, but if he attends it, it suffices him. If he passes through a town and prays with them, it suffices him; otherwise it is not obligatory upon him. The Prophet ﷺ prayed Dhuhr on the day of Arafah during the Farewell Pilgrimage on a Friday, and he did not pray Jumuah — because he was a traveler; he prayed it as Dhuhr."*
> — Shaykh Ibn Baz, [binbaz.org.sa](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/15606/%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%85-%D8%B5%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B3%D8%A7%D9%81%D8%B1)

### 3.3 Those with Valid Excuses

**Shaykh Ibn Baz** (may Allah have mercy on him) also stated:

> "Whoever did not attend the Friday prayer with the Muslims due to some lawful reason, such as illness or the like, or some other reasons, should pray the Zuhr prayer. Likewise, a woman prays the Zuhr prayer and so does the traveller and those living in the desert, as is proved by the Sunnah, and it is the opinion of the majority of the scholars."
> — Shaykh Ibn Baz, [fatwaislam.com](https://www.fatwaislam.com/fis/index.cfm?scn=fd&ID=1188)

## 4. The Time of Jumuah Prayer

**Shaykh Ibn Baz** (may Allah have mercy on him) discussed the time of Jumuah:

> "وقت صلاة الجمعة: تجوز صلاة الجمعة قبل زوال الشمس، ولكن الأفضل بعد الزوال خروجًا من خلاف العلماء؛ لأن أكثر العلماء يقولون: لا بد أن تكون صلاة الجمعة بعد الزوال، وهذا هو قول الأكثرين، وذهب قوم من أهل العلم إلى جوازها قبل الزوال في الساعة السادسة وفيه أحاديث وآثار تدل."
>
> *"The time of Jumuah prayer: it is permissible to pray Jumuah before the sun's zenith (zawal), but it is better to do so after zawal — to avoid the difference of opinion among the scholars; because the majority of scholars say that Jumuah must be after zawal. This is the view of the majority. Some people of knowledge held that it is permissible before zawal, in the sixth hour [i.e., the last portion of the morning], and there are hadiths and reports that indicate this."*
> — Shaykh Ibn Baz, [binbaz.org.sa](https://binbaz.org.sa/majmou-fatawa/12?page=16)

The **strongest view** is that Jumuah is prayed at the time of Dhuhr — after the sun passes its zenith (zawal) — and this is the practice of the Muslims throughout the ages.

## 5. The Adhan and Iqamah for Jumuah

### 5.1 The Original Sunnah: One Adhan

**Shaykh Ibn Baz** (may Allah have mercy on him) explained:

> "نعم هو كما قال السائل، كان الأمر في عهد النبي ﷺ أذان واحد مع الإقامة، كان إذا دخل النبي ﷺ للخطبة والصلاة أذن المؤذن، ثم خطب النبي ﷺ الخطبتين، ثم يقام للصلاة، هذا هو الأمر المعلوم، وهو الذي جاءت به السنة، كما قال السائل، وهو أمر معروف عند أهل العلم والإيمان."
>
> *"Yes, it is as the questioner said. The practice during the time of the Prophet ﷺ was a single adhan along with the iqamah. When the Prophet ﷺ entered for the khutbah and prayer, the mu'adhdhin would call the adhan, then the Prophet ﷺ would deliver the two khutbahs, then the iqamah would be called for the prayer. This is the well-known practice, and it is what the Sunnah brought, as the questioner said, and it is well-known among the people of knowledge and faith."*
> — Shaykh Ibn Baz, [binbaz.org.sa](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/14005/%D9%85%D8%A7-%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B0%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D9%86-%D9%84%D8%B5%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A9)

### 5.2 The Addition of the First Adhan by Uthman (may Allah be pleased with him)

> "ثم إن الناس كثروا في عهد الخليفة الراشد عثمان بن عفان -رضي الله تعالى عنه- في المدينة، فرأى أن يزاد الأذان الثالث، ويقال له: الأذان الأول؛ لأجل تنبيه الناس على أن اليوم يوم الجمعة، حتى يستعدوا ويبادروا إلى الصلاة قبل الأذان المعتاد المعروف بعد الزوال، وتابعه بهذا الصحابة في عهده."
>
> *"Then the people became numerous during the time of the Rightly-Guided Caliph Uthman ibn Affan (may Allah be pleased with him) in Madinah, so he saw fit to add a third adhan, called the 'first adhan,' to alert the people that it is Friday, so they would prepare and hasten to the prayer before the usual adhan known after zawal. The Companions followed him in this during his time."*
> — Shaykh Ibn Baz, [binbaz.org.sa](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/14005/%D9%85%D8%A7-%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B0%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D9%86-%D9%84%D8%B5%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A9)

### 5.3 Ibn Taymiyyah's Account

**Shaykh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah** (may Allah have mercy on him) narrated:

> "أنَّه كان يصلِّي الجمعةَ بأذانٍ وإقامةٍ لا يؤذِّنُ لها إلَّا إذا قعد على المنبرِ، وكذلك كان الأمرُ على عهدِ أبي بكرٍ وعمرَ، فلمَّا كان في أثناءِ خلافةِ عثمانَ كثُرَ النَّاسُ، فأمرَ بالنِّداءِ الثَّالثِ على دارٍ قريبةٍ من المسجدِ من جهةِ المشرقِ يقالُ لها الزَّوراءُ"
>
> *"He [the Prophet ﷺ] used to pray Jumuah with one adhan and one iqamah — the adhan was not called except when he sat upon the minbar. And this was the practice during the time of Abu Bakr and Umar. Then, during the caliphate of Uthman, the people became numerous, so he ordered a third call to be made from a house near the mosque on the eastern side, called az-Zawra'."*
> — Ibn Taymiyyah, *Al-Jawab as-Sahih* (6/368), via [dorar.net](https://dorar.net/h/sKZGtLi2)

> **Note:** This report is **mutawatir** (mass-transmitted) and authentically established.

**Summary of the adhan:**
- In the time of the Prophet ﷺ, Abu Bakr, and Umar (may Allah be pleased with them): **one adhan** when the imam sat on the minbar, then the iqamah after the khutbah.
- In the time of Uthman (may Allah be pleased with him): a **first adhan** was added outside the mosque to alert people, due to the growing population. This was approved by the Companions and became the established practice.

## 6. The Khutbah: Its Ruling, Pillars, and Conditions

### 6.1 The Khutbah Is a Condition for the Validity of Jumuah

As mentioned above, **Shaykh Ibn Baz** (may Allah have mercy on him) stated clearly that the khutbah is a condition (*shart*) for the validity of the Jumuah prayer, because the Prophet ﷺ never abandoned it. The Prophet ﷺ said: **"Pray as you have seen me pray"** (Sahih al-Bukhari), and he always delivered two khutbahs before the Jumuah prayer.

### 6.2 The Obligations (Wajibat) of the Khutbah

**Shaykh Ibn Baz** (may Allah have mercy on him) listed what the khutbah should contain:

> "أن يكون فيها حمدُ الله، والثناء على الله، والشهادتان، ووعظ الناس وتذكيرهم، وبعضهم شرط فيها الصلاة على النبي ﷺ، وبعضهم شرط قراءة آيةٍ، فينبغي أن يفعل هذا، ينبغي أن يحمد الله ويُثني عليه، ويتشهد الشَّهادتين، ويُصلي على النبي ﷺ، ويعظ الناس ويُذكرهم."
>
> *"It should contain: praise of Allah, extolling Allah, the two testimonies of faith (shahadatayn), admonishing the people and reminding them. Some made sending salat upon the Prophet ﷺ a condition, and some made reciting a verse a condition. So one should do all of this: praise Allah and extol Him, testify the two shahadahs, send salat upon the Prophet ﷺ, and admonish and remind the people."*
> — Shaykh Ibn Baz, [binbaz.org.sa](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/31480/%D9%85%D8%A7-%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B7-%D8%AE%D8%B7%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A9)

### 6.3 The Purpose of the Khutbah: Admonition and Reminder

**Shaykh Ibn Baz** (may Allah have mercy on him) further clarified:

> "والمقصود: وعظ الناس، وتذكيرهم، وتعليمهم، وتوجيههم إلى الخير، وتحذيرهم من الشرِّ، وإنْ قرأ آيةً في أثناء الخطبة أو آيات كان هذا مما ينبغي؛ لأنه كان يقرأ آيات في الخطبة عليه الصلاة والسلام، لكن اختلفوا: هل هذا شرط أم لا؟ فقومٌ جعلوه شرطًا، وقومٌ قالوا: ليس بشرطٍ، ولكن مشروع أن يقرأ بعض الآيات، فإذا قرأ آيةً أو آيتين أو أكثر؛ لأنَّ هذا متأكد خروجًا من خلاف مَن قال بعدم صحَّتها من دونها."
>
> *"The purpose is: admonishing the people, reminding them, teaching them, guiding them to good, and warning them from evil. If he recites a verse or verses during the khutbah, this is something that should be done, because he ﷺ used to recite verses in the khutbah. But they differed: is this a condition or not? Some made it a condition, and some said it is not a condition — but it is recommended (mashru') to recite some verses. So if he recites a verse or two or more, this is strongly recommended, to avoid the difference of opinion of those who say the khutbah is not valid without it."*
> — Shaykh Ibn Baz, [binbaz.org.sa](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/31480/%D9%85%D8%A7-%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B7-%D8%AE%D8%B7%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A9)

### 6.4 The Structure: Two Khutbahs with a Sitting Between Them

Islamweb reports the established Sunnah:

> "روى الجماعة عن جابر بن سمرة قال: كان رسول الله -صلى الله عليه وسلم- يخطب قائماً، ويجلس بين الخطبتين، ويقرأ آيات، ويذكر الناس."
>
> *"It is reported by the Group [the six books of hadith] from Jabir ibn Samurah who said: 'The Messenger of Allah ﷺ used to deliver the khutbah standing, sit between the two khutbahs, recite verses, and admonish the people.'"*
> — Islamweb, [source](https://islamweb.net/ar/fatwa/30374/%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AC%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%B7%D8%A8%D8%A9)

### 6.5 Summary of the Khutbah's Obligations and Sunan

Based on the scholars' statements, the khutbah should include:

| Element | Ruling | Evidence |
|---------|--------|----------|
| Praise of Allah (hamd) | Obligatory | Hadith of Abu Hurayrah (Abu Dawud) |
| Two testimonies of faith (shahadatayn) | Obligatory | Consensus |
| Sending salat upon the Prophet ﷺ | Recommended/obligatory according to some | Ibn Baz |
| Admonition and reminder (wa'dh wa tadhkir) | Obligatory — the purpose of the khutbah | Jabir ibn Samurah (the Six) |
| Reciting a verse of Quran | Recommended (to avoid difference of opinion) | Ibn Baz |
| Standing for the khutbah | Obligatory | Jabir ibn Samurah (the Six) |
| Sitting between the two khutbahs | Sunnah mu'akkadah | Jabir ibn Samurah (the Six) |
| Two khutbahs | Obligatory | Prophet's continuous practice |

## 7. How to Deliver the Khutbah: Structure and Etiquette

### 7.1 The Soul of the Khutbah: Admonition

**Shaykh al-Albani** (may Allah have mercy on him) explained the essence of the khutbah:

> "Know that the type of Khutbah which is legislated is that which the Messenger –sallAllaahu alayhi wa sallam– regularly used to be upon, which was persuading (with good rewards) the people [and] dissuading (warning) them — this in reality is the soul of the Khutbah for which it was legislated."
>
> "Admonition in the Khutbah of Jumma' is what should be the context of the talk. Therefore, if the Khateeb (the one delivering the Khutbah) does this, then he has performed a legislated action, except that if he precedes it with praising and glorifying Allaah, and sending Salat upon His Messenger, or to proceed in his admonition [with] the catastrophies mentioned in the Qur'aan then this would be better and more complete."
> — Shaykh al-Albani, [abdurrahman.org](https://abdurrahman.org/2015/05/22/the-purpose-of-the-jumma-khutbah-friday-sermon/)

### 7.2 Making the Khutbah Relevant to the Occasion

**Shaykh Ibn Uthaymeen** (may Allah have mercy on him) gave excellent advice on tailoring the khutbah:

> "هذا طيب يعني: كون الإنسان يجعل الخطبة مناسبة لما حدث، هذا طيب، وهذا هو الغالب على خطب النبي -صلى الله عليه وسلم- ، ولهذا إذا وقعت حادثة تحتاج إلى خطبة ، قام وخطب حتى بغير جمعة. كون الإنسان يراعي الأحوال ويخطب في المناسبات هذا طيب؛ مثلاً في رمضان يتحدث عن الصيام، وفي الحج يتحدث عن الحج، وفي ربيع الأول عن الهجرة، يعني: ينظر المناسبات، هذا لا بأس به، وهو دليل على أن الخطيب فقيه وحكيم."
>
> *"This is good — that a person makes the khutbah relevant to what has occurred. This is good, and this was the general pattern of the Prophet's khutbahs ﷺ. For this reason, if an incident occurred that required a khutbah, he would stand and deliver it even outside of Jumuah. That a person considers the circumstances and delivers khutbahs on appropriate occasions is good — for example, in Ramadan he speaks about fasting, during Hajj he speaks about Hajj, in Rabi' al-Awwal about the Hijrah — he considers the occasions. There is no problem with this, and it is evidence that the khatib is a faqih (understanding) and hakim (wise)."*
> — Shaykh Ibn Uthaymeen, [troid.org](https://www.troid.org/reciting-verses-relevant-to-the-khutbah-in-salat-al-jumuah/)

### 7.3 The Khutbah Should Be Short

The scholars agreed that shortening the khutbah is recommended. **Dorar.net** reports:

> "يُسنُّ تَقصيرُ الخُطبةِ قال النوويُّ: (يكون قصرها معتدلًا، ولا يُبالغ بحيث يَمحَقُها) ((المجموع)) (4/529). وقال القرافيُّ: (اتَّفق الجميعُ على استحسانِ قَصْر الخطبة) ((الذخيرة)) (2/345)."
>
> *"It is Sunnah to shorten the khutbah. An-Nawawi said: 'Its shortening should be moderate, not exaggerated to the point of obliterating it.' (Al-Majmu', 4/529). Al-Qarafi said: 'All agreed upon the recommendation of shortening the khutbah.' (Adh-Dhakhirah, 2/345)."*
> — Dorar.net, [source](https://dorar.net/feqhia/1639/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B7%D9%84%D8%A8-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%A8%D8%B9-%D9%85%D8%A7-%D9%8A%D8%B3%D9%86-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%B7%D8%A8%D8%A9)

The Prophet ﷺ said: *"The lengthening of the prayer by a man and the shortness of his khutbah is a sign of his understanding (fiqh). So lengthen the prayer and shorten the khutbah."* (Sahih Muslim, Hadith 869)

### 7.4 Raising the Hands During the Khutbah

**Shaykh Ibn Baz** (may Allah have mercy on him) clarified an important point:

> "رفع اليدين غير مشروع في خطبة الجمعة ولا في خطبة العيد لا للإمام ولا للمأمومين، وإنما المشروع الإنصات للخطيب والتأمين على دعائه بينه وبين نفسه من دون رفع صوت، وأما رفع اليدين فلا يشرع؛ لأن النبي ﷺ لم يكن يرفع يديه في خطبة الجمعة ولا في خطبة الأعياد."
>
> *"Raising the hands is not legislated in the Jumuah khutbah or the Eid khutbah — neither for the imam nor for the worshippers. Rather, what is legislated is listening attentively to the khatib and saying Amin to his supplication silently, without raising the voice. As for raising the hands, it is not legislated, because the Prophet ﷺ did not used to raise his hands in the Jumuah khutbah or the Eid khutbahs."*
> — Shaykh Ibn Baz, [binbaz.org.sa](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/5112/%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%85-%D8%B1%D9%81%D8%B9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%A7%D8%AB%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%A8%D8%AE%D8%B7%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A9)

> **Exception:** The only exception is when the khatib **seeks rain (istisqa')** during the khutbah, in which case the Prophet ﷺ raised his hands. This is specific to the rain-seeking supplication.

### 7.5 Practical Structure of the Two Khutbahs

Based on the scholars' guidance and the Prophet's practice, the structure is:

**First Khutbah:**
1. Praise Allah and extol Him (al-hamd wa al-thana')
2. Testify the two shahadahs (ashhadu an la ilaha illa Allah, ashhadu anna Muhammadan rasulullah)
3. Send salat upon the Prophet ﷺ (sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam)
4. Recite a verse or verses from the Quran
5. Admonish and remind the people (wa'dh wa tadhkir) — this is the main body
6. Conclude with a supplication for forgiveness

**Sitting between the two khutbahs:** A brief, moderate pause.

**Second Khutbah:**
1. Praise Allah and extol Him
2. Testify the two shahadahs
3. Send salat upon the Prophet ﷺ
4. Continue admonition (shorter than the first)
5. Make du'a for the Muslims

## 8. Listening to the Khutbah: Rulings on Talking and Arriving Late

### 8.1 The Obligation of Silence

**Shaykh Ibn Baz** (may Allah have mercy on him) gave a comprehensive ruling:

> "لا يجوز الكلام والإمام يخطب مع الناس، لا مع العاطس ولا مع غيره، الواجب الإنصات لسماع الخطيب، إلا مع الخطيب لا بأس، إذا أراد أن يسأل الخطيب عن شيء، أو ينكر عليه شيئاً أخطأ فيه يجب إنكاره، فالكلام مع الخطيب لا بأس، والخطيب لا بأس يتكلم هو؛ لأن هذا من جنس خطبته، أما الناس فيما بينهم فلا."
>
> *"It is not permissible to speak while the imam is delivering the khutbah — not to someone who sneezes nor to anyone else. The obligation is to listen attentively to the khatib, except speaking with the khatib himself — there is no harm in that, if one wants to ask the khatib about something, or object to an error he made — objecting is obligatory. Speaking with the khatib is fine, and the khatib speaking is fine because it is part of his khutbah. But the people speaking among themselves — no."*
> — Shaykh Ibn Baz, [binbaz.org.sa](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/5415/%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D8%AB%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%AE%D8%B7%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A9)

### 8.2 Even Saying "Yarhamukallah" to a Sneezer Is Prohibited

**Shaykh Ibn Baz** (may Allah have mercy on him) continued:

> "حتى ولو عطس وحمد الله، لا تقول: يرحمك الله، كما لا تقوله في الصلاة، إذا عطس وهو يصلي وحمد الله ما يقال: يرحمك الله، هكذا في الخطبة المستمع لها كالمصلي فلا يتكلم، ولا يرد السلام، ولا يبدأ بالسلام، ولا يشمت العاطس، هذا هو الواجب عليه؛ لقول النبي ﷺ: **إذا قلت لصاحبك: (أنصت) يوم الجمعة والإمام يخطب فقد لغوت** مع أنه أمره بالمعروف، سماه لاغياً وهو آمر بالمعروف."
>
> *"Even if someone sneezes and praises Allah, do not say 'Yarhamukallah' to him — just as you would not say it in the prayer. If someone sneezes while praying and praises Allah, 'Yarhamukallah' is not said. Likewise in the khutbah — the listener is like the one praying: he does not speak, does not return salam, does not initiate salam, and does not respond to a sneezer. This is what is obligatory upon him, due to the saying of the Prophet ﷺ: 'If you say to your companion: "Be quiet" on Friday while the imam is delivering the khutbah, you have engaged in idle talk (laghw)' — even though he was enjoining good, he called him laghw (idle) while he was enjoining good."*
> — Shaykh Ibn Baz, [binbaz.org.sa](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/5415/%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D8%AB%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%AE%D8%B7%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A9)

### 8.3 The Severity of Talking During the Khutbah

**Shaykh Ibn Baz** (may Allah have mercy on him) added:

> "وفي الحديث الآخر: **من تكلم يوم الجمعة والإمام يخطب فهو كمثل الحمار يحمل أسفاراً، والذي يقول له: أنصت، ليست له جمعة**، هذا يدل على أن الجمعة تلغو ويفوته ثوابها بسبب كلامه حال الخطبة، ولو أن كلامه في أمر طيب، في أمر بمعروف، أو نهي عن منكر، أو تشميت عاطس، فالواجب ترك ذلك."
>
> *"And in another hadith: 'Whoever speaks on Friday while the imam is delivering the khutbah is like a donkey carrying books, and the one who says to him: "Be quiet" — his Jumuah is invalid.' This indicates that the Jumuah is voided and he misses its reward due to his speaking during the khutbah — even if his speech is about something good, enjoining good, forbidding evil, or responding to a sneezer. The obligation is to leave all of that."*
> — Shaykh Ibn Baz, [binbaz.org.sa](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/5415/%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D8%AB%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%AE%D8%B7%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A9)

> **Hadith references:** The hadith "If you say to your companion 'be quiet'..." is reported in Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim. The hadith "Whoever speaks... is like a donkey carrying books" is reported by Ahmad (9637); graded **da'if** by some but its meaning is supported by the authentic hadiths.

### 8.4 Arriving Late

Islamweb clarifies an important ruling:

> "Whoever misses the Khutba, even deliberately, but arrived on time for the prayer, then his prayer is valid, even if he joins the prayer while in the second Rak'ah; he will make up for the missed Rak'ah and his prayer is complete."
>
> "The Prophet (Sallallahu Alaihi wa Sallam) said: 'Whoever joins the prayer in the last Rak'ah of the Jumu'ah prayer or other prayers, his prayer is indeed complete.' Reported by Nasa'i."
>
> "However, if someone joins the prayer after the last Ruku he is considered as having missed Jumu'ah so, he has to pray 4 Rakahs as Dhuhr."
> — Islamweb, [source](https://islamweb.net/en/fatwa/87967/the-khutba-and-friday-prayer)

**Shaykh Ibn Baz** (may Allah have mercy on him) explained the rule for catching Jumuah:

> "عليكم أن تصلوا ظهرًا؛ لأن الجمعة ما تدرك إلا بركعة، فإذا جاء المسبوق، وقد فاتت الركعتان؛ يصلي ظهرًا، ينويها ظهرًا أربعًا، والذي يقتدي به كذلك ظهرًا؛ لأن الجمعة إنما تدرك بركعة واحدة يقول النبي ﷺ: **من أدرك ركعة من الصلاة؛ فقد أدرك الصلاة** نعم."
>
> *"You must pray Dhuhr, because Jumuah is only caught by one rak'ah. If the latecomer comes and both rak'ahs have passed, he prays Dhuhr — intending it as Dhuhr, four rak'ahs — and whoever follows him also prays Dhuhr. Because Jumuah is only caught by one rak'ah, as the Prophet ﷺ said: 'Whoever catches one rak'ah of the prayer has caught the prayer.' Yes."*
> — Shaykh Ibn Baz, [binbaz.org.sa](https://binbaz.org.sa/categories/fiqhi/54/fatwa?page=17)

## 9. Who Is Qualified to Give the Khutbah and Lead Jumuah?

### 9.1 The Conditions for the Khatib/Imam

**Shaykh Ibn Baz** (may Allah have mercy on him) stated:

> "كونه مسلماً، يجيد القراءة هذا هو المشروط فيه، أما كونه أعلم الناس، أو كونه عالمًا، أو كونه سنه كذا لا يشترط، لكن إذا تيسر أنه ذو بصيرة، وأن قراءته حسنة، ويجيد الخطبة هذا هو المطلوب يؤم القوم أقرؤهم لكتاب الله."
>
> *"That he be a Muslim and good at recitation — this is what is required. As for being the most knowledgeable, or being a scholar, or being a certain age — these are not conditions. But if it is possible that he has insight (basirah), good recitation, and is skilled at delivering the khutbah — this is what is desired. The one who leads the people should be the best reciter of the Book of Allah."*
> — Shaykh Ibn Baz, [binbaz.org.sa](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/15548/%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B7-%D8%AE%D8%B7%D9%8A%D8%A8-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B5%D9%84%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AA)

He further clarified:

> "هذا هو الأفضل، أن يكون أقرأ من غيره، يعني ذا قراءة جيدة، وذا علم وبصيرة، يستطيع الخطبة، سواءٌ كان كبير السن، أو شابًا، لكن يكون قد بلغ الحلم خروجًا من خلاف من قال: لا يؤم المتنفل المفترض، فإذا كان قد بلغ الحلم مكلفًا، كان أحوط؛ خروجًا من الخلاف، ويكون ممن يجيد القراءة، ويجيد الصلاة، يحسن الصلاة، ويجيد الخطبة إذا كان في الجمعة، وفي الأعياد ممن يجيد الخطبة، ولا يلحن في الفاتحة، بل يجيد قراءة الفاتحة."
>
> *"This is better — that he be a better reciter than others, meaning one with good recitation, knowledge, and insight, who is able to deliver the khutbah — whether he is elderly or young. But he should have reached puberty, to avoid the difference of opinion of those who say: 'A non-obligated person should not lead an obligated person.' If he has reached puberty and is accountable, this is more cautious — to avoid the difference of opinion. And he should be skilled at recitation, skilled at prayer, good at prayer, and skilled at the khutbah if it is Jumuah or Eid — skilled at the khutbah, not making grammatical errors in al-Fatihah, but skilled at reciting al-Fatihah."*
> — Shaykh Ibn Baz, [binbaz.org.sa](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/15548/%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B7-%D8%AE%D8%B7%D9%8A%D8%A8-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B5%D9%84%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AA)

### 9.2 The Khatib Should Have Knowledge or Read from a Trusted Book

**Shaykh Ibn Baz** (may Allah have mercy on him) said:

> "شروط الخطيب أن يكون ذا علم، ذا بصيرة، أو يخطب من كتاب مأمون، قد وضعه أهل العلم والبصيرة، فيخطب منه على الناس، إذا كان صوته يسمع الناس، أو من طريق المكبر وكان عدلًا، أما إذا كان معروفًا بالمعاصي؛ فينبغي ألا يولى الخطابة، ينبغي أن يولى أهل العدل، وأهل الخير والفضل."
>
> *"The conditions for the khatib are that he should have knowledge and insight, or he delivers the khutbah from a trusted book that the people of knowledge and insight have prepared — so he delivers it to the people. If his voice reaches the people, or through a microphone, and he is upright ('adl). As for one known for sins — he should not be appointed for the khutbah. The people of uprightness, goodness, and virtue should be appointed."*
> — Shaykh Ibn Baz, [binbaz.org.sa](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/10659/%D9%85%D8%A7-%D9%8A%D8%B4%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%B7-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%B7%D9%8A%D8%A8)

### 9.3 If a Sinful Person Leads, the Prayer Is Still Valid

**Shaykh Ibn Baz** (may Allah have mercy on him) added:

> "فالمقصود: أن الخطيب إذا كان مسلمًا؛ صحت صلاته، وصحة خطبته؛ إذا حصل المقصود بها، من وعظ الناس، وتذكيرهم من جهة نفسه لكونه عالمًا، أو من كتاب يحصل به المقصود مما ألف في خطب الجمعة؛ فلا حرج في ذلك، وينبغي للمسؤولين ألا يولوا إلا أهل الفضل والعدالة والاستقامة، لكن لو قدر أنه ذو معصية، وصلى بالناس؛ صحت صلاته على الصحيح، مادام مسلمًا، فالمعصية لا تبطل صلاته، ولا صلاة من خلفه، لا جمعة ولا جماعة."
>
> *"The point is: if the khatib is a Muslim, his prayer is valid and his khutbah is valid — if the purpose of the khutbah is achieved, which is admonishing and reminding the people, either from himself because he is a scholar, or from a book that achieves the purpose of what has been authored for Jumuah khutbahs — there is no harm in that. And those in authority should not appoint anyone except people of virtue, uprightness, and rectitude. But if it so happens that he is a sinner and leads the people in prayer — his prayer is valid according to the correct view, as long as he is a Muslim. Sin does not invalidate his prayer, nor the prayer of those behind him — neither Jumuah nor regular congregation."*
> — Shaykh Ibn Baz, [binbaz.org.sa](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/10659/%D9%85%D8%A7-%D9%8A%D8%B4%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%B7-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%B7%D9%8A%D8%A8)

### 9.4 The Imam and Khatib Should Be the Same Person

Islamweb mentions:

> "فالإمامة في الجمعة مثل الإمامة في الصلاة يشترط فيها أن يكون الإمام فيها مسلماً، عاقلاً، ذكراً، باتفاق، وتشترط هذه الشروط في الخطيب أيضا، فإذا اختل بعضها لم تصح الخطبة ولا تشرع الصلاة خلفه أيضا واشترط المالكية لصحة صلاة الجمعة أن يكون الإمام هو الخطيب فلو صلى غير من خطب لغير عذر فالجمعة باطلة."
>
> *"The imamah in Jumuah is like the imamah in prayer — it is required that the imam be a Muslim, sane, and male, by consensus. These conditions also apply to the khatib. If any of these is missing, the khutbah is invalid and praying behind him is not legislated. The Maliki school required for the validity of Jumuah that the imam be the same as the khatib — if someone other than the khatib prays (without excuse), the Jumuah is invalid."*
> — Islamweb, [source](https://islamweb.net/ar/fatwa/180121/)

### 9.5 The Imam's Responsibility to Straighten the Rows

**Shaykh Ibn Uthaymeen** (may Allah have mercy on him) mentioned one of the imam's duties:

> "From the responsibilities of the Imām is that he is ardent upon establishing and straightening the rows in both his speech and action. So if mere speech does not suffice, he then commands them to straighten the rows and line them up. He asserts that upon them and warns them from opposing that; and he straightens them with his hand if they do not listen just as our Prophet (salallāhu 'alaihi wasallam), our Imām and our example did."
> — Shaykh Ibn Uthaymeen, [abukhadeejah.com](https://abukhadeejah.com/jamah-straightening-rows-filling-the-gaps-shoulder-to-shoulder-feet-to-feet/)

## 10. Leading the Jumuah Prayer: Step-by-Step

### 10.1 The Intention

**Shaykh Ibn Baz** (may Allah have mercy on him) clarified that the intention is in the heart, not spoken:

> "ينوي بقلبه ويكفي، لا يقول شيئًا إلا التكبير، ينوي بقلبه الصلاة مثل بقية الصلوات، ثم إذا كبر الإمام؛ كبر الله أكبر، ناويًا بقلبه صلاة الجمعة، مثلما يكبر ناويًا صلاة الظهر والعصر والمغرب والعشاء وصلاة العيد إلى غير ذلك. أما بعض ما يقول بعض الناس: نويت أن أصلي كذا، هذا لا أصل له، بدعة، لا يتلفظ بالنية، النية محلها القلب."
>
> *"He intends with his heart and that suffices — he does not say anything except the takbir. He intends the prayer with his heart like the rest of the prayers. Then when the imam makes takbir, he says 'Allahu Akbar,' intending Jumuah prayer with his heart — just as he makes takbir intending Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha, Eid prayer, etc. As for what some people say: 'I intend to pray such-and-such' — this has no basis, it is a bid'ah. The intention is not articulated; the place of the intention is the heart."*
> — Shaykh Ibn Baz, [binbaz.org.sa](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/9932/%D9%83%D9%8A%D9%81%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%82%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%82%D8%A8%D9%84-%D8%B5%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A9)

### 10.2 The Number of Rak'ahs: Two

Jumuah consists of **two rak'ahs**, and this is not a shortening of Dhuhr — it is complete and prescribed as two. Islamweb reports:

> "The Jumu'ah (Friday prayer) was prescribed as two Rak'ahs and it [Jumu'ah] is not a shortened form of Thuhr according to the correct opinion of the scholars. Indeed, it is confirmed that Ibn 'Umar said: '...The Friday prayer is two Rak'ahs, it is complete without being shortened, as stated by the Prophet.' [An-Nasaa'i, Ibn Maajah and Al-Albaani classified it as Saheeh (authentic)]"
> — Islamweb, [source](https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/142059/why-friday-prayer-is-only-two-rakahs)

The evidence is the hadith of Umar ibn al-Khattab (may Allah be pleased with him):

> "صلاةُ السفرِ ركعتان وصلاةُ الجمعةِ ركعتان وصلاةُ الفطرِ ركعتان وصلاةُ الأضحى ركعتان تمامٌ غيرُ قصرٍ على لسانِ نبيِّكم"
>
> *"The prayer of travel is two rak'ahs, the prayer of Jumuah is two rak'ahs, the prayer of Eid al-Fitr is two rak'ahs, the prayer of Eid al-Adha is two rak'ahs — complete, not shortened — upon the tongue of your Prophet."*
> — Reported by Umar ibn al-Khattab, via [dorar.net](https://dorar.net/h/NjxZrwyt)

### 10.3 The Procedure: Following the Prophet's Prayer

**Shaykh Ibn Baz** (may Allah have mercy on him) described the Prophet's prayer:

> "كانت صلاته ﷺ تخفيفًا في تمام كما قال أنس : 'ما صليت خلف أحد أتم صلاة ولا أخف صلاة من النبي عليه الصلاة والسلام' كانت صلاته تخفيفًا في تمام؛ يتم ركوعها وسجودها واعتدالها بعد الركوع وبين السجدتين ولكن لا يطيل إطالة تمل الناس وتشق عليهم... وكان يركع ركوعًا فيه إتمام وفيه تخفيف، ربما سبح سبع تسبيحات .. عشر تسبيحات: سبحان ربي العظيم .. سبحان ربي العظيم، ويقول في ركوعه: سبحانك اللهم ربنا وبحمدك، اللهم اغفر لي .. سبوح قدوس رب الملائكة والروح"
>
> *"His prayer ﷺ was light in completion, as Anas (may Allah be pleased with him) said: 'I have not prayed behind anyone whose prayer was more complete or lighter than the Prophet ﷺ.' His prayer was light in completion — he would complete his ruku', sujud, rising from ruku', and between the two prostrations, but he would not prolong it in a way that would bore the people or burden them... He would bow with completion and lightness, perhaps glorifying seven times... ten times: 'Subhana Rabbi al-'Adheem... Subhana Rabbi al-'Adheem.' And he would say in his ruku': 'Subhanaka Allahumma Rabbana wa bihamdik, Allahummaghfir li... Subbuh Quddus Rabb al-Mala'ikah wa ar-Ruh.'"*
> — Shaykh Ibn Baz, [binbaz.org.sa](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/12388/%D8%B5%D9%81%D8%A9-%D8%B5%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D8%A8%D9%8A-%D8%B5%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%84%D9%87-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%87-%D9%88%D8%B3%D9%84%D9%85)

### 10.4 Following the Imam

The fundamental rule is stated in the hadith:

> "The Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) said: 'The Imām is appointed to be followed, so if he makes takbīr, then make takbīr; if he enters into rukū', then enter into rukū' (after him); when he rises from that, then rise; when he says: samiʿ Allāhu liman hamida (Allah hears the one who praises Him), then say: Rabbanā walakal-hamd (Our Lord, and for you is all praise). If he prays sitting, then all of you pray seated.' (Bukhārī, 722)"
> — [abukhadeejah.com](https://abukhadeejah.com/the-practice-of-prayer-in-the-life-of-a-muslim-salah-islam-2-4/)

### 10.5 The Step-by-Step Procedure

After the second khutbah, the imam leads the two rak'ahs as follows:

**First Rak'ah:**
1. The **iqamah** is called.
2. The imam makes the **intention** in his heart for Jumuah prayer.
3. The imam says **"Allahu Akbar"** (takbirat al-ihram), raising his hands.
4. The imam recites the **opening supplication** (du'a al-istiftah).
5. The imam says **"A'udhu billahi min ash-shaytan ar-rajim, Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim"**.
6. The imam recites **Surah al-Fatihah** aloud.
7. The imam recites **Surah al-A'la** (or Surah al-Jumu'ah) aloud.
8. The imam says **"Allahu Akbar"** and goes into **ruku'**, saying "Subhana Rabbi al-'Adheem" (three or more times).
9. The imam rises saying **"Sami'a Allahu liman hamidah"**, and upon standing says **"Rabbana wa lakal-hamd"**.
10. The imam says **"Allahu Akbar"** and goes into **sujud**, saying "Subhana Rabbi al-A'la" (three or more times).
11. The imam rises from sujud saying **"Allahu Akbar"**, sits briefly, then says **"Allahu Akbar"** for the second sujud.
12. The imam rises from the second sujud saying **"Allahu Akbar"** to stand for the second rak'ah.

**Second Rak'ah:**
1. The imam recites **Surah al-Fatihah** aloud.
2. The imam recites **Surah al-Ghashiyah** (or Surah al-Munafiqun) aloud.
3. The imam performs **ruku'** and **sujud** as in the first rak'ah.
4. After the second sujud, the imam sits for the **tashahhud** (final sitting).
5. The imam recites the **tashahhud**: "At-tahiyyatu lillahi, as-salawatu wat-tayyibat, as-salamu 'alayka ayyuhan-nabiyyu wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh, as-salamu 'alayna wa 'ala 'ibadillah as-salihin, ashhadu an la ilaha illa Allah, wa ashhadu anna Muhammadan 'abduhu wa rasuluh."
6. The imam recites **salat upon the Prophet ﷺ**: "Allahumma salli 'ala Muhammad wa 'ala ali Muhammad..."
7. The imam makes a **supplication** (du'a).
8. The imam turns his head to the right saying **"As-salamu 'alaykum wa rahmatullah"**, then to the left saying the same.

> **Note:** The imam should make the prayer **light but complete** — not prolonging it to burden the people, as the Prophet ﷺ did.

## 11. What to Recite in Jumuah Prayer

### 11.1 The Recommended Surahs

**Shaykh Ibn Baz** (may Allah have mercy on him) confirmed:

> "قراءة هاتين السورتين في صلاة الجمعة والعيد سنة؛ لأن النبي ﷺ كان يقرأ بهما، وإذا قرأ الإمام في بعض الأحيان غيرهما فلا بأس؛ ليعلم الناس أن قراءتهما غير واجبة، وكان النبي ﷺ في بعض الأحيان يقرأ في صلاة الجمعة بـ 'الجمعة' و'المنافقين'"
>
> *"Reciting these two surahs in Jumuah and Eid prayer is a Sunnah, because the Prophet ﷺ used to recite them. If the imam recites something other than them sometimes, there is no harm — to teach the people that reciting them is not obligatory. And the Prophet ﷺ sometimes recited in Jumuah prayer 'Al-Jumu'ah' and 'Al-Munafiqun.'"*
> — Shaykh Ibn Baz, [binbaz.org.sa](https://binbaz.org.sa/majmou-fatawa/30?page=15)

He also said:

> "المقدم: قراءة سَبِّحِ والغاشية -يا شيخ- واردة؟ الشيخ: سنة مؤكدة، في صلاة الجمعة، سَبِّحِ والغاشية، أو الجمعة والمنافقين، سورة الجمعة وسورة المنافقين، أو سورة الجمعة مع هَلْ أَتَاكَ حَدِيثُ الْغَاشِيَةِ [الغاشية:1] كلها واردة."
>
> *"Questioner: Is reciting 'Sabbih' [Surah al-A'la] and 'Al-Ghashiyah' reported? Shaykh: A confirmed Sunnah, in Jumuah prayer — 'Sabbih' and 'Al-Ghashiyah,' or 'Al-Jumu'ah' and 'Al-Munafiqun,' Surah al-Jumu'ah and Surah al-Munafiqun, or Surah al-Jumu'ah with 'Hal ataka hadith al-Ghashiyah' [Al-Ghashiyah:1] — all are reported."*
> — Shaykh Ibn Baz, [binbaz.org.sa](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/13987/%D9%85%D8%A7-%D9%8A%D9%86%D8%A8%D8%BA%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%AA%D8%B4%D8%AA%D9%85%D9%84-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%87-%D8%AE%D8%B7%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A9)

### 11.2 The Hadith Evidence

**Hadith of an-Nu'man ibn Bashir** (may Allah be pleased with him):

> "كان النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم يقرأ في العيدين وفي الجمعة: بسبح اسم ربك الأعلى، وهل أتاك حديث الغاشية، وربما اجتمعا في يوم واحد فيقرأ بهما"
>
> *"The Prophet ﷺ used to recite in the two Eids and in Jumuah: 'Sabbih isma rabbika al-A'la' [Surah 87] and 'Hal ataka hadith al-Ghashiyah' [Surah 88]. And sometimes they [Eid and Jumuah] would fall on the same day, and he would recite them."*
> — Narrated by an-Nu'man ibn Bashir | **Grading: Hasan Sahih** — Tirmidhi (533), Nasa'i (1424), Ahmad (18438), via [dorar.net](https://dorar.net/h/CQ3r7YEU?osoul=1)

**Hadith of Ibn Abbas** (may Allah be pleased with them both):

> "كان النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم كان يقرأ في الجمعة في الفجر: الم تنزيل السجدة، وهل أتى على الإنسان. وفي الجمعة بسورة الجمعة، والمنافقون"
>
> *"The Prophet ﷺ used to recite on Friday in the Fajr prayer: 'Alif Lam Mim Tanzil' [Surah as-Sajdah 32] and 'Hal ata 'ala al-insan' [Surah al-Insan 76]. And in the Jumuah prayer: Surah al-Jumu'ah and Surah al-Munafiqun."*
> — Narrated by Ibn Abbas | **Grading: Sahih** (authentic) — Sahih Muslim (2/161, Hadith 727), Abu Dawud (1074), via [dorar.net](https://dorar.net/h/JcGP4iTw?osoul=1)

**Hadith of Abu Hurayrah** (may Allah be pleased with him):

> "Ibn Abi Raafi' said: 'Marwan appointed Abu Hurayrah as his deputy in Madeenah and he himself left for Makkah. Therefore, Abu Hurayrah led us in the Jumu'ah prayer and he recited after Surah Al-Jumu'ah in the second Rak'ah: "When the hypocrites came to you…" [Surah 63] I then met Abu Hurayrah as he came back and I said to him: "You have recited two Surahs which 'Ali ibn Abi Taalib used to recite in Kufah." Upon this Abu Hurayrah said: "I heard the Messenger of Allaah reciting these two Surahs in the Friday prayer." [Muslim]'"
> — Islamweb, [source](https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/131686/reciting-surah-al-jumuah-every-friday-instead-of-surah-al-kahf)

### 11.3 Summary of Recommended Recitations

| First Rak'ah | Second Rak'ah | Evidence |
|--------------|---------------|----------|
| Surah al-A'la (87) | Surah al-Ghashiyah (88) | An-Nu'man ibn Bashir (Tirmidhi, Nasa'i — Hasan Sahih) |
| Surah al-Jumu'ah (62) | Surah al-Munafiqun (63) | Ibn Abbas (Sahih Muslim), Abu Hurayrah (Sahih Muslim) |

> **Note:** The imam recites **aloud (jahr)** in both rak'ahs of Jumuah, as it is from the audible prayers (salah jahriyyah), like Fajr, Maghrib, and Isha.

### 11.4 Reciting the Full Surahs

From the teachings of **Shaykh Ibn Uthaymeen** (may Allah have mercy on him):

> "سورة الجمعة والمنافقون....استحباب قراءة هاتين السورتين في صلاة الجمعة، واستحباب قراءتهما كاملتين، يعني لا ينصفهما فيقرأ واحدة ويجعلها نصفين في كل ركعة نصف، بل يكمل. ﴿ سبح اسم ربك الأعلى﴾، ﴿ هل أتاك حديثُ الغاشية﴾ يسن قراءة هاتين السورتين في صلاة العيد وفي صلاة الجمعة"
>
> *"Surah al-Jumu'ah and al-Munafiqun... the recommendation of reciting these two surahs in Jumuah prayer, and the recommendation of reciting them in full — meaning he does not split them, reciting one and dividing it into halves for each rak'ah, but rather he completes them. 'Sabbih isma rabbika al-A'la' and 'Hal ataka hadith al-Ghashiyah' — it is recommended to recite these two surahs in Eid prayer and in Jumuah prayer."*
> — Shaykh Ibn Uthaymeen, [saaid.net](http://www.saaid.net/Doat/alshwairek/545.htm)

## 12. Differences Between Jumuah and Dhuhr

| Aspect | Jumuah | Dhuhr |
|--------|--------|-------|
| **Number of rak'ahs** | 2 (complete, not shortened) | 4 |
| **Khutbah** | Required (2 khutbahs) | Not required |
| **Recitation** | Audible (jahr) | Silent (sirr) |
| **Obligated upon** | Resident, free, adult men | Everyone obligated to pray |
| **Time** | Same as Dhuhr (after zawal) | After zawal |
| **Congregation** | Required (minimum disputed: 3 or 40) | Recommended but not a condition |
| **Place** | Must be in a settlement/town | Anywhere |
| **Sunnah before** | No specific sunnah before | Sunnah rawatib (4 rak'ahs) |
| **Sunnah after** | 2 or 4 rak'ahs after | 4 rak'ahs after |

Regarding the sunnah prayers after Jumuah:

> "There is no sunnah prayer before Jumu'ah prayer. When the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) prayed Jumu'ah, he would enter his house and pray two Rak'ahs of Sunnah, and he commanded those who had prayed it to pray four rak'ahs afterwards."
> — [islamqa.info](https://islamqa.info/amp/en/answers/6653)

## 13. Mistakes and the Prostration of Forgetfulness

If the imam makes a mistake in the prayer (adding, omitting, or doubting), the **prostration of forgetfulness (sujud as-sahw)** is performed.

### 13.1 The Ruling

Islamweb explains:

> "The prostration for forgetfulness (Sujood as-Sahw) are two prostrations which a person makes before the Tasleem. All of this was confirmed by the Prophet. In Saheeh al-Bukhari, it is reported that Abu Sa'eed al-Khudri narrated that the Prophet said: 'If one of you has some doubts during his prayer and he does not recall (the number of Rak'as) he has prayed, three or four, then he can put an end to his doubt by performing the prayer according to what he was certain of [i.e. the lesser amount] and then perform two prostrations before the Tasleem (saying Salam to conclude the prayer).'"
> — Islamweb, [source](https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/81875/prostrations-for-forgetfulness-in-prayer)

### 13.2 The Congregation Follows the Imam

From the teachings of **Shaykh Ibn Uthaymeen** (may Allah have mercy on him):

> "If the imam experiences an incident of forgetfulness in the prayer, those led in prayer should follow him in offering the prostration of forgetfulness, because the Prophet (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) said: 'Indeed, the imam is appointed to be followed, so do not act differently from him... and if he offers prostration, then offer prostration.' [Narrated by Al-Bukhārī and Muslim on the authority of Abu Hurayrah (may Allah be pleased with him)]"
> — Shaykh Ibn Uthaymeen, [binothaimeen.net](https://shekhcp.binothaimeen.net/api/download-pdf/7d7ac21e-1047-42f3-b490-e83cab69d859/translated_books)

## 14. The Sunan and Etiquette of Friday

The Prophet ﷺ said in the hadith of Salman al-Farsi (may Allah be pleased with him):

> "Whoever takes a bath on Friday, purifies himself as much as he can, then uses his (hair) oil or perfumes himself with the scent of his house, then proceeds (for the Jumua prayer) and does not separate two persons sitting together (in the mosque), then prays as much as (Allah has) written for him and then remains silent while the Imam is delivering the Khutba, his sins in-between the present and the last Friday would be forgiven."
> — Sahih al-Bukhari, [salafipublications.com](https://salafipublications.com/sps//sbk/sahihalbukhari.cfm?scn=dsphadeeth&HadeethID=841)

The Sunan of Friday include:
1. **Ghusl** (ritual bath) — before going to the prayer
2. **Using perfume/oil** — applying one's best scent
3. **Wearing one's best clothes** — dressing well
4. **Going early** to the mosque
5. **Reciting Surah al-Kahf** — recommended on Friday
6. **Sending abundant salat upon the Prophet ﷺ** — especially on Friday
7. **Walking to the mosque** (not running)
8. **Sitting close** and not stepping over people
9. **Remaining silent** during the khutbah
10. **Praying the sunnah** after Jumuah (2 or 4 rak'ahs)
