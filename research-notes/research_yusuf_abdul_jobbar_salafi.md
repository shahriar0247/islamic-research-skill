## 3. General Salafi Principles for Evaluating an Unknown Contemporary Scholar's Manhaj and Aqeedah

The following principles are quoted verbatim from named Salafi scholars and are directly relevant to assessing someone whose manhaj and aqeedah are not known.

### 3.1 The Foundation: Ahlus-Sunnah Are Those Who Follow the Salaf and Abandon Innovation

**Imam al-Asbahaanee (d. 535H)** said:

> "The sign of Ahlus-Sunnah is that they follow the Salafus-Saalih and abandon all that is innovated and newly introduced into the Deen."
> — *Al-Hujjah fee Bayaanil Mahajjah* 1/364
> **Source URL:** https://www.salafipublications.com/sps/sp.cfm?subsecID=slf01&articleID=SLF010001&articlePages=1  
> **Also at:** https://ashabulhadeeth.com/2010/01/28/what-is-salafiyyah/

**Imam Abu Haneefah (d. 150H)** said:

> "Adhere to the athar (narration) and the tareeqah (way) of the Salaf (Pious Predecessors) and beware of newly invented matters for all of it is innovation."
> — Reported by As-Suyootee in *Sawn al-Mantaq wal-Kalaam* p. 32
> **Source URL:** https://www.salafipublications.com/sps/sp.cfm?subsecID=slf01&articleID=SLF010001&articlePages=1  
> **Also at:** https://ummaharchive.org/p/it-is-obligatory-upon-you-to-follow

**Shaykh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah (d. 728H)** said:

> "There is no criticism for the one who proclaims the way (madhdhab) of the Salaf, who attaches himself to it and refers to it. Rather, it is obligatory to accept that from him by unanimous agreement (Ittifaaq) because the way (madhdhab) of the Salaf is nothing but the Truth (Haqq)."
> — *Majmoo al-Fataawaa* 4:149
> **Source URL:** http://salafis.com/articles/emndqhc-ibn-taymiyyah-obligatory-to-accept-ascription-to-the-way-of-the-salaf.cfm  
> **Also at:** https://abukhadeejah.com/the-label-salafi-and-who-they-are-by-ibn-taymiyyah-ibn-abdul-wahhaab-ibn-baaz-al-albaanee-and-ibn-uthaimeen/

### 3.2 Aqeedah and Manhaj Are Inseparable; Corruption in Manhaj Springs from Corruption in Aqeedah

**Shaykh Muhammad ibn Saalih al-'Uthaymeen (d. 1421H)** said:

> "Manhaj — may Allaah bless you — is built upon the aqidah, so whoever's aqidah is sound, then his manhaj will be sound without doubt, because the Prophet (sallallaahu alayhi wasallam), when he mentioned the splitting of the Ummah into seventy-three [sects], all of them in the Fire except for one, they (the Companions) said, 'Which one is it O Messenger of Allaah?', and he replied: **'Whoever is upon the like of I and my Companions are upon.'**
>
> So his saying, 'Whoever is upon the like of I and my Companions are upon', meaning in aqidah and in manhaj and in action, and in everything. And it is not possible for this (i.e. aqidah) and this (i.e. manhaj) to be divergent... **when a person takes a manhaj that opposes the manhaj of the Messenger (alayhis salaatu was salaam), and his rightly-guided Caliphs, then its meaning is that his aqidah is not sound. Otherwise, when the aqidah is sound, the manhaj too will be sound.**"
> — Cassette: *Liqaa al-Baab al-Maftooh* no. 128
> **Source URL:** http://www.manhaj.com/manhaj/articles/xyzse-shaykh-ibn-uthaymeen-errors-in-manhaj-occur-due-to-underlying-errors-in-aqidah.cfm

**Shaykh 'Ubayd ibn 'Abdillaah al-Jaabiree (d. 1442H)** said:

> "Aqidah is that which is obligatory for a man to believe concerning Allaah the Mighty and Majestic, and that which has come from Him and concerning His Messengers and whatever His Messengers came with... As for manhaj, then it is a corroboration, establishment of the usool of the deen and its furoo' (foundations and branches), the manhaj is the path by which a person corroborates the foundations and branches of the deen. So if this path is in agreement with the Book and the Sunnah and the way of the Salaf us-Saalih, then this is a true manhaj. And if it is in opposition to that then it is a corrupt manhaj.
>
> **And Islaam is brought together (composed) of these two, (a) the soundness, correctness in aqidah, and (b) the soundness and safety in manhaj. Therefore, one of them cannot be separated from the other... The person whose manhaj is corrupt, have trust that this follows on from the corruption in his aqidah. When the aqidah is upright, in the correct way, then the manhaj will also be upright.**"
> — Cassette: *Al-Iydhaah wal-Bayaan fee Kashf ba'd Tareeq Farqat al-Ikhwaan*
> **Source URL:** https://www.manhaj.com/manhaj/articles/otddj-shaykh-ubayd-al-jaabiree-on-differentiating-between-aqidah-and-manhaj.cfm

**Shaykh Naasir ud-Deen al-Albaani (d. 1420H)** said:

> "That which I believe and worship Allaah with is that there are both generalities (in similarity) and specific (aspects) between the Salafi manhaj and aqidah. Aqidah is more specific than manhaj... However, manhaj is more vast than aqidah or Tawhid. As for those who claim that the differentiation that has come in this question (between aqidah and manhaj), then they desire by this to make it permissible for themselves to adopt ways and means in their da'wah to Islaam which the Salaf us-Salih were not upon... **It is for this reason that we say that manhaj is more vast than aqidah and Tawhid, hence it is necessary to adhere to what the Salaf us-Salih were upon with respect to both these affairs.**"
> — *Al-Asaalah Magazine*, Volume 22
> **Source URL:** http://www.manhaj.com/manhaj/articles/lvzvm-shaykh-al-albaanee-on-differentiating-between-aqidah-and-manhaj.cfm

### 3.4 A Scholar Must Be Known by His Scholars and His Companionship

**IslamQA** (based on the fatawa of Shaykh Ibn Baaz, Shaykh Ibn 'Uthaymeen, and the Permanent Committee) states:

> "The sign of the scholar and faqeeh who is qualified to issue fatwas is that he is able to use as evidence the verses of the Qur’an and the hadiths of the Prophet (blessings and peace of Allah be upon him), distinguishing what is sound from what is not, what abrogates and what is abrogated, what is specific in meaning and what is general in meaning, and who understands the meaning and the context of revelation... **Another sign of the scholars is that they are very religiously committed and have a good attitude, and they are also keen to follow the example of the righteous of the early generations, namely the Sahaabah and Taabi’een, and the leading scholars. So in general they do not drift away from their path.**"
> **Source URL:** https://islamqa.info/en/answers/198487

**Abu Iyaad Amjad Rafiq** wrote on the Salafi principle of judging by companionship:

> "From the perfection of the guidance of the Messenger (sallallaahu alayhi wasallam) in preserving the truth and maintaining the unity and strength of Ahl al-Sunnah, the followers of the Salaf, is the principle of judging a man by his companionship... **All one needs to do is to look at their friendships, allegiances, who they travel to or visit and who travels to and visits them, by which their reality is known. This is especially so in relation to callers, who give lectures and sit on platforms.**"
> **Source URL:** https://abuiyaad.com/a/juding-person-companhip

**Salafi Publications** writes:

> "Know O Sunni, that the false claimants to Salafiyyah who have innovated and become ahzaab (parties, sects)... though they may hide and conceal much of that which is with them, **they will not be able to hide it in their companionship and those whom they befriend and show love and loyalty for.** And this is from the manhaj of the Salaf..."
> **Source URL:** https://salafipublications.com/sps/sp.cfm?subsecID=SLF02&articleID=SLF020005&pfriend=

**Shaykh 'Abdul-Muhsin al-'Abbaad** said:

> "It is not binding for every person to have tazkiyah (commendation), it is not binding. However, the speech here is about a person having ability and grounding... And it is not binding that when you see someone teaching that you go and ask him, 'Do you have a tazkiyah or not? Have a Tazkiyah?'"

> **Two ways to acquire tazkiyah:**

## 6. Conclusion and Application of the Salafi Principles

Based on the research:

- **No direct Salafi source** has been found that endorses, praises, warns against, or classifies Dr. Yusuf Abdul-Jobbar.
- He therefore falls, in Salafi methodological terms, into the category of an **unknown contemporary caller/figure** whose manhaj and aqeedah have not been openly tested or attested by the people of knowledge.
- The general Salafi principles collected above teach that a claimant to knowledge and to Salafiyyah is judged by:
  1. **His actual aqeedah and manhaj** as expressed in his speech, writing, and conduct (Shaykh Uthaymeen, Shaykh Ubayd, Shaykh Albaani).
  2. **His companionship, allegiances, platforms, and who he befriends or promotes** (Abu Iyaad, Salafi Publications, Salafi Research Institute).
  3. **Whether he follows the Salaf in what they were upon in belief, worship, and behaviour** and **abandons innovation** (Imam al-Asbahaanee, Imam Abu Haneefah, Ibn Taymiyyah).
  4. **Whether he has true knowledge grounded in the Book and the authentic Sunnah**, not merely certificates or public titles (Shaykh Fawzaan, Shaykh 'Abbaad, IslamQA).
  5. **Whether he is known and attested by the scholars** (Shaykh Muhammad ibn Haadee, Shaykh 'Ubayd).

Until any of the above is established from a recognised Salafi source, **no firm Salafi classification of Dr. Yusuf Abdul-Jobbar's manhaj or aqeedah can be made** in this research file. Anyone seeking to take knowledge from him, to sit with him, or to recommend him should first look at his open statements on aqeedah, tawhid, the Sunnah, bid'ah, and the Salaf; at his teachers and their tazkiyat; and at the company and institutions he keeps — in light of the principles quoted above — and then take the matter to a known scholar of Ahlus-Sunnah if there is any doubt.
