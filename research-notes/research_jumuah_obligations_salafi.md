# Jumu'ah (Friday Prayer) Obligations and Mandatory Acts for the General Attendee
## From Salafi Scholars Only

---

## 1. Is Jumu'ah Obligatory (Fard) for Every Muslim Male? Conditions and Excuses

### The Four Conditions: Male, Adult, Free, Resident

**Shaykh Saalih al-Fawzan (hafidhahullah)** — commenting on Sharh as-Sunnah of Imam al-Barbahaaree:

> "Congregational prayer in the mosque is an obligation upon the sane, adult males who are able to attend. This is the most correct saying of the scholars."

- **Source:** *Ithaaful-Qaaree bit-Ta`leeqaat `alaa Sharhis-Sunnah* (A Gift To The Reader In Annotation Of Sharh As-Sunnah), by Shaykh Saalih ibn Fowzaan al-Fowzaan, translated by Aboo Talhah Daawood Burbank
- **URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2018/03/03/whoever-abandons-the-jumuah-prayer-and-the-congregational-prayers-in-the-mosque/

---

**Imam al-Barbahaaree** (as quoted by Shaykh al-Fawzan):

> "Whoever abandons the Jumu'ah Prayer and the congregational prayers in the mosque, without a valid excuse, then he is an innovator. The valid excuse is such as an illness, because of which he is unable to go out to the mosque, or fear of an oppressive ruler and whatever is besides that then there is no excuse for you."

- **Source:** *Sharh as-Sunnah*, Point 128, annotated by Shaykh Saalih al-Fawzan
- **URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2018/03/03/whoever-abandons-the-jumuah-prayer-and-the-congregational-prayers-in-the-mosque/

---

**Shaykh 'Abdul-'Azeez Bin Baz (rahimahullah)**:

> "Whoever did not attend the Friday prayer with the Muslims due to some lawful reason, such as illness or the like, or some other reasons, should pray the Zuhr prayer. Likewise, a woman prays the Zuhr prayer and so does the traveller and those living in the desert, as is proved by the Sunnah, and it is the opinion of the majority of the scholars, and no consideration should be paid to those who deviate from them."

- **Source:** *Fatawa Islamiyah*, Vol. 2, Page 448
- **URL:** https://www.fatwaislam.com/fis/index.cfm?scn=fd&ID=1188

---

### The Hadith of the Four Exceptions

**The Prophet (sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam) said:**

> "The Friday prayer in congregation is an obligatory duty upon every Muslim, with the exception of four: a slave, a woman, a child and a sick person."

- **Reference:** (Sunan Abi Dawud, Hadith 1067; al-Albani: Sahih)
- **Note:** Abu Dawud said Tariq did not hear from the Prophet directly, but al-Nawawi said: "This is not detrimental to its authenticity, for it is a Sahabi mursal, which is a proof, and the hadith is upon the conditions of the two Shaykhs (al-Bukhari and Muslim)." It has many supporting narrations.
- **URL:** https://sunnah.com/bulugh/2/393

---

**Shaykh Ibn 'Uthaymin (rahimahullah)** — regarding those who work on Fridays:

> "If their attendance of the Friday prayer causes a major loss, then those who are directly responsible for the work needed are excused for missing the Friday prayer. They pray zuhr instead. However, this should be limited to the minimum number of employees who can accomplish what is required. They should take turns: this Friday for so-and-so and next Friday for so-and-so. As for if no loss will occur, but rather, one will miss out on profits, then it is wajib for them to attend the Friday prayer."

- **Source:** *Majmu' Rasa'il wa Fatawa al-'Uthaymin*, 16/37
- **URL:** https://dorar.net/en/feqhia/122

---

### Summary of Conditions from dorar.net (Summary of Fiqh, citing Ibn 'Uthaymin):

> "The Friday prayer is wajib upon men who are free, legally accountable, residents who have no excuse for missing it. Consensus on this was related by Ibn al-Mundhir and Ibn 'Abd al-Barr."

> "Those not obliged to offer the Friday prayer must pray zuhr. If they attend the Friday prayer and offer it, this suffices them. Consensus on this was related by Ibn al-Mundhir, Ibn 'Abd al-Barr, al-Juwayni, Ibn Qudamah, and al-Nawawi."

- **URL:** https://dorar.net/en/feqhia/122

## 2. The Quran Verse: Surah al-Jumu'ah (62:9)

### The Verse

> **"O you who believe (Muslims)! When the call is proclaimed for the Salat (prayer) on the day of Friday (Jumu'ah prayer), come to the remembrance of Allah [Jumu'ah religious talk (Khutbah) and Salat (prayer)] and leave off business (and every other thing), that is better for you if you did but know!"**
> (Quran, Surah al-Jumu'ah [62]:9)

> **"Then when the (Jumu'ah) Salat (prayer) is finished, you may disperse through the land, and seek the Bounty of Allah (by working, etc.), and remember Allah much, that you may be successful."**
> (Quran, Surah al-Jumu'ah [62]:10)

---

### Tafsir of Ibn Kathir

**Imam Ibn Kathir (rahimahullah)** said:

> "O you who believe! When the call is proclaimed for the Salah on Al-Jumu'ah (Friday), then hasten (Fas'aw) to the remembrance of Allah" meaning, go to it and head for it. The meaning of Sa'y (hasten) here does not refer to walking quickly. It only refers to the importance of it. 'Umar bin Al-Khattab and Ibn Mas'ud - may Allah be pleased with them - recited it; 'Then proceed to the remembrance of Allah.' As for walking in haste to the prayer, that was indeed prohibited, since it was recorded in the Two Sahihs from Abu Hurayrah that the Prophet said, 'When you hear the Iqamah, proceed to offer the prayer with calmness and solemnity and do not rush. And pray whatever you catch, and complete whatever you have missed.'"

> "When the call is proclaimed for the Salah on Friday, referring to the Adhan which was called, during the time of the Prophet, when he came out of his house and sat on the Minbar. The Adhan would be called before the Prophet near the door of the Masjid. As for the earlier Adhan that the Leader of the faithful, Uthman bin Affan added, it was done because the Muslims increased in number during his time."

- **Source:** *Tafsir Ibn Kathir*, Surah al-Jumu'ah 62:9
- **URL:** http://www.quran4u.com/Tafsir%20Ibn%20Kathir/PDF/062%20Juma.pdf
- **Also available at:** https://quranx.com/tafsirs/62.9

---

### Tafsir of as-Sa'di

**Shaykh 'Abdur-Rahman as-Sa'di (rahimahullah)** said:

> "Allah, the Most High, commanded His believing servants to be present for the Jumu'ah prayer, and to hasten to it and strive for it when the call is made. Here 'sa'y' (hastening) means to be eager, to give importance to the Jumu'ah prayer and to make it the most important matter — it does not mean running, which is prohibited when going to prayer. He said: {وَذَرُوا الْبَيْعَ} meaning: when the call for Jumu'ah prayer is made, leave off buying and selling and set out for the prayer. {ذَٰلِكُمْ خَيْرٌ لَكُمْ} because the Jumu'ah prayer is better for you than being occupied with buying and selling and neglecting your obligatory prayer, which is more emphasized than all other obligations. {إِنْ كُنْتُمْ تَعْلَمُونَ} if you know this reality — that what is with Allah is better and more enduring, and whoever prefers the worldly life over the religion falls into true loss while thinking he is gaining profit."

**As-Sa'di also derived key rulings from these verses:**

> "From these verses there are several benefits: Among them: that Jumu'ah is an obligation (fard) upon all believers, it is obligatory upon them to hasten to it, and to be eager and concerned with it. Among them: that the two khutbahs on the day of Jumu'ah are two obligations that must be attended, because He explained 'dhikr' here to mean the two khutbahs, and Allah commanded to proceed to it and hasten to it. Among them: the legislated nature of the call (adhan) for the day of Jumu'ah, and the command to do it. Among them: the prohibition of buying and selling after the call for Jumu'ah, and its prohibition — and that is only because it causes one to miss the obligation and distracts from it. This indicates that any matter, even if permissible in its origin, if it results in missing an obligation, then it is not permissible in that situation."

- **Source:** *Tayseer al-Kareem ar-Rahman fi Tafsir Kalam al-Mannan* (Tafsir as-Sa'di), Surah al-Jumu'ah 62:9
- **URL (Arabic):** https://www.esinislam.com/Tafseer-Al-Quran/Tafseer-As-Saadiy/Surah-62.htm
- **URL (Urdu translation of as-Sa'di):** https://tohed.com/tafsir/saadi/62/9/

---

**Shaykh Saalih al-Fawzan (hafidhahullah)** — commenting on the same verse:

> "'O you who believe, when the prayer is called for on the Day of Jumu'ah, then rush to the Remembrance of Allaah and leave off the trading (commerce). That is better for you if you did but know. So when the prayer is finished then spread throughout the land and seek from Allaah's Bounty and remember Allaah much in order that you may be successful.' [Surah Al-Jumu'ah: 9-10]"

> "So the prayer, which some people claim takes time away from them seeking after the rizq (sustenance) and from buying and selling, is in fact the opposite of what they claim. It opens for them the door of provision, comfort and blessing. This is because the sustenance (rizq) is in the Hand of Allaah. So if you were to turn your attention towards remembering Him and worshipping Him, He would facilitate and open the door of sustenance for you. 'And Allaah is the best of Providers.' [Surah Al-Jumu'ah: 11]"

- **Source:** From the al-Ibaanah eBook — *Forbidden Business Transactions*, by Shaykh Saalih al-Fawzan
- **URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2015/01/14/do-not-let-your-commerce-divert-you-from-the-remembrance-of-allaah-shaykh-saalih-fawzan/

## 3. The Hadith About Missing Three Jumu'ahs

### The Hadith

**The Prophet (sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam) said:**

> "Whoever neglects the Friday prayer three times (in a row) without an excuse, then Allah sets a seal upon his heart."

- **Reference:** (Jami' at-Tirmidhi, Hadith 500; al-Albani: Hasan Sahih)
- **URL:** https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:500

**Also narrated as:**

> "Whoever missed three jumu'ahs out of negligence, Allah will place a seal over his heart."

- **Reference:** (Sunan an-Nasa'i, Hadith 1369; al-Albani: Hasan Sahih)
- **URL:** https://sunnah.com/nasai:1369

**Also narrated:**

> "He who leaves the Friday prayer (continuously) for three Friday on account of slackness, Allah will print a stamp on his heart."

- **Reference:** (Sunan Abi Dawud, Hadith 1052; al-Albani: Hasan Sahih)
- **URL:** https://sunnah.com/abudawud/2/663

**Also narrated:**

> "The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) said: 'Whoever abandons Friday (prayer) three times, for no necessary reason, Allah will place a seal over his heart.'"

- **Reference:** (Sunan Ibn Majah, Hadith 1126; al-Albani: Hasan)
- **URL:** https://sunnah.com/ibnmajah:1126

**Additional narration from Ibn 'Umar and Abu Hurairah:**

> "People must cease to neglect the Friday prayer or Allah will seal their hearts and then they will be among the negligent."

- **Reference:** (Sahih Muslim, Hadith 865; al-Albani: Sahih)
- **URL:** https://sunnah.com/muslim:865

### al-Albani's Grading

The hadith of Abu al-Ja'd ad-Damri ("Whoever misses three Jumu'ahs out of negligence, Allah will seal his heart") is graded **Hasan Sahih** by Shaykh al-Albani. This grading is confirmed across multiple collections:
- Tirmidhi 500: al-Albani: Hasan Sahih
- Abu Dawud 1052: al-Albani: Hasan Sahih
- Ibn Majah 1125: al-Albani: Hasan Sahih
- Nasa'i 1369/1370: al-Albani: Hasan Sahih

- **Source for grading:** https://en.tohed.com/hadith/tirmidhi/500/ and https://en.tohed.com/hadith/abu-dawud/1052/

## 4. Is Listening to the Khutbah Obligatory? What is the Evidence?

### The Hadith on Silence During the Khutbah

**The Prophet (sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam) said:**

> "When the Imam is delivering the Khutba, and you ask your companion to keep quiet and listen, then no doubt you have done an evil act."

- **Reference:** (Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 934; al-Albani: Sahih)
- **URL:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:934

**Also narrated:**

> "If you (even) ask your companion to be quiet on Friday while the Imam is delivering the sermon, you have in fact talked irrelevance."

- **Reference:** (Sahih Muslim, Hadith 851a; al-Albani: Sahih)
- **URL:** https://sunnah.com/muslim:851a

**Also narrated:**

> "If you say to your companions: 'Be quiet' on a Friday while the Imam is delivering the sermon, you have engaged in Laghw (idle talk or behaviour)."

- **Reference:** (Sunan Ibn Majah, Hadith 1110; al-Albani: Sahih)
- **URL:** https://sunnah.com/ibnmajah:1110

**Also narrated:**

> "When you tell (your brother on Friday) to be silent while the imam is giving the sermon you are guilty of idle talk."

- **Reference:** (Sunan Abi Dawud, Hadith 1112; al-Albani: Sahih)
- **URL:** https://sunnah.com/abudawud:1112

### The Hadith on the Reward of Listening and Remaining Silent

**The Prophet (sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam) said:**

> "If anyone performs Wudu' properly, then comes to the Friday prayer, listens to the Khutbah (religious talk) attentively and keeps silent, his (minor) sins between that Friday and the following Friday will be forgiven, with the addition of three more days; but he who touches pebbles has caused an interruption."

- **Reference:** (Sahih Muslim, Hadith [Riyad as-Salihin 1148]; al-Albani: Sahih)
- **URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2014/09/04/riyad-us-saliheen-imaam-nawawi-chapter-210/

**Also narrated:**

> "If a man takes bath on Friday, (or) purifies himself as much as he can with Wudu', oils his hair, applies whatever perfume available in his house, sets forth for the mosque, does not separate two people (to make a seat for himself), performs Salat what is prescribed for him, remains silent when the Imam speaks, his (minor) sins between that Friday and the following Friday will be forgiven."

- **Reference:** (Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith [Riyad as-Salihin 1154]; al-Albani: Sahih)
- **URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2014/09/04/riyad-us-saliheen-imaam-nawawi-chapter-210/

### Shaykh al-Albani on Listening to the Qur'an

**Shaykh Muhammad Nasir ad-Din al-Albani (rahimahullah)** said regarding listening to the recitation of the Qur'an:

> "If the circumstance is a gathering of knowledge, remembrance and recitation of the Qur'an, then in this situation it is obligatory to pay complete attention to it. And whoever doesn't do so is sinning because he is disobeying Allah's statement in the Qur'an: 'And when the Qur'an is recited, then listen to it and remain silent, in order that you may receive mercy.' [Surah Al-A'raaf: 204]"

- **Source:** *Playing the Qur'aan (on tape) while not Listening to it*, Imaam Al-Albaanee
- **URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2014/09/30/playing-the-quraan-on-tape-while-not-listening-to-it-imaam-al-albaanee/

> **Note:** This principle of obligatory silence and listening when the Qur'an or knowledge is being recited/delivered applies even more so to the Jumu'ah khutbah, which is a gathering of knowledge, remembrance, and recitation of Qur'an.

## 5. The Two Rak'ahs of Jumu'ah Prayer — How They Are Prayed

### Shaykh al-Albani on the Description of the Prayer

**Shaykh Muhammad Nasir ad-Din al-Albani (rahimahullah)** said:

> "He should recite aloud in the Dawn (Fajr) Prayer, the Jumu'ah Prayer, the two 'Eid Prayers, the Prayer for seeking rain, the Eclipse Prayer and in the first two rak'ahs of the Maghrib and 'Ishaa Prayers."

- **Source:** *The Abridgement of the Prophet's Prayer Described*, Shaykh al-Albani
- **URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2016/07/23/the-abridgement-of-the-prophets-prayer-described/

**Shaykh al-Albani also said:**

> "The Messenger of Allah –sallAllaahu alayhi wa sallam– used to pray the Jumma' prayer reciting Soorah al-Jumma' and Soorah al-Munafiqoon, and likewise as is collected by Muslim from Ibn Abbas and an-Num'aan bin Basheer –RadhiAllaahu anhumma: 'The Messenger –sallAllaahu alayhi wa sallam– used to recite in the two Eid prayers and in the Jumma' prayer with Soorah al-Ala and al-Ghashiyah.'"

- **Source:** *Ajwibaa an-Nafi'ah* by Shaykh al-Albani, p. 57
- **URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2015/05/22/the-purpose-of-the-jumma-khutbah-friday-sermon/

### Shaykh Ibn Baz on Praying Two Rak'ahs Upon Entering During the Khutbah

**Shaykh 'Abdul-'Azeez Bin Baz (rahimahullah)** said:

> "The Sunnah upon entering the masjid is to pray two rak'ahs of tahyatul masjid (a Prayer for greeting the mosque), even if the Imaam is delivering the Friday khutbah, due to the sayings of the Prophet sallallaahu 'alayhi wa sallam: 'When anyone of you enters the masjid, then let him not sit until he prays two rak'ahs.' Recorded by al-Bukhaaree (1/293) and Muslim (2/155). Likewise Muslim relates in his Saheeh (3/1415), from Jaabir radiallaahu 'anhu, that the Prophet sallallaahu 'alayhi wa sallam said: 'When any of you comes on the day of Jumu'ah (Friday), and the Imaam is delivering the khutbah, then let him pray two rak'ahs and let him make them short.' So this is a clear and authentic text concerning this issue. It is not permissible for anyone to oppose it."

- **Source:** *Al-Fataawaa* (1/53), also published in *Al-Istiqaamah* Issue No. 7, Rabî' ul-Awwal 1418H / July 1997
- **URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2014/12/26/praying-during-the-friday-sermon-khutbah-shaykh-bin-baz/

### Catching One Rak'ah

**The Prophet (sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam) said:**

> "Whoever could get one rak'ah of a prayer, (in its proper time) he has got the prayer."

- **Reference:** (Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 580; Sahih Muslim, Hadith 607; al-Albani: Sahih)
- **URL:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:580 and https://sunnah.com/muslim:607a

**Shaykh Ibn Baz (rahimahullah)** said regarding catching one rak'ah of Jumu'ah:

> "Whoever caught a Rak'ah of the Jumu'ah prayer, then he caught the Jumu'ah prayer, as is reported in an authentic Hadith from the Messenger of Allah. [...] This is according to the generality of the prophet's statement: 'Whoever caught a Rak'ah of the prayer, then verily he caught the prayer.' (Al-Bukhari no. 580 and Muslim no. 607)"

- **Source:** *Fatawa Islamiyah*, vol. 3, pp. 24-25, DARUSSALAM
- **URL:** https://www.fatwaislam.com/fis/index.cfm?ID=894&scn=fd

## 6. Is the Khutbah a Condition/Rukn of Jumu'ah?

### as-Sa'di: The Two Khutbahs Are Obligations

**Shaykh 'Abdur-Rahman as-Sa'di (rahimahullah)** said:

> "Among them [the benefits of these verses]: that the two khutbahs on the day of Jumu'ah are two obligations (fard) that must be attended, because He explained 'dhikr' here to mean the two khutbahs, and Allah commanded to proceed to it and hasten to it."

- **Source:** *Tafsir as-Sa'di* (Tayseer al-Kareem ar-Rahman), Surah al-Jumu'ah 62:9
- **URL:** https://www.esinislam.com/Tafseer-Al-Quran/Tafseer-As-Saadiy/Surah-62.htm

### Ibn al-Qayyim: The Purpose of the Khutbah

**Imam Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyyah (rahimahullah)** said:

> "On the day of Jumu'ah there is a Khutbah (sermon) the objective of which is to glorify Allaah and regard Him as Magnificent, and testify to singling out Allaah Alone as the Creator, and testify that His Messenger -sallAllaahu alayhi wa sallam- brought the message. Also to remind the worshippers about the Messenger's days. To warn the people from Allaah's torment and His punishment, and advising them with that which will bring them closer to Allaah and to His Paradise. Likewise prohibiting them from that which will bring them close to His Anger and His Fire. Therefore, this is the purpose / intent of the Khutbah and gathering for it."

- **Source:** *Zad al-Ma'ad* by Ibn Qayyim, vol. 1, p. 398, point 22
- **URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2015/05/22/the-purpose-of-the-jumma-khutbah-friday-sermon/

### al-Albani: The Description of the Legislated Khutbah

**Shaykh Muhammad Nasir ad-Din al-Albani (rahimahullah)** said:

> "Know that the type of Khutbah which is legislated is that which the Messenger –sallAllaahu alayhi wa sallam– regularly used to be upon, which was persuading (with good rewards) the people [and] dissuading (warning) them — this in reality is the soul of the Khutbah for which it was legislated."

> "The Messenger of Allaah –sallAllaahu alayhi wa sallam– used to deliver two Khutbahs and sit between them, he would also recite the Qur'aan, remind the people and warn them."

- **Source:** *al-Ajwiba an-Nafiah 'an 'Asseelah lajna Masjid aj-Jamia'*, summarised from pp. 94-99
- **URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2015/05/22/the-purpose-of-the-jumma-khutbah-friday-sermon/

### al-Albani: Shortening the Khutbah and Lengthening the Prayer

**Shaykh al-Albani (rahimahullah)** said:

> "From 'Amaar bin Yassir who said: I heard the Messenger of Allaah –sallAllaahu alayhi wa sallam– saying: 'Indeed a person who lengthens the prayer and shortening the Khutbah is a sign of the Fiqh of a man.' [Collected by Imam Muslim]"

> "Lengthen the prayer, and shorten the Khutbah, and indeed there is magic in speech."

- **Source:** *Ajwibaa an-Nafi* by Shaykh al-Albani, p. 57
- **URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2015/05/22/the-purpose-of-the-jumma-khutbah-friday-sermon/

### The Khutbah as a Condition for Validity (from Summary of Fiqh)

> "It is a condition for the validity of the Friday prayer that there are two sermons beforehand, as the Noble Prophet ﷺ always did. It has been narrated from Sayyidina Abdullah ibn Umar رضي الله عنه that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ would stand and deliver two sermons, sitting for a short while between the two sermons."

- **URL:** https://dorar.net/en/feqhia/122 (citing the general position with consensus)

**Shaykh Ibn 'Uthaymin (rahimahullah)** — on the khutbah being a condition:

> "That it be preceded by two sermons; since the Prophet ﷺ always did that. The two sermons are obligatory and they are a precondition for the validity of (Salat al-Jumu'ah)."

- **Source:** As cited in Summary of Fiqh (al-Fiqh al-Manhaji and al-Mulakhkhas al-Fiqhi)
- **URL:** https://www.al-feqh.com/en/jummah-prayer-friday-prayer

## 7. The Obligation of Attending Early / Before the Imam Arrives

### The Hadith on the Five Hours

**The Prophet (sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam) said:**

> "Any person who takes a bath on Friday like the bath of Janaba and then goes for the prayer (in the first hour i.e. early), it is as if he had sacrificed a camel (in Allah's cause); and whoever goes in the second hour it is as if he had sacrificed a cow; and whoever goes in the third hour, then it is as if he had sacrificed a horned ram; and if one goes in the fourth hour, then it is as if he had sacrificed a hen; and whoever goes in the fifth hour then it is as if he had offered an egg. When the Imam comes out (i.e. starts delivering the Khutba), the angels present themselves to listen to the Khutba."

- **Reference:** (Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith [Book 13: Friday Prayer]; al-Albani: Sahih)
- **URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2014/09/02/sahih-bukhari-book-013/

**Also narrated:**

> "When it is a Friday, the angels stand at the gate of the mosque and keep on writing the names of the persons coming to the mosque in succession according to their arrivals. The example of the one who enters the mosque in the earliest hour is that of one offering a camel (in sacrifice). The one coming next is like one offering a cow and then a ram and then a chicken and then an egg respectively. When the Imam comes out (for Jumua prayer) they (i.e. angels) fold their papers and listen to the Khutba."

- **Reference:** (Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith [Book 13]; al-Albani: Sahih)
- **URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2014/09/02/sahih-bukhari-book-013/

### Shaykh Ibn 'Uthaymin on When the First Hour Begins

**Shaykh Muhammad bin Salih al-'Uthaymin (rahimahullah)** said:

> "The hours which the Messenger (صلّى الله عليه و سلّم) has mentioned are five in number; he said: 'Whoever bathed on Friday, performing the Ghusl of major ritual impurity (Janabah), then went (to the Masjid) in the first hour, he is like one who offers a camel as a sacrifice, and he who comes at the second hour is like one who offers a cow, and he who comes at the third hour is like one who offers a ram with horns, and he who comes at the fourth hour is like one who offers a hen, and he who comes at the fifth hour is like one who offers an egg.' So, he has divided the time from sunrise until the arrival of the Imam into five parts. Each part might be equivalent to the well known hour (i.e. 60 minutes), or the hour might be more or less, because the time changes, so the hours are five between the sunrise and the arrival of the Imam for the prayer. It begins from sunrise, although it has been said that it is from the start of Fajr, but the former is more likely, because before sunrise is the time for the Fajr prayer."

- **Source:** Shaykh 'Uthaymeen, cited with reference [01] to Al-Bukhari (881) and Muslim
- **URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2019/01/14/when-does-the-first-hour-of-the-friday-prayer-begin/

### Additional Hadith on Early Attendance and Reward

**The Prophet (sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam) said:**

> "Whoever performs Ghusl (well) on the day of Jumu'ah, leaves early, walking not riding, and sits close to the Imam and listens without talking, will earn the reward of fasting and performing standing (in prayer) for an entire year for every step he takes."

- **Reference:** (Musnad Ahmad, Sunan Abu Dawud, at-Tirmidhi; al-Albani: Hasan)
- **URL:** https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-10-sora-62.html (citing Tafsir Ibn Kathir)

### Abu Khadeejah on Reserving Places

**Abu Khadeejah** said:

> "The male worshipper is commanded with attending the Masjid early and with standing near to the Imaam himself, and not reserving his place by the use of a stick, or a staff or a prayer mat. Those who reserve places in this manner do so with the intention of praying in the first row and attaining the reward for that. However by merely placing a stick, or a prayer mat in the front row and then leaving the masjid, a person opposes the Sunnah."

- **URL:** https://abukhadeejah.com/reserving-places-in-the-masjid/

## 8. What Invalidates Jumu'ah Attendance

### Sleeping Through the Khutbah

**The Permanent Committee for Research and Verdicts (Shaykh Ibn Baz, Shaykh 'Abdullah bin Ghudayaan, Shaykh 'Abdur-Razzaaq al-'Afeefee)** said:

> "Indeed the light sleep in which consciousness is not fully lost does not nullify the wudhoo. For it is indeed established that the Prophet (sal Allaahu alaiyhi wa sallam) occasionally used to delay the isha prayer until the heads of the Companions would be nodding off with sleep, then they (the Companions) would pray and they would not make the wudhoo (again)."

- **Source:** Fatwa 12885, P263, Volume 5, *Fataawa of the Permanent Committee*
- **URL:** https://www.fatwaislam.com/fis/index.cfm?ID=647&scn=fd

> **Note:** This fatwa addresses light sleep during the khutbah. Deep sleep that fully removes consciousness would nullify wudu, and thus affect the validity of the prayer that follows. Missing Jumu'ah completely due to oversleeping requires praying Zuhr (4 rak'ahs) instead.

### Speaking During the Khutbah

As established in Section 4, speaking during the khutbah — even telling someone to be quiet — is considered "laghw" (idle talk) and is prohibited. The Prophet (sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam) said:

> "When the Imam is delivering the Khutba, and you ask your companion to keep quiet and listen, then no doubt you have done an evil act."

- **Reference:** (Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 934; al-Albani: Sahih)
- **URL:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:934

### Trading After the Second Adhan

**Shaykh Saalih al-Fawzan (hafidhahullah)** — citing the verse:

> "O you who believe (Muslims)! When the call is proclaimed for the Salat (prayer) on the day of Friday (Jumu'ah prayer), come to the remembrance of Allah [Jumu'ah religious talk (Khutbah) and Salat (prayer)] and leave off business (and every other thing), that is better for you if you did but know!"

> [Soorah Al-Jum'ah. Aayaat 9-10]

- **Source:** From al-Ibaanah eBook — *Forbidden Business Transactions*, Shaykh Saalih al-Fawzan
- **URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2015/06/04/impermissible-for-a-muslim-to-indulge-in-trade-after-the-2nd-adhan-of-jumuah/

### Missing Three Jumu'ahs Out of Negligence

As established in Section 3, missing three Jumu'ahs out of negligence results in Allah sealing the heart. This is among the most severe warnings.

### Giving Salaam and Shaking Hands During the Khutbah

**Shaykh Ibn Baz and Shaykh 'Abdullah bin Ghudayaan (rahimahumullah)** said:

> "He should shake him by the hand and not speak, then he should return the salaam (verbally) to him after the first khutbah has ceased. This is if he gave you salaam during the course of the first khutbah. If however he gave you salaam during the course of the Imaam delivering the second khutbah then you should give him the reply to his salaam after the Imaam has finished from the second khutbah."

- **Source:** *Fataawaa Al-Lajnah ad-Daa'imah*, vol. 8, page 246, question 2 of fatwa number 3774
- **URL:** http://www.fatwaislam.com/fis/index.cfm?ID=838&scn=fd

### Raising Hands During the Khutbah

**Shaykh Ibn 'Uthaymin (rahimahullah)** said (as cited by the Permanent Committee):

> "It is not prescribed for the Imam or the worshippers to raise their hands during the Friday Khutbah, because the Messenger of Allaah (sallallaahu alaihi wa sallam) did not do so, and neither did his righteous successors, may Allaah be pleased with them. But if the Imam is calling for rain in the Friday Khutbah, it is prescribed for him and the worshippers to raise their hands, because when the Prophet (sallallaahu alaihi wa sallam) prayed for rain, he raised his hands and the people raised their hands."

- **Source:** *Fataawa Islamiyyah*, DARUSSALAM, vol. 2, p. 477 (also Fataawa al-Lajnah ad-Daa'imah)
- **URL:** https://www.fatwaislam.com/fis/index.cfm?scn=fd&ID=1237

## 9. Rulings from Specific Scholars

### Ibn Taymiyyah

**Shaykh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah (rahimahullah)** — on Eid falling on Friday:

> "Whosoever attends the Eid, then the Jumu'ah falls away from him. However; it is upon the Imaam to establish the Jumu'ah prayer, to allow anyone wishing to attend to do so — as well as those that did not attend the Eid. Since this is what has been conveyed from the Prophet (Sallallaahu 'alaihi wa sallam) and his companions, there is no differing known amongst the companions in that. So one who does not attend the Jumu'ah prayer instead prays Dhuhr — thus the Dhuhr is prayed in its time."

- **Source:** *Ad-Durar as-Sanniyyah fil Ajwibatin Najdiyyah*, vol. 5, page 50
- **URL:** http://www.fatwaislam.com/fis/index.cfm?ID=823&scn=fd

### Ibn al-Qayyim

**Imam Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyyah (rahimahullah)** said:

> "On the day of Jumu'ah there is a Khutbah (sermon) the objective of which is to glorify Allaah and regard Him as Magnificent, and testify to singling out Allaah Alone as the Creator, and testify that His Messenger -sallAllaahu alayhi wa sallam- brought the message. Also to remind the worshippers about the Messenger's days. To warn the people from Allaah's torment and His punishment, and advising them with that which will bring them closer to Allaah and to His Paradise. Likewise prohibiting them from that which will bring them close to His Anger and His Fire. Therefore, this is the purpose / intent of the Khutbah and gathering for it."

- **Source:** *Zad al-Ma'ad* by Ibn Qayyim, vol. 1, p. 398, point 22
- **URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2015/05/22/the-purpose-of-the-jumma-khutbah-friday-sermon/

### Ibn Baz

**Shaykh 'Abdul-'Azeez Bin Baz (rahimahullah)** — on missing Jumu'ah:

> "Whoever did not attend the Friday prayer with the Muslims due to some lawful reason, such as illness or the like, or some other reasons, should pray the Zuhr prayer. Likewise, a woman prays the Zuhr prayer and so does the traveller and those living in the desert, as is proved by the Sunnah, and it is the opinion of the majority of the scholars, and no consideration should be paid to those who deviate from them."

- **Source:** *Fatawa Islamiyah*, Vol. 2, Page 448
- **URL:** https://www.fatwaislam.com/fis/index.cfm?scn=fd&ID=1188

**Shaykh Ibn Baz (rahimahullah)** — on praying tahiyyatul masjid during the khutbah:

> "The Sunnah upon entering the masjid is to pray two rak'ahs of tahyatul masjid, even if the Imaam is delivering the Friday khutbah, due to the sayings of the Prophet sallallaahu 'alayhi wa sallam: 'When anyone of you enters the masjid, then let him not sit until he prays two rak'ahs.' [...] 'When any of you comes on the day of Jumu'ah, and the Imaam is delivering the khutbah, then let him pray two rak'ahs and let him make them short.' So this is a clear and authentic text concerning this issue. It is not permissible for anyone to oppose it."

- **Source:** *Al-Fataawaa* (1/53), *Al-Istiqaamah* Issue No. 7
- **URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2014/12/26/praying-during-the-friday-sermon-khutbah-shaykh-bin-baz/

**Shaykh Ibn Baz (rahimahullah)** — on catching one rak'ah:

> "Whoever caught a Rak'ah of the Jumu'ah prayer, then he caught the Jumu'ah prayer, as is reported in an authentic Hadith from the Messenger of Allah. [...] 'Whoever caught a Rak'ah of the prayer, then verily he caught the prayer.' (Al-Bukhari no. 580 and Muslim no. 607)"

- **Source:** *Fatawa Islamiyah*, vol. 3, pp. 24-25, DARUSSALAM
- **URL:** https://www.fatwaislam.com/fis/index.cfm?ID=894&scn=fd

### al-Albani

**Shaykh Muhammad Nasir ad-Din al-Albani (rahimahullah)** — on the legislated khutbah:

> "Know that the type of Khutbah which is legislated is that which the Messenger –sallAllaahu alayhi wa sallam– regularly used to be upon, which was persuading (with good rewards) the people [and] dissuading (warning) them — this in reality is the soul of the Khutbah for which it was legislated."

> "The Messenger of Allaah –sallAllaahu alayhi wa sallam– used to deliver two Khutbahs and sit between them, he would also recite the Qur'aan, remind the people and warn them."

- **Source:** *al-Ajwiba an-Nafiah*, summarised from pp. 94-99
- **URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2015/05/22/the-purpose-of-the-jumma-khutbah-friday-sermon/

**Shaykh al-Albani (rahimahullah)** — on shortening the khutbah:

> "Indeed a person who lengthens the prayer and shortening the Khutbah is a sign of the Fiqh of a man." [Collected by Imam Muslim]

> "Lengthen the prayer, and shorten the Khutbah, and indeed there is magic in speech."

- **Source:** *Ajwibaa an-Nafi* by Shaykh al-Albani, p. 57
- **URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2015/05/22/the-purpose-of-the-jumma-khutbah-friday-sermon/

**Shaykh al-Albani (rahimahullah)** — on reciting aloud in Jumu'ah:

> "He should recite aloud in the Dawn (Fajr) Prayer, the Jumu'ah Prayer, the two 'Eid Prayers, the Prayer for seeking rain, the Eclipse Prayer and in the first two rak'ahs of the Maghrib and 'Ishaa Prayers."

- **Source:** *The Abridgement of the Prophet's Prayer Described*
- **URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2016/07/23/the-abridgement-of-the-prophets-prayer-described/

### al-Uthaymin

**Shaykh Muhammad bin Salih al-'Uthaymin (rahimahullah)** — on those who work on Fridays:

> "If their attendance of the Friday prayer causes a major loss, then those who are directly responsible for the work needed are excused for missing the Friday prayer. They pray zuhr instead. However, this should be limited to the minimum number of employees who can accomplish what is required. They should take turns: this Friday for so-and-so and next Friday for so-and-so. As for if no loss will occur, but rather, one will miss out on profits, then it is wajib for them to attend the Friday prayer."

- **Source:** *Majmu' Rasa'il wa Fatawa al-'Uthaymin*, 16/37
- **URL:** https://dorar.net/en/feqhia/122

**Shaykh Ibn 'Uthaymin (rahimahullah)** — on the first hour of Jumu'ah:

> "So, he has divided the time from sunrise until the arrival of the Imam into five parts. Each part might be equivalent to the well known hour (i.e. 60 minutes), or the hour might be more or less, because the time changes, so the hours are five between the sunrise and the arrival of the Imam for the prayer. It begins from sunrise."

- **URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2019/01/14/when-does-the-first-hour-of-the-friday-prayer-begin/

**Shaykh Ibn 'Uthaymin (rahimahullah)** — on the language of the khutbah:

> "The correct opinion in this matter is that it is not permissible for the one who delivers the Friday sermon to do so in a language which those present do not understand. So, if the people are not Arabs, and they do not know the Arabic language, then he should deliver the sermon in their language, because this is the means of communicating the message to them, and the point of the sermon is to explain the limits set by Allaah, the Most Glorified, the Most High to the worshippers, and to admonish and guide them. However, the Qur'anic verses must be recited in the Arabic language and then explained in the language of the people."

- **Source:** *Fatawa Arkaanul Islaam*, Darussalam, vol. 2, p. 557
- **URL:** https://fatwaislam.com/fis/index.cfm?ID=693&scn=fd

**Shaykh Ibn 'Uthaymin (rahimahullah)** — on saying Ameen during the khutbah:

> "It is not an innovation to say Aameen when the Khatib supplicates during the Khutbah. If he begins to supplicate for the Muslims, then it is a preferred act to say Aameen to his supplication, but this should not be done in unison, nor in a loud voice. Rather, every person should recite individually and in a low voice, so that there is no annoyance or shouting."

- **Source:** *Fataawa Islamiyyah*, DARUSSALAM, vol. 2, p. 477
- **URL:** https://www.fatwaislam.com/fis/index.cfm?ID=781&scn=fd

### al-Fawzan

**Shaykh Saalih al-Fawzan (hafidhahullah)** — on abandoning Jumu'ah:

> "Whoever abandons the Jumu'ah Prayer and the congregational prayers in the mosque, without a valid excuse, then he is an innovator. The valid excuse is such as an illness, because of which he is unable to go out to the mosque, or fear of an oppressive ruler and whatever is besides that then there is no excuse for you."

> "Congregational prayer in the mosque is an obligation upon the sane, adult males who are able to attend. This is the most correct saying of the scholars."

- **Source:** *Ithaaful-Qaaree bit-Ta`leeqaat `alaa Sharhis-Sunnah*, annotation of Sharh as-Sunnah by Imam al-Barbahaaree
- **URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2018/03/03/whoever-abandons-the-jumuah-prayer-and-the-congregational-prayers-in-the-mosque/

**Shaykh Saalih al-Fawzan (hafidhahullah)** — on commerce diverting from Jumu'ah:

> "So the prayer, which some people claim takes time away from them seeking after the rizq (sustenance) and from buying and selling, is in fact the opposite of what they claim. It opens for them the door of provision, comfort and blessing. This is because the sustenance (rizq) is in the Hand of Allaah. So if you were to turn your attention towards remembering Him and worshipping Him, He would facilitate and open the door of sustenance for you. 'And Allaah is the best of Providers.' [Surah Al-Jumu'ah: 11]"

- **Source:** From the al-Ibaanah eBook — *Forbidden Business Transactions*
- **URL:** https://abdurrahman.org/2015/01/14/do-not-let-your-commerce-divert-you-from-the-remembrance-of-allaah-shaykh-saalih-fawzan/

| Obligation | Evidence | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Jumu'ah is fard 'ayn upon every free, adult, sane, resident Muslim male | Surah al-Jumu'ah 62:9; Hadith of Tariq ibn Shihab (Abu Dawud 1067, Sahih) | Quran + Sunnah |
