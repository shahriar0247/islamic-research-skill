# Islamic Research — Salafi Scholarship

السلام عليكم ورحمة الله وبركاته

A collection of Islamic research notes and articles compiled using **only verbatim quotes, fatwas, tafsir, and hadith gradings from Salafi scholars** — including Ibn Taymiyyah, Ibn al-Qayyim, Ibn Kathir, al-Dhahabi, Imam Ahmad, al-Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, at-Tirmidhi, an-Nasa'i, Ibn Majah, Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab, Ibn Baz, al-Albani, al-Uthaymeen, al-Fawzan, as-Sa'di, al-Luhaydan, and others (may Allah have mercy on those who have passed and preserve those who remain).

## ⚠️ Important — Read Before Using

**DO NOT TAKE FATWA FROM AI.**

AI can make mistakes. AI can hallucinate. AI can misattribute statements. **Anything correct in this repository is from Allah, and anything wrong is from Shaytan.** Do not base your religious practice on AI output alone.

- **Take only the direct statements** and their understanding from the **exact sayings/quotations of the scholars** as cited in the notes.
- **Always verify** any quote, hadith grading, or ruling against the original source (the book, the fatwa website, the scholar's verified works).
- **It is always better to sit with a scholar** and ask him your questions directly rather than to rely on AI or online research. Nothing replaces a qualified teacher who knows your specific situation.

That said, the reality is that many people are not close to scholars, and they will research online anyway — where they will often find incorrect, weak, or misleading information. The purpose of this repository is to direct that research toward the **verified statements of the people of knowledge** rather than leaving people to wander.

## Methodology

Every claim, ruling, or statement about Islam in these notes is directly attributable to a named Salafi scholar. Where a Salafi source could not be found for a specific point, it is marked explicitly as **"NOT FOUND FROM SALAFI SOURCE"** rather than being filled in with a guess.

The approved sources include: islamqa.info, salafipublications.com, abdurrahman.org, troid.org, learnaboutislam.co.uk, fatwaislam.com, islamweb.net, sunnah.com, binbaz.org.sa, alalbani.info, and others.
