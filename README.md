# Islamic Research with AI — Salafi/Ahle-Hadeeth Scholarship

السلام عليكم ورحمة الله وبركاته

Islamic research notes compiled using **only verbatim quotes from Salafi scholars** — Ibn Taymiyyah, Ibn al-Qayyim, Ibn Baz, al-Albani, al-Uthaymeen, al-Fawzan, and others.

## ⚠️ Read This First

**Do not take fatwa from AI.** Anything correct here is from Allah; anything wrong is from Shaytan. **Take only the direct quotes of the scholars** and verify against the original sources.

**Sitting with a scholar is always better.** But many people aren't close to scholars and will research online anyway — this repo directs that research toward verified scholar statements rather than leaving people to wander.

## Structure

```
/SKILL.md           # Research methodology
/research-notes/    # Research notes and articles
```

## Ask a Question

Don't have AI or want a specific question researched? **Comment, fork, or open a new issue** with your question and it will be researched and added inshaAllah. Corrections and additional sources are also welcome.

## Using This Skill with AI

This skill works with AI tools that support custom skills/instructions. To use it:

<details>
<summary><b>Devin (CLI)</b></summary>

1. Copy the `SKILL.md` file to `.devin/skills/islamic-research/SKILL.md` in your project.
2. Ask Devin any Islamic question — the skill triggers automatically.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Claude / Cursor / Windsurf</b></summary>

1. Copy the `SKILL.md` content into your project's rules file (e.g. `.cursorrules`, `CLAUDE.md`, or `.windsurfrules`).
2. Ask the AI your question — it will follow the methodology.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>ChatGPT / Gemini / any AI</b></summary>

1. Copy the contents of `SKILL.md` and paste it as your first message (or as a custom instruction/system prompt).
2. Then ask your question.
</details>

**Reminder:** Whatever AI you use — verify the output against the original scholar sources. Do not take fatwa from AI.
